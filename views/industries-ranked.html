Scarcity plus qualification-gated supply is the strongest pricing position on this page, and Solar's own guidance of holding 27–28% EBITDA rests on energetics and defence mix.
 4.45 89 100%

The largest volume pool and the fastest-growing private one. Demand is set by the services rather than the market, so switching is slow and pricing is negotiated — strong but not commodity pass-through.
 4.20 84 100%

High-value, low-volume, and now majority-private: Bharat Forge holds a 60% share of ATAGS (184 guns of the ₹6,900 cr order) and L&T supplies the K9 Vajra-T (₹7,629 cr, 100 guns) [REPORTED-T2]. Value per platform is high and requalification is glacial, which protects price — but revenue is lumpy and tender-timed, which is why cash conversion and op_share are scored below the ammunition layer.
 3.40 68 100%

Gun-barrel metallurgy is the qualification gate: Bharat Forge is the world's largest forgings maker and the ATAGS barrel source, Yantra India is the state barrel forger, and cold-hammer forging machinery is imported [REPORTED-T2]. A small revenue pool with disproportionate strategic weight — the barrier is process know-how, not scale.
 3.30 66 100%

BEL and BDL sit on the integration layer with the largest absolute order books — BDL at ~₹26,176 cr as of 31-Mar-2026 [REPORTED-T1] — but cash conversion is weak against government receivables and pricing is cost-plus or L1-tender rather than proprietary. Large pool, thin retained margin.
 3.25 65 100%
6 Special Steels & Alloys
Few qualified domestic sources: MIDHANI for special steels, superalloys and titanium, Kalyani Steels for the ATAGS barrel alloy [REPORTED-T2]. The barrier is qualification rather than volume, and defence steel is a minority share of both companies' revenue, so the pool is strategically important and economically modest.
 2.75 55 100%

No listed coverage in the selected universe, so the pool's economics are visible only through its customers' cost base — scored as a cost-bearer, not a value keeper.
 2.40 48 100%

Mid-value and shifting from DPSU to private integrated hubs — Adani's Gwalior LMG line (up to 100,000 weapons/yr, >90% domestic sourcing) and IRRPL's AK-203 at Korwa (601,427 rifles) [REPORTED-T2].
