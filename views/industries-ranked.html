India Satellite Payloads 6 nodes · ranked by value capture
#NodeScore (1-5)/100Coverage
1 TWTA / RF power amplification
Highest score on the chain: the function is essential to every transponder and SAR transmitter, was historically single-sourced abroad, and now has two domestic paths (BEL's TWTA facility under the SAC MoU, Sep-2024; SAC's GaN SSPA). Barriers are the vacuum-electronics and GaN process know-how, not capital. Rank moved up this run because BEL productionisation converts an import dependency into a domestic order book.
 3.85 77 100%
2 Navigation payload & atomic clocks
No substitute exists for a working clock, so pricing power and switching costs are as high as anything in the chain; the pool is small in revenue terms because there are few satellites, and it is captured by SAC rather than by any listed company. The indigenous clock flying on NVS-03 (30-Oct-2026) is the first evidence that the design can be industrialised.
 3.80 76 100%
3 Space optics, detectors & focal planes
The richest domestic pool by margin - Paras earned 44% EBIT on its optics segment in FY26 - with the fastest-growing demand pool behind it (12-satellite EO-PPP constellation by 2029). Scored below TWTA only because the highest-value element, the detector/FPA, is still imported and therefore not yet capturable in India.
 3.80 76 100%
4 Payload integration, screening & calibration
The largest operating-profit pool available to listed investors: Astra, Centum, Data Patterns, BEL and Paras all sit here, at margins above their corporate averages. Held back by a single state buyer that sets price, and by cash conversion of 374 days at Astra.
 3.75 75 100%
5 Antennas, feeds & reflectors
Steady, material revenue for Astra and BEL, but the least strategic node: several qualified suppliers, no export-control choke point, and apertures below the deployable-reflector class are genuinely competitive.
 3.35 67 100%
6 MMIC & rad-hard die
Lowest score despite the highest strategic weight: the pool is currently foreign, capture in India is design-only (Aelius MMIC IP inside Astra), and a fab route would be capital-heavy with long payback. Scored as the opportunity it would become, not the pool that exists.
 3.25 65 100%
