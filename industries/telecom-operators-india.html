Snapshot

Telecom operators & service providers — Jio, Airtel, Vi and BSNL, the demand layer that funds the entire equipment value chain. Services architecture (inputs → platform → delivery → segments). Scope: India, NSE/BSE.

Process & equipment
  • Inputs: spectrum, talent and network capex. Platform: core network, RAN and IT/OSS-BSS.
  • Delivery: retail, distribution and towers. Segments: consumer, enterprise, FWA and IoT. RAN and core gear is imported.
Bottlenecks
  • Spectrum cost and auction demand.
  • RAN and core import dependence.
  • ARPU vs data explosion (₹194.57 ARPU).
  • Vodafone Idea viability; tower tenancy consolidation; revenue leakage at 5G scale.
Value pools
  • The annuity layer — subscription revenue.
  • ARPU repair and 5G monetisation are the levers.
  • Enterprise/FWA mix adds value.
  • Adjacencies: OSS/BSS (Subex) and undersea (Tata Comm).
Watch items
  • Spectrum auctions (incl. mmWave).
  • Tariff repair.
  • VI funding / rollout.
  • 5G monetisation and FWA.
Coverage gaps

No listed pure-play for several operator inputs; BSNL is unlisted — nearest listed: Bharti Airtel, RIL (Jio), Vodafone Idea, Bharti Hexacom.

05 Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunity dual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

Spectrum cost & auction demandReserve prices stay high while competition thins — the 2024 auction sold only ~1.3% of the spectrum on offer (₹11,341 cr). Resilience: policy-led, multi-year.

Opportunity

Cheaper capacity aheadTRAI has proposed opening auctions to ISPs and M2M providers and adding 37–40 GHz (mmWave) at ~14% below 26 GHz pricing — a lower cost of raw capacity.

Risk

RAN & core import dependenceIndia’s 5G networks run predominantly on Nokia and Ericsson gear; domestic RAN is only now emerging. Resilience: slow.

Opportunity

India-made network gearTejas Networks, ITI and HFCL scale local RAN / optical under PLI — a protected import-substitution runway.

Risk

ARPU vs data explosionARPU is ₹194.57 while the average subscriber consumes 25.70 GB/month and revenue per GB is ₹7.87 — heavy data growth without commensurate pricing. Resilience: structural.

Opportunity

Tariff repair & mixIndustry tariff increases, 5G monetisation and a shift toward enterprise / FWA can lift ARPU without losing volume.

Risk

Vodafone Idea viabilityVi keeps losing subscribers (199.59 mn, net −4.0 mn in Dec-2025) and the Government of India is its largest shareholder (~23%) after AGR dues. Resilience: uncertain.

Opportunity

Restart benefits the chainAny AGR relief or fresh capex by Vi flows straight to towers (Indus captures 70%+ of Vi rollouts) and equipment vendors.

Risk

Tower tenancy consolidationIndus’ tenancy ratio has fallen to 1.65x and industry tower additions are moderating as operator rollouts mature. Resilience: cyclical.

Opportunity

5G loading & sharing5G loading (~35–40% of towers radiating 5G) and a Vi rollout restart support rental growth; Indus gains share from rival towercos.

Risk

Revenue leakage at 5G scale5G is ~40% of wireless data (29,094 PB) but ARPU uplift is thin; billing / assurance gaps leak value as services multiply. Resilience: manageable.

Opportunity

AI-driven assurance & CPaaSSubex (RA / fraud) and Tanla / Route Mobile (CPaaS, anti-spam) monetise the complexity — software value rising with network value.

Deepest hidden import dependency: the silicon inside the network — the 5G RAN, core and transport equipment that India’s operators buy is dominated by Nokia and Ericsson, and the semiconductors inside them are imported. Local RAN is the open prize.
