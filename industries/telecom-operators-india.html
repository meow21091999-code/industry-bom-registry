00 Digest short summary · full detail in the sections below
Snapshot

Telecom operators — Jio Platforms, Bharti Airtel, Vodafone Idea and BSNL — are the demand layer that funds the whole network equipment chain. Services architecture: inputs → platform → delivery → segments. Scope India, NSE/BSE, re-run 2026-10-29; Jio Platforms listed on 28-Oct-2026, so the largest operator is now a direct listed exposure.

Process & equipment
  • There is no factory BOM: the process is spectrum → capex → network build → traffic → billing.
  • Spine: RAN and 5G core (Nokia, Ericsson, Samsung, Tejas), optical transport, fibre, towers, OSS/BSS, data centres.
  • The build is now densification, not coverage — 593,244 5G BTS at Sep-2026, 99.9% of districts covered.
  • Equipment is imported; the domestic share sits in fibre/cable, towers and optical transport.
Bottlenecks
  • Spectrum: no auction in FY26 or FY27; TRAI’s Feb-2026 blueprint (~₹2.1 lakh cr reserve) awaits a DoT back-reference, and Vi renewals are pending.
  • RAN/core import dependence — MONTHS-to-YEARS to requalify domestic alternates.
  • ARPU vs data: ₹187.12 ARPU against ₹7.13 per GB and 28.39 GB per user per month.
  • Vi leverage: ₹1.56 lakh cr of deferred spectrum plus AGR; the ₹25,000 cr bank facility is still untied.
  • Tower tenancy at 1.62x, with incremental additions running at ~1.37x.
Value pools
  • The annuity — subscription revenue — stays with operators: AGR of ₹3.34 lakh cr in FY26.
  • Value leaks to imports at the RAN/core layer, the single largest capex line.
  • Domestic capture: towers (Indus), fibre/cable (HFCL, Sterlite), optical transport (Tejas), revenue assurance (Subex).
  • Adjacent pools: enterprise and cloud (Tata Communications), CPaaS (Tanla, Route Mobile), satcom (licensed, not yet commercial).
  • Capex is rotating to fibre, FWA CPE and data centres as 5G coverage build completes.
Market & growth
  • AGR ₹3,34,659 cr in FY26, +10.44% YoY [REPORTED-T1]; 3-year CAGR 10.2% nominal [CALCULATED].
  • Volume: 1,330.58 mn subscribers (+10.81%) and 2,85,376 PB of data (+24.74%) in FY26.
  • Growth is volume-led: ARPU rose only 3.59% and per-GB realisation fell 10.59%, so price/mix adds little.
  • Third-party sizes are not comparable: Mordor puts 2026 MNO service revenue at $159.80bn while Reports Insights puts total telecom services at $115.19bn for 2025 including pay-TV [CONFLICTING DATA — different scopes].
Watch items
  • Spectrum: no FY27 auction; 600 MHz and upper 6 GHz deferred past WRC-27.
  • Satcom: the DCC’s 5% AGR charge awaits Cabinet; Starlink’s security and FDI clearances are unresolved.
  • Vi: ₹45,000 cr capex plan for FY27-29, funding still being tied up.
  • Jio Platforms’ first post-listing quarter becomes the sector’s cleanest disclosure.
Coverage gaps

No listed pure-play for spectrum (state-allocated), retail and distribution, or operator talent and managed services. BSNL is unlisted; the nearest listed proxies are noted in the tree.

05 Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunity dual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

Spectrum renewal cliff, no auctionNo auction was held in FY26 and none is expected in FY27 — the second consecutive year the Cabinet’s annual-sale timeline has been missed, with TRAI’s 24-Feb-2026 recommendations still awaiting a DoT back-reference on reserve price. Vodafone Idea’s renewals are the live exposure. Resilience: policy-led, multi-year.

Opportunity

Cheaper capacity when the sale comesTRAI proposed a 20–30% cut to reserve prices versus 2024, halved net-worth entry requirements to ₹50 cr per circle, easier 600 MHz terms (24-year term, four-year payment holiday, 5% upfront) and a 10% discount for building sites in coverage gaps. Lower cost of raw capacity, and administrative assignment can bridge Vi’s renewals.

Risk

RAN & core import dependenceIndia’s 5G networks run predominantly on Nokia, Ericsson and Samsung gear, with the silicon inside them imported. Domestic RAN is emerging but sub-scale. Resilience: MONTHS to YEARS for requalification at network level.

Opportunity

India-made network gearTejas Networks, ITI and HFCL scale local RAN and optical transport; 136 telecom R&D projects worth ₹543 cr have been approved under TTDF. A protected import-substitution runway on the largest capex line.

Risk

ARPU vs the data explosionWireless ARPU is ₹187.12 while a data subscriber uses 28.39 GB a month and realisation is ₹7.13 per GB — down 10.59% through FY26. Volume grows faster than revenue. Resilience: STRUCTURAL.

Opportunity

Tariff repair & mixICRA expects industry tariff rationalisation over the next 12–24 months; premiumisation is already working — Airtel at ₹264 ARPU and Vi’s customer ARPU up 10.2% YoY on upgrades alone. FWA, enterprise and postpaid lift ARPU without volume loss.

Risk

Vodafone Idea funding gapVi carries ₹1.56 lakh cr of deferred spectrum and AGR obligations, reported a ₹3,754 cr net loss in Q1FY27, and has not yet closed the ₹25,000 cr bank facility behind its ₹45,000 cr three-year capex plan. Resilience: uncertain, but materially de-risked.

Opportunity

Vi’s restart flows down the chainThe AGR reassessment to ₹64,046 cr and the shift of near-term payments to FY36–FY41 freed cash for capex; Vi added subscribers in Q1FY27 for the first time since merger. Indus has taken ~71% of Vi’s rollouts since 2QFY25, and vendors gain from every site.

Risk

Tower tenancy dilutionIndus’ tenancy ratio is 1.62x against incremental additions of ~1.37x, and ~5,000 Jio tenancies are expected to exit in 2HFY27. A three-private-operator market caps co-location upside. Resilience: cyclical.

Opportunity

5G loading and share shiftIndus added 3,097 towers and 4,236 co-locations in Q1FY27 (+6.3% and +5.1% YoY) and is winning expired tenancies from rivals. 5G densification, small cells and edge sites are the next tenancy layer.

Risk

5G monetisation gap5G carries a large share of data traffic but no separate tariff; revenue leakage widens as services multiply. Resilience: manageable.

Opportunity

Slicing, assurance and CPaaSAirtel has launched 5G network slicing for postpaid (Postpaid Fast Lane); Subex (assurance, fraud), Tanla and Route Mobile (CPaaS, anti-spam) monetise the complexity — software value rising with network value.

Risk

Satellite substitution at the edgeStarlink, Eutelsat OneWeb and Jio Satellite all hold GMPCS licences, with 20 Starlink gateways already built in India. Once the Cabinet clears the 5% AGR spectrum charge and security clearances land, rural and enterprise access faces a new competitor. Resilience: regulatory-gated, near-term.

Opportunity

Satcom as a partnership, not only a threatBoth incumbents hold their own licences and distribution: Jio Satellite and Airtel-backed OneWeb can bundle satellite into rural and enterprise offerings rather than cede the segment. Ground-segment, gateway and CPE supply is an unclaimed domestic pool.

Deepest hidden import dependency: the silicon inside the network. The 5G RAN, core and transport equipment India’s operators buy is dominated by Nokia, Ericsson and Samsung, and the semiconductors inside those radios are imported. Domestic RAN and optical transport — Tejas, ITI, HFCL — is the open prize, and it is the one capex line where localisation can move the value pool rather than just the assembly margin.
06 Value pools & cost waterfall where value is captured · and which pool leaks to imports
Where value is captured
  • The annuity layer — subscription revenue. AGR ₹3.34 lakh cr in FY26, +10.44%.
  • ARPU repair and 5G monetisation are the levers; volume growth alone does not lift revenue per GB.
  • Enterprise, FWA and postpaid mix add value without adding spectrum.
  • Adjacencies: OSS/BSS and assurance (Subex), CPaaS (Tanla, Route Mobile), undersea and enterprise (Tata Communications).
Who captures it (India-listed)
  • Jio Platforms NSE/BSE: JPL — 508.43 mn wireless subscribers (Aug-2026); Q1FY27 revenue ₹39,173 cr, EBITDA margin 53.3%; listed 28-Oct-2026 at ₹1,358 and closed ₹1,417.50 [REPORTED]
  • Bharti Airtel NSE/BSE: BHARTIARTL — 491.60 mn wireless subscribers; ARPU ₹264, the industry high; India revenue ₹41,214 cr in Q1FY27 [REPORTED]
  • Vodafone Idea NSE/BSE: IDEA — 193.1 mn customers, first positive net adds since merger; GoI holds ~49% [REPORTED]
  • Bharti Hexacom NSE/BSE: BHARTIHEXA — Airtel subsidiary operating only Rajasthan and the North-East circles; listed Apr-2024 [REPORTED]
  • Indus Towers NSE/BSE: INDUSTOWER — 267,611 towers and 432,250 co-locations at Jun-2026; tenancy 1.62x [REPORTED]
Pools that leak to imports
  • RAN, 5G core and transport equipment — the largest single capex line, dominated by Nokia, Ericsson and Samsung [INFERENCE, based on vendor concentration reported in trade press].
  • FWA customer-premises equipment and IoT modules are imported.
  • Coverage gaps: no listed pure-play for spectrum, retail/distribution or operator talent; BSNL is unlisted.
