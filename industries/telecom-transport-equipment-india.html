DGDigest — at a glanceshort summary; full detail below
Snapshot
  • The backhaul and core of India’s networks: DWDM/OTN, packet transport, IP/MPLS routers, Ethernet switches and the optics and silicon inside them.
  • India designs and builds the systems; the transceivers, ASICs and coherent DSPs inside them are imported. Scope: India, NSE/BSE.
Process & equipment
  • SMT assembly of line cards and backplanes, shelf build, then optical alignment and traffic test — the same spine at Tejas, HFCL, ITI and the Flex-run Cisco and Ciena lines.
  • The gating asset is the test bench, not the placement machine; MTCTE/ITSAR lab capacity queues every launch.
Bottlenecks
  • Optical transceivers — 85–90% imported by value; no Indian laser-chip, TOSA/ROSA or DSP source. YEARS.
  • Switch/router and OTN silicon — US-designed, no carrier-grade Indian design house or fab. YEARS–STRUCTURAL.
  • PCBAs — ~80% imported, 15% BCD, Chapter 85 exemptions lapsed in Budget 2026. MONTHS.
  • BharatNet dependence — completes March 2027; private transport capex has not replaced it. CYCLICAL.
Value pools
  • Value sits in the optics and silicon; India captures integration, software and a rising share of design.
  • Tejas won the router and OTN nodes outright and still lost ₹207 cr in Q2 FY27 — winning the box does not win the margin.
  • HFCL re-rated on fibre and DCI, not transport: ~64% of its ₹28,911 cr book is OFC/DCI.
  • The component pool is opening: Dixon–Gemtek (SFP/BOSA) and GX Quantum Photonics, both ECMS-backed.

  • Q2 FY27 revenue: Tejas +55%, HFCL +143.6%, Netweb +335.7% YoY [REPORTED-T1] — but in fibre and AI systems, not transport.
Watch items
  • 31 Mar 2027 — PLI 1.0 ends and BharatNet Phase-3 is due; successor with the Finance Ministry, targeted Q1 FY28.
  • Dec 2026 / 3 Feb 2027 — GX production start and Dixon–Gemtek JV close.
  • Early Nov 2026 — HFCL board decision on vertical restructuring.
  • 31 Dec 2026 — NCCS and MTCTE relaxations expire.
Coverage gaps
  • No NSE/BSE pure-play in transceivers, ASICs, DSPs, timing or backplanes; nearest are Tejas, HFCL, ITI, Netweb, Dixon (JV), Exicom.

05 Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunity dual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

High-speed optical transceivers400G/800G modules are ~85–90% imported by value and >95% of 100G+ components come from abroad; laser chips, TOSA/ROSA and coherent DSPs have no Indian source. Resilience: YEARS. [REPORTED]

Opportunity

First domestic module linesDixon–Gemtek signed a definitive 60:40 JV on 7 Oct 2026 (completion targeted 3 Feb 2027) for SFP and BOSA; GX Quantum Photonics holds ECMS approval on a ₹100 cr phase-1 and starts production in Dec 2026. Both are module/packaging plays, not laser-chip plays. [REPORTED]

Risk

Switch/router and OTN siliconCore switching ASICs, OTN switch fabrics and coherent DSPs are designed in the US and imported; there is no Indian carrier-grade design house or fab. Resilience: YEARS to STRUCTURAL. [REPORTED]

Opportunity

Design, not fabrication, is the entryCisco now runs silicon design in India alongside six manufactured product lines; the component-focused PLI successor and the ₹25,000 cr design incentive fund target exactly this gap. [REPORTED]

Risk

PCBA and multilayer boards~80% of PCBAs in network gear are imported and the 15% BCD on telecom PCBAs raises landed cost; Budget 2026 lapsed a swathe of Chapter 85 exemptions from 31 Mar 2026, adding cost to imported parts. Resilience: MONTHS. [REPORTED]

Opportunity

Local PCBA and board capacityThe ₹40,000 cr ECMS outlay explicitly covers PCBs and connectors, and Cisco’s Chennai line is moving to deeper local sourcing — the PCB drill-down under this entry tracks the board side. [REPORTED]

Risk

BharatNet dependenceBoth indigenous router vendors built their books on one programme: Tejas has shipped 17,000+ of a planned ~60,000 routers, HFCL has taken RVNL awards worth ₹2,167 cr and ₹2,666 cr, and the programme completes in March 2027. Private-operator transport capex has not replaced it. Resilience: CYCLICAL. [REPORTED]

Opportunity

Exports and data centresTejas is shipping an end-to-end 5G network to South America and 400G/800G coherent DWDM to private operators; HFCL reports ~57% of its order book as export. The next leg is hyperscale DCI, not rural middle-mile. [REPORTED]

Risk

Balance-sheet and execution capacityTejas carries ₹4,384 cr net debt and ₹2,314 cr of inventory against a ₹2,315 cr order book, and lost ₹207 cr in Q2 FY27; ITI’s statutory auditors issued a disclaimer of conclusion on the Q1 FY27 results. Resilience: CYCLICAL. [REPORTED]

Opportunity

Consolidation and capital disciplineHFCL is reviewing its verticals with E&Y and a board decision is expected by early November 2026; a cleaner separation of products from EPC would make the transport-equipment pool directly investable. [REPORTED]

Deepest hidden import dependency: the coherent DSP and the switch ASIC sitting inside every DWDM line card and IP/MPLS router — US-designed silicon with no Indian design house or fab, and no certification path around it. India assembles boxes around imported silicon; the control-plane chip is the true chokepoint, and unlike optics there is not yet even a module-assembly beachhead. [INFERENCE]
06 Value pools & cost waterfall where value is captured · and which pool leaks to imports
Where value is captured
  • Silicon and optics inside the box capture the margin; integration and software capture the rest.
  • BharatNet, telco backhaul and now hyperscale data-centre interconnect are the demand anchors.
  • Localisation is moving from box assembly to module assembly (SFP/BOSA) and board-level work.
Who captures it (India-listed)
  • Tejas Networks NSE/BSE: TEJASNET — TJ1600 DWDM/OTN and TJ1400 routers; 400G/800G coherent shipping; L1 in a pan-India strategic OTN tender in Q2 FY27
  • HFCL NSE/BSE: HFCL — IP/MPLS routers, L2/L3 switches, OTN products inside a ₹28,911 cr book dominated by OFC and DCI
  • ITI Limited NSE/BSE: ITI — transmission and switching; three BharatNet Phase-3 packages under execution
  • Netweb Technologies NSE/BSE: NETWEB — SONiC-based 10G–400G switches, now a small line beside AI systems
  • Exicom Tele-Systems NSE/BSE: EXICOM — modular DC power and racks into BharatNet-3 via the HFCL consortium
  • Tata Consultancy Services NSE/BSE: TCS — BSNL 4G/5G integrator; issues the RAN add-on LoI to Tejas
Pools that leak to imports
  • Optical transceivers, coherent DSPs, switch ASICs, timing devices and backplanes — the highest-margin layers all leave the country.
  • Bottleneck nodes: DWDM / OTN Optical Transport, PTN / Packet Transport, IP / MPLS Routers, Optical Transceivers, Switch / Router ASIC & Coherent DSP, PCB / Backplane & PCBA, Timing & Synchronisation, Transport System OEM.
