Snapshot

Routers, switches & optical transport — DWDM/OTN/PTN transport, routers and switches, the backhaul and core of the network. India assembles; the silicon and optics are imported. Scope: India, NSE/BSE.

Process & equipment
  • Line cards and chassis are SMT-assembled and tested in India; DWDM/OTN and IP/MPLS platforms integrate imported optics and switch silicon.
  • SMT, optical test and burn-in are the key steps.
Bottlenecks
  • High-speed optical transceivers (85–90% imported).
  • Switch/router ASIC and coherent DSP (~100% imported, US-designed).
  • PCBA import duty and supply chain; Chinese import dependence; shallow local value addition.
Value pools
  • Value sits in the silicon and optics inside the box.
  • Indian vendors capture integration and software.
  • BharatNet and data centres are the demand anchors.
  • Localisation: optical modules, PCBA, network OS.
Watch items
  • BharatNet Phase-3 execution.
  • PCBA duty changes.
  • Cisco–Flex Chennai local assembly.
  • 400G transport adoption.
Coverage gaps

No listed pure-play: DWDM/OTN optics, switch ASIC, coherent DSP, backplane — nearest: Tejas/HFCL/Netweb (systems).

05 Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunity dual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

High-speed optical transceivers400G/800G modules are ~85–90% imported by value and >95% of 100G+ components are imported; active transceivers are classed as duty-free "parts", undercutting nascent domestic assembly. [REPORTED]

Opportunity

First domestic movesDixon–Gemtek JV (SFP/BOSA, Dixon 60%, ECMS beneficiary) and GX Group's photonics plant (SFP, TOSA/ROSA; ~₹500 cr, production from Dec-2026) are the beachhead. [REPORTED]

Risk

Switch/router & OTN siliconCore routers, OTN switching ASICs and coherent DSPs are US-designed and imported (dependency index ~72); no Indian fab or design depth. [REPORTED]

Opportunity

Design-led manufacturingPLI 4–7% + 1% design-led incentive rewards genuine IP; Tejas–NEC collaboration and semiconductor-design ambitions are the entry points. [REPORTED]

Risk

PCBA import duty & supply chain~80% of PCBAs in network gear (and >50% in OFC gear, MSCs, routers) are imported; the 2024 BCD hike on telecom PCBAs to 15% raised landed costs. [REPORTED]

Opportunity

Local PCBA & assemblyCisco–Flex Chennai phase-2 targets multi-layer PCBA locally; PLI-driven assembly is shifting value into India. [REPORTED]

Risk

Chinese import dependence>80% of critical telecom components (incl. 4G/5G antennas, signal processors) are sourced from China; Chinese goods are ~40% of India's telecom imports. [REPORTED]

Opportunity

Trusted-source procurementBharatNet PMA/Make-in-India orders are flowing to HFCL (₹8,100 cr consortium), ITI (₹6,956 cr) and Tejas. [REPORTED]

Risk

Shallow local value additionLocalisation is below 15% for some telecom equipment; generic equipment makers face up to 26% fiscal disability vs global peers. [REPORTED]

Opportunity

Protected demand poolThe ₹65,000 cr BharatNet Phase-3 middle-mile and 5G backhaul build create a policy-backed domestic order book. [REPORTED]

Deepest hidden import dependency: the coherent DSPs and switch/router ASICs sitting inside every DWDM line card and IP/MPLS router — designed in the US (Broadcom/Marvell-class silicon), with no Indian design house or fab. India assembles boxes around imported silicon; the control-plane chip is the true chokepoint. [INFERENCE]
