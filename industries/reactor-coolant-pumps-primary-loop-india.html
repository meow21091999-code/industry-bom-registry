Snapshot

Reactor coolant pumps and the primary loop — the pumps that circulate coolant through the core. A 700 MWe unit uses four PCPs (2 loops × 2), each with a ~6 MW, 6.6 kV motor; order-to-delivery has run 24–48 months.

Process & equipment
  • Pump shaft and casing forgings, cobalt-free hardfacing, mechanical seals and 6.6 kV motors.
  • Dynamic test-bed qualification is a live constraint on delivery.
Bottlenecks
  • Single-source PCP qualification — KSB is the sole qualified domestic supplier to NPCIL.
  • Test-bed capacity is the live bind.
  • Main coolant pump order-to-delivery took ~48 months.
Value pools
  • KSB holds a ₹1,235 cr nuclear order book (Jun-2026); Kirloskar Brothers supplies primary-heat-transfer and shutdown-cooling pumps.
  • Flowserve (US) is licensed under 10 CFR Part 810 to transfer PCP technology to Core Energy (India).
Watch items
  • KSB ₹500 cr Kaiga 5&6 PCP order (8 pumps + motors, 2022).
  • A second qualified PCP source.
  • Domestic dynamic-test facilities.
Coverage gaps
  • Single qualified domestic PCP source; the second is a foreign-licensed localisation.

02 Value-Chain Ladder & Supplier Tiers raw material → aftermarket · Tier-4 → OEM
Tier 4 · Raw
Iron · chromium · nickel · molybdenum · cobalt · steel scrap · heavy-water feedstock · electrical steel · copper nuclear-grade ingot feedstock; alloying elements largely imported
Tier 3 · Specialty
Nuclear-grade stainless steel & special alloys (SS 316L/316LN, CF8A castings, SA-508 forgings) · cobalt-free hardfacing · seal face materials (SiC/graphite) · water-lubricated-bearing composites seal faces and some bearing materials are imported
Tier 2 · Component
Pump casing & impeller · motor · mechanical-seal cartridge · bearings · flywheel · loop piping, headers & valves · auxiliary (SDCP/ECCS/moderator) pumps
Tier 1 · System / EPC
PCP / RCP OEM & nuclear-island EPC — KSB Limited (PCP) · BHEL (motors) · L&T (vessels, headers) · Kirloskar Brothers (aux pumps)
OEM / Owner
Design & owner-operator — BARC (design) · NPCIL (owner) · DAE all unlisted; VVER main coolant pumps bought from Russia (Atomenergomash)
Aftermarket
Seal & bearing replacement · impeller/shaft refurbishment · pump changeout · spares · life extension recurring over a 40–60-year asset life; RCP seal failure is a common forced-outage cause

05 Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunity dual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

Single-source PCP qualificationKSB is the sole qualified domestic supplier of primary coolant pumps to NPCIL. A single point of failure for every 700 MWe unit. Resilience: years (certification-gated).

Opportunity

A second qualified sourceFlowserve (US) is licensed under 10 CFR Part 810 to transfer PCP technology to CORE Energy Systems for manufacture at Coimbatore — the first such US authorisation for India.

Risk

Test-bed capacity is the live bindGHAVP PCP testing was paused on third-party test-bed equipment, delaying the first fully indigenous 700 MWe PCP. Resilience: months.

Opportunity

Domestic dynamic-test facilitiesKSB already built a 220 MWe PCP dynamic-test facility; a second qualified test bed de-risks the whole fleet schedule.

Risk

Class-1 seals & bearings are the deepest importRCP mechanical seals are held by Framatome (Jeumont), Flowserve and John Crane; no NSE/BSE pure-play. Resilience: years.

Opportunity

Seal & bearing localisationKSB runs an in-house mechanical-seal line at Shirwal; MTAR makes indigenous water-lubricated bearings for reactors.

Risk

One heavy-forging shopLTSSHF is the only nuclear-grade heavy-forging facility in the region and its credit profile is weak. A single point of failure for casings, headers and vessels. Resilience: years.

Opportunity

Forging scale-upFleet-mode demand (10×700 MWe + SMRs) justifies a second qualified forging source and an LTSSHF recapitalisation.

Risk

Fabrication & test machinery importedThe 5-axis CNC, Hirth gear grinders, EDM, seal-making and dynamic test-bed equipment are foreign-built. Resilience: months–years.

Opportunity

Machine-tool & test-bed localisationA domestic heavy-machine-tool and test-rig base would de-risk the entire pump-fabrication chain.

Risk

Skilled tradesNuclear-qualified welders, NDT inspectors and process engineers bind tighter than materials and cannot be bought quickly.

Opportunity

Incumbent qualification pipelinesKSB / BHEL / L&T qualification pipelines are a durable moat as fleet mode ramps.

Risk

Owner & design are unlistedDesign (BARC), ownership (NPCIL) and the fuel cycle sit inside DAE entities with no NSE/BSE listing. Resilience: structural.

Opportunity

SHANTI Act 2025Graded liability, a statutory AERB and limited private participation open SPV models for the next tranche of reactors and SMRs.

Deepest hidden dependency: the pump is India’s most investable nuclear-equipment node — KSB (PCP), BHEL (motors) and L&T (headers, forgings) are listed and qualified — yet the two highest-value layers, the Class-1 seal & design IP and the fabrication/test machinery, are imported or held inside the incumbent, and the owner (NPCIL) and designer (BARC) are unlisted. The pump is investable; the seal IP and the tooling are not.
