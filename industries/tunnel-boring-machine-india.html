DGDigest — at a glanceshort summary; full detail below
Snapshot

TBMs are project-embedded capital goods: a 90–110 m, ~500–900 t machine that bores one drive and is then rebuilt, reused or buried on site. India runs 70–100 machines at any time, imports virtually every large-diameter unit, and has no domestic OEM. The machine is a small share of tunnel cost — the money sits in the EPC contract, the lining and the consumables.

Process & equipment
  • Build spine: heavy fabrication of cutterhead and shield → large-diameter machining (vertical boring/turning, gantry machining to 8 m+) → gear cutting and induction hardening of bearing raceways → hydraulic and electrical integration → factory acceptance test with cutter-load and main-drive test stands.
  • Site spine: diaphragm-walled shaft and launch cradle, underground assembly, segment casting yard, muck removal (screw conveyor plus rail/belt for EPB; slurry circuit plus separation plant for slurry machines), grout plant, backup gantries.
  • Indian localisation today is fabrication and assembly, not component manufacture: Herrenknecht Chennai (~70% local content, 10–12 metro TBMs a year) and Robbins Bangalore (80–85%).
Bottlenecks
  • Main slewing bearing (three-row cylindrical roller, up to 8 m+ OD) — Liebherr / thyssenkrupp Rothe Erde / SKF. Resilience: years to structural.
  • Disc cutters and cutter rings — imported, and the largest recurring consumable. Resilience: months to years.
  • Main-drive gearboxes and high-power VFDs — Indian qualification only now beginning. Resilience: years.
  • Guidance and control — OEM-captive, no Indian player. Resilience: structural.
  • Chinese export licensing from 18-Nov-2026 on complete machines and on the four component families above.
Value pools
  • The machine is the small pool: ₹200–500 cr per TBM against ₹850–900 cr per km of metro tunnelling — the contractor captures the larger one.
  • Domestic capture today: shield and cutterhead fabrication, assembly, wiring, hydraulics and cylinders, precast segments (cement, steel, admixtures, casting yards).
  • Leaks to import: main bearing, gearboxes, VFDs, cutters and rings, seals, guidance, large bearing rings.
  • Annuity: cutter rings and bearings, seals, grease, foam and bentonite, plus remanufacture — Herrenknecht has built or rebuilt 40+ machines in Chennai.
  • The localisation opportunity is precisely the four families China just licensed: cutterheads, screw conveyors, segment erectors, main drives.

05 Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunity dual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

Main slewing bearingThree-row cylindrical roller bearing, 2–8 m+ OD, 42CrMo4-L with induction-hardened raceways; a failure cannot be fixed in the tunnel and ends the drive. Held by Liebherr, thyssenkrupp Rothe Erde and SKF. Resilience: years to structural.

Opportunity

Bearing-ring metallurgy and induction hardeningThe single highest-barrier localisation target; India has steel and forging but no large-ring raceway capability. Robbins is already trying to build it with Liebherr in India.

Risk

Disc cutters and cutter ringsThe consumable that sets advance rate and is the largest recurring cost on a hard-rock drive; 17–20 in assemblies on carbide/hardfaced tool-steel rings. Resilience: months (many makers) but years to qualify for a live drive.

Opportunity

A domestic cutter and ring makerNo Indian maker exists. AIA Engineering already casts high-chrome wear parts; cutter rings are the adjacent, unserved product.

Risk

Main-drive gearboxes and VFDsPlanetary main drives and high-power variable-frequency drives are concentrated in European suppliers and now sit on China's controlled-parts list. Resilience: years.

Opportunity

Gearbox and drive localisationRobbins is qualifying Indian gearbox and VFD partners — a live, dated opportunity rather than a theoretical one.

Risk

Guidance, control and machine softwareLaser/IMU guidance, automated steering and TBM data acquisition are OEM-captive; tolerances have tightened from ~50 mm to ~10 mm, raising the software's value. Resilience: structural.

Opportunity

Tunnel-guidance and TBM-software capabilityThe deepest software-hardware gap; an Indian engineering-services firm could own it without building a machine.

Risk

Chinese export licensing (18-Nov-2026)Complete TBMs plus cutterheads, screw conveyors, segment erectors, main drive systems and remanufacturing technology now need licences for 'sensitive' destinations and registration elsewhere. Resilience: years for machines, structural for Chinese-brand remanufacture.

Opportunity

Domestic content in exactly those four familiesThe control list doubles as an industrial policy: it names the four sub-systems India must make.

Deepest hidden dependency: not the machine and not the steel — the main slewing bearing and the main-drive gearbox. A TBM can be fabricated, wired and assembled in Chennai or Bangalore at 70–85% local content while the two components that decide whether it ever turns are still imported, and now sit on a controlled-parts list. Every ‘localised’ Indian TBM rests on them.
Stress test — China's licensing regime, decision-dated 18-Nov-2026. Affected nodes: complete TBMs (Chinese brands and machines built in Chinese plants of European brands), cutterheads, screw conveyors, segment erectors, main drives, remanufacturing technology. Resilience: years for a new machine (12–18 month build plus requalification), weeks for a spare routed from a European plant, structural for Chinese-brand remanufacture. Who loses: contractors with Chinese-origin machines on order and projects still at tender stage, and metros absorbing cost. Who benefits: Herrenknecht Chennai and Robbins Bangalore (spare capacity already built), BEML's ToT case, European component makers, domestic fabricators of the four named component families, and the second-hand and rebuild market. Second-round test: round one (2024–25) was customs delay, and the workaround was local assembly plus re-routing — round two names the components and the remanufacturing technology explicitly, so the round-one workaround is closed. Lead indicators: MOFCOM licence approvals, India's monthly import data for HS 8430 31, Herrenknecht Chennai output, the BEML tie-up award, and tender clauses excluding Chinese-origin equipment.
06 Value pools & cost waterfall where value is captured · and which pool leaks to imports
Where value is captured
  • The tunnel, not the machine: ₹850–900 cr per km of metro tunnelling against ₹200–500 cr for the machine itself.
  • Domestic capture: fabrication, assembly, wiring, hydraulics and cylinders, precast segments and casting yards.
  • Annuity: cutter rings and bearings, seals, grease, foam and bentonite, plus machine remanufacture.
Who captures it (India-listed)
  • Afcons, L&T, J Kumar, Cemindia, NCC — the contractor layer that owns the fleet and books the tunnel.
  • UltraTech / Ambuja / Dalmia, SAIL / Tata Steel / JSW — the lining material flow.
  • Ashapura (bentonite), Yuken (hydraulics), Polycab / KEI (cables), Schaeffler / SKF / Timken (bearings).
Pools that leak to imports
  • Main slewing bearing and main-drive gearboxes.
  • Disc cutters and cutter rings.
  • High-power VFDs and the drive train.
  • Guidance, control and machine software.
