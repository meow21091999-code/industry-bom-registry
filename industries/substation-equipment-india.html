Snapshot

Substation equipment — power transformers & reactors, GIS/AIS switchgear, protection & automation, HVDC terminals and instrument transformers, assembled for the 33–765 kV AC and ±500/±800 kV DC grid. Demand is set by the sanctioned grid-capex cycle: the NEP to 2032 (₹9.15 L cr) plus the GEC-III / PM Dhara scheme cleared by Cabinet on 30-Sep-2026 (₹1.86 L cr, of which ₹1.36 L cr is intra-state transmission, to evacuate 135 GW of renewables by FY33).

Process & equipment
  • Core winding and CRGO core-cutting, vacuum/oil processing and tank fabrication; GIS enclosure and interrupter assembly; CRP/IED panels; HVDC valve halls.
  • Shed height, crane capacity and HV test bays gate entry at the top voltage classes; a new entrant needs 2–3 years to become bid-eligible.
Bottlenecks
  • CRGO electrical steel — ~90% imported; DGTR anti-dumping probe live.
  • EHV bushings — global shortage has delayed transformer deliveries.
  • OLTC (>80% advanced vacuum imported), IED/numerical-relay design IP, and HVDC valve design — all foreign.
  • Certified winding/test manpower and 765 kV test-bay slots.
Value pools
  • Transformer & switchgear OEMs — Hitachi Energy India, CG Power, GE Vernova T&D, Siemens Energy India, BHEL, Voltamp, TRIL, Indo Tech.
  • HVDC terminal consortia (GE Vernova, Hitachi + BHEL) and substation EPC (Bajel, Ashoka Buildcon).
  • Aftermarket service, retrofit and GIS upgrades; the GEC-III order cycle to FY33.
Watch items
  • GEC-III tendering from FY27 (Cabinet, 30-Sep-2026).
  • CEA SF6-free GIS mandate for new substations above 220 kV (bids after 1-Jul-2026).
  • CRGO anti-dumping decision (DGTR probe opened 22-Jun-2026).
  • Voltamp Unit-III commissioned 7-Oct-2026; 765 kV lead times.
Coverage gaps
  • CRGO, EHV bushings, OLTC, vacuum interrupters, HVDC converter valves and numerical relays — no NSE/BSE pure-play.

05Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunitydual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

Transformer core (CRGO electrical steel)~90% imported (China / Japan / Korea / Russia). Annual consumption ~4.0–4.5 lakh t against ~40,000–50,000 t domestic output. DGTR opened an anti-dumping probe on 22-Jun-2026 (complainant: JSW-JFE Electrical Steel Nashik); GTRI warns duties could raise transformer cost without cutting dependence. CRGO is 25–30% of transformer cost.

Opportunity

CRGO — localisation playJSW-JFE (Nashik) and SAIL (Bokaro) are the domestic route; PLI specialty-steel (Scheme 1.1) lowered the CRGO threshold to ₹3,000 cr / 50,000 t. No NSE/BSE pure-play — nearest listed proxy is JSW Steel. [DERIVED]

Risk

EHV bushingsA global bushing shortage has stretched transformer deliveries and stalled substation completion (APAR Q3 FY26 earnings call; management expects normalisation over ~6 months). Capacitor-graded bushings above 245 kV are predominantly imported (Trench, Modern/HSP).

Opportunity

EHV bushings — localisation playCG Power has partial in-house capability; domestic qualification of 765 kV bushings is the substitution play. No listed pure-play. [DERIVED]

Risk

OLTC / tap changers>80% of advanced vacuum OLTCs are imported; Reinhausen alone holds ~35–40% and the top five ~65%. EMR (Easun-MR JV), Amson and Trishul serve the domestic retrofit market; OLTC lead times now run past 24 months. No NSE/BSE pure-play.

Opportunity

OLTC — localisation playBuilding the vacuum-interrupter supply chain and IEC 60214-1 grid-grade certification is the gap to close. No listed pure-play. [DERIVED]

Risk

Numerical relays / IEDs & HVDC valve designCore IED technology is foreign (ABB / Hitachi / Siemens / GE / Schneider). HVDC thyristor/IGBT valves are made by BHEL (Bhopal converters, Bengaluru valves) but the design IP is licensed (Hitachi / Alstom / Siemens / GE); ~65–70% of power-semiconductor components are imported.

Opportunity

Valve & IED design — localisation playClosing this runs through the device layer — domestic high-power MOSFET/IGBT/SiC fabs (e.g. the approved Odisha SiC fab) and indigenous IED design. Listed exposure is via MNC subsidiaries. [DERIVED]

Risk

SF₆ / SF₆-free gasCEA now requires SF₆-free GIS for new substations above 220 kV (bids after 1-Jul-2026; approved gases g3 (3M) and AirPlus (ABB)). Green GIS costs 6–8× conventional and relies on imported components and gases; fluoronitrile (C4FN) is made in India by Laxmi Organic (~60 t/yr).

Opportunity

SF₆-free gas — localisation playHitachi Energy (EconiQ) and Siemens Energy (Blue) hold the listed positions; domestic fluoronitrile and gas-handling scale is the substitution play. [DERIVED]

Risk

Qualified capacity & certified manpowerCapacity at the top of the range is held by a short list of qualified names; a new entrant needs 2–3 years to become bid-eligible. Delivery timelines (Ministry of Power, Mar-2026): 220 kV 11–15 months, 400 kV ~15 months, 765 kV 20–24 months. 765 kV serial production is limited to 3–4 firms.

Opportunity

Qualified capacity — expansion playAnnounced/under-construction transformer capacity is large (IEEMA/MOFSL: 375 GVA FY25 → 650 GVA FY27E; Hitachi ₹2,000 cr LPT plant FY28; Voltamp Unit-III; Indo Tech to 50,000 MVA). The binding constraint is certified winding/test manpower, which cannot be bought quickly. [DERIVED]

Where value sits: transformer & switchgear OEMs (Hitachi Energy India, CG Power, GE Vernova T&D, Siemens Energy India, BHEL, Voltamp, TRIL, Indo Tech) and HVDC terminal consortia; then aftermarket service, retrofit and GIS upgrades. The GEC-III order cycle to FY33 broadens the pipeline.
06 Value pools & cost waterfall where value is captured · and which pool leaks to imports
Where value is captured
  • Transformer & switchgear OEMs — Hitachi Energy India, CG Power, GE Vernova T&D, Siemens Energy India, BHEL, Voltamp, TRIL, Indo Tech.
  • HVDC terminal consortia (GE Vernova, Hitachi + BHEL) and substation EPC (Bajel, Ashoka Buildcon).
  • Aftermarket service, retrofit and GIS upgrades; the GEC-III / PM Dhara order cycle to FY33.
Who captures it (India-listed)
  • Hitachi Energy India NSE: POWERINDIA — transformers, GIS, grid automation, HVDC; ₹2,000 cr Large Power Transformer plant at Karjan, Vadodara (target FY28)
  • CG Power NSE: CGPOWER — power transformers, EHV switchgear; 765 kV reactor package 7RT-28 L1 (₹166.7 cr, Aug-2026); Nashik MV GIS / RMU plant
  • GE Vernova T&D India NSE: GVT&D — transformers, HVDC; L1 for the 6 GW Barmer–South Kalamb ±800 kV HVDC terminals (Sep-2026)
  • Siemens Energy India NSE: ENRIN — transformers, GIS, HVDC; Blue SF₆-free
  • BHEL NSE: BHEL — 765 kV transformer package 7TR-21 L1 (₹514.6 cr, Aug-2026); HVDC converter transformers + valves
  • Voltamp Transformers NSE: VOLTAMP — Unit-III (Jarod) commissioned 7-Oct-2026, 6,000 MVA first phase
Pools that leak to imports
  • CRGO electrical steel, EHV bushings and OLTC are largely imported.
  • IED/numerical-relay design IP and HVDC valve design are foreign-licensed; ~65–70% of power-semiconductor components imported.
  • Bottleneck nodes: CRGO core, EHV bushings, OLTC, SF₆-free gas, numerical relays / IEDs, HVDC converter valves.
