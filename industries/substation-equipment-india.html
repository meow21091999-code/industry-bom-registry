Snapshot

Substation equipment — transformers, GIS/AIS switchgear, protection & automation, HVDC terminals and instrument transformers. The NEP outlay to 2032 is ₹9.15 L cr; 1,270 GVA of transformation capacity is to be added; the India transformer market is ₹40,000 cr (FY26) with 33 GW of HVDC planned by 2032.

Process & equipment
  • Core winding and CRGO core building, transformer oil, pressboard/kraft paper, bushings, OLTC, GIS modules and IEDs.
  • Core materials — CRGO, bushings, OLTC and IED silicon — are largely imported.
Bottlenecks
  • CRGO electrical steel — ~90% imported.
  • Bushings, OLTC and vacuum interrupters; transformer oil and pressboard.
  • IED silicon and rising lead times.
Value pools
  • Transformer & switchgear OEMs — Hitachi Energy India, CG Power, GE Vernova T&D, Siemens Energy India, BHEL, Voltamp, TRIL, Indo Tech.
  • Aftermarket service, retrofit and GIS upgrades.
Watch items
  • 33 GW of HVDC capacity planned by 2032.
  • CRGO domestic capacity (JSW-JFE) scaling.
  • Transformer lead times.
Coverage gaps
  • CRGO, bushings and OLTC are largely imported.

02Value-chain ladder

Raw material → aftermarket, with the Tier-4 → OEM supplier bands. Note the inversion: value capture sits upstream in CRGO, OLTC, bushings and IEDs — not in Indian tank-and-winding assembly, which is competitive.

Tier 4 · Raw
CRGO / CRNO electrical steel · copper · aluminium · mineral-oil feedstock · silicon · iron ore · epoxy feedstock JSW Steel (JSW-JFE) · SAIL · Hindalco · Vedanta
Tier 3 · Specialty
Transformer oil · pressboard / kraft paper · bushings · OLTC · vacuum interrupters · IED silicon · SF₆-free gas blends largely imported
