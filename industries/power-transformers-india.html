Snapshot
  • India's transformer market is ~₹40,000 cr (FY26), riding the ₹9.16 lakh cr T&D build-out; ~86,000 MVA was added in FY25 alone.
  • Demand is strong and visibility long, but lead times (24–42 months at EHV) and imported inputs (CRGO, bushings, OLTC) gate delivery.
Process & equipment
  • Core cutting: CRGO coils (0.18–0.35 mm) slit and cut-to-length to ±0.1 mm with burr ≤0.02 mm, then step-lap mitered.
  • Winding: HV disc/interleaved coils and LV foil coils from paper/nomex-insulated copper or aluminium.
  • Drying & impregnation: vapour-phase or vacuum drying to <0.5% moisture, then vacuum oil filling.
  • Testing: routine (ratio, impedance, loss, dielectric) per IS 2026/IEC 60076; impulse/short-circuit at accredited labs.
Bottlenecks
  • CRGO electrical steel — ~90% imported; live anti-dumping probe. Resilience: structural.
  • HV bushings (OIP/RIP) — significant import dependence; RIP cores imported. Resilience: import-substitution underway.
  • On-load tap changer (OLTC) — advanced vacuum OLTCs >80% imported (Reinhausen-led). Resilience: concentrated foreign supply.
  • CTC/copper winding conductor — capacity a choke point; copper 85–90% of CTC cost.
  • EHV testing infrastructure — limited accredited HV/short-circuit capacity lengthens lead times.
Value pools
  • Power-transformer OEMs are the largest pool; CG Power's Power segment at 21.9% PBIT margin.
  • EHV/765 kV and HVDC transformers carry high entry barriers and pricing power.
  • Backward integration (CTC, pressboard, RIP bushing) is a margin lever.
  • Bushing value sits with condenser-graded RIP makers (60–70% premium over OIP).
Watch items
  • DGTR CRGO/amorphous anti-dumping finding; a precedent duty was imposed on Chinese CRNO in Dec 2025.
  • BIS licence renewals for foreign CRGO suppliers.
  • Copper price swings — 35–45% of active material cost.
  • New capacity: CG Power to ~110,000 MVA by end-2026; Hitachi Energy ₹2,000 cr Karjan plant.
Coverage gaps
  • No NSE/BSE-listed OLTC pure-play (Reinhausen, Hitachi, Huaming are foreign/unlisted).
  • Bushings: only Yash Highvoltage, and it trades on the BSE SME platform.
  • Tanks, radiators, fasteners, gauges and Buchholz/PRV have no listed pure-play.
  • Domestic CRGO sits inside the JSW–JFE JV, not a separately listed entity.

05 Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunity dual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

CRGO electrical steel (core)~90% of India's CRGO requirement is imported (CN/JP/KR/RU); 4.0–4.5 lakh t demand vs 40–50k t domestic. Resilience: structural.

Opportunity

Domestic GOESJSW–JFE scaling 50k→250k t (Nashik) + a Vijayanagar line (~350k t, ₹5,845 cr), fully online only after FY2028.

Risk

HV bushings (OIP/RIP)India is significantly import-dependent for EHV bushings and RIP cores. Resilience: import-substitution underway.

Opportunity

RIP localisationYash Highvoltage's ₹153 cr plant (to 550 kV, H2 FY27) with in-house core making.

Risk

On-load tap changer (OLTC)Advanced vacuum OLTCs are >80% imported; Reinhausen holds ~35–55% of the global market. Resilience: concentrated foreign supply.

Opportunity

Domestic OLTCBHEL and EMR/Easun MR make/retrofit OLTCs, but no indigenous vacuum OLTC at competitive scale yet.

Risk

CTC / copper winding conductorCTC drawing capacity is a choke point; copper is 85–90% of CTC cost and highly volatile.

Opportunity

CTC capacityApar (12,000 MT/yr), KSH, Precision Wires and Vidya Wires (3,000 MTPA new) are adding capacity.

Risk

EHV testing infrastructureLimited accredited high-voltage and short-circuit testing capacity lengthens lead times.

Opportunity

New labsIn-house impulse/UHV labs (TARIL 1200 kV; KLR impulse lab) are being added.

Deepest hidden import dependency: the core, bushing and tap-changer stack — CRGO electrical steel (~90% imported), HV bushings (RIP cores imported) and advanced OLTCs (>80% imported). India builds the transformer but buys its heart and its control.
06 Value pools & cost waterfall where value is captured · and which pool leaks to imports
Where value is captured
  • Power-transformer OEMs — the largest pool; CG Power's Power segment at 21.9% PBIT.
  • EHV/765 kV and HVDC transformers — high entry barriers, pricing power.
  • Backward integration (CTC, pressboard, RIP bushing) — a margin lever.
  • Bushing value — RIP carries a 60–70% premium over OIP.
Who captures it (India-listed)
  • CG Power NSE:CGPOWER — Power segment FY26 ₹5,138 cr (+46%)
  • Hitachi Energy India NSE:POWERINDIA — FY26 revenue ₹8,148 cr; backlog ₹29,555 cr
  • GE Vernova T&D India NSE:GVT&D — FY26 revenue ₹6,206 cr
  • TARIL NSE:TARIL — pure-play, ₹2,395 cr, six backward-integration plants
  • Voltamp / Shilchar / Atlanta — pure-play transformer makers
Pools that leak to imports
  • CRGO core — ~90% imported.
  • OLTC — >80% imported.
  • HV bushings — RIP cores imported.
