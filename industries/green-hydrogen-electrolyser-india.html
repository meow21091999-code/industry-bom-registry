Snapshot

India is trying to build a green-hydrogen economy — splitting water with renewable power — anchored by the National Green Hydrogen Mission (2023). The supply chain is electrolyser → renewable power → water → storage/transport → green ammonia / refinery / steel offtake. Policy has awarded capacity far ahead of delivery: 862,000 t/yr of green H₂ and 3,000 MW/yr of electrolysers, but only ~8,000 t/yr and ~2 GW are commissioned.

Process & equipment
  • Two dominant stack routes: pressurised alkaline (workhorse) and PEM; SOEC and AEM are pilot-stage.
  • Alkaline stack = Ni-coated SS plates, Raney-Ni electrodes, Zirfon diaphragm, KOH lye, nickel foam.
  • PEM stack = PFSA (Nafion) membrane, IrO₂ anode / Pt cathode, gold-coated Ti PTL, gold-coated SS plates.
  • Stack is only 24–32% of cost; balance of plant (68–76%) — rectifiers, transformers, separators, DM water, purification, compression — is where India is strongest.
Bottlenecks
  • Membranes — Nafion (PFSA) and Zirfon are proprietary, near-100% imported; ~0% domestic.
  • Catalysts — iridium (global supply ~7.5 t/yr) and platinum; India has no reserves. Resilience: structural.
  • Critical minerals — nickel, zirconium, titanium, Mo, rare earths; China controls up to ~95% of some. Resilience: years.
  • Stack manufacturing — assembly is localising, but stack IP is licensed from Nel, McPhy, thyssenkrupp nucera, HydrogenPro, Hystar.
  • Demand — green H₂ is 2–3× grey on cost; SIGHT itself names demand creation the key bottleneck.
Value pools
  • BoP & integration is India's real, near-term value pool — 68–76% of electrolyser cost, already ~45% domestic (pumps, vessels, power systems).
  • Stack manufacturing is the contested prize — L&T, Reliance, Adani, Waaree, Advait, BHEL; most IP is licensed in.
  • Downstream molecules (green ammonia, methanol, SAF) capture the export value — Reliance's 15-yr Samsung C&T green-ammonia SPA (US$3bn+) is the template.
  • Project/EPC & the RTC-power annuity sits with NTPC Green, Adani Green, Tata Power, JSW.
  • Leaking to imports: membrane, catalyst, PTL, Ti/Ni feedstock, stack IP, and most mfg equipment.
Market & growth
  • Green-hydrogen market est. ~US$1.4bn today, ~40%+ CAGR FY26–30E value (nominal), off a low base — [REPORTED-T3].
  • Volume growth is policy-led and lumpy: commissioned capacity ~8,000 t/yr vs 862,000 t/yr awarded — conversion, not demand, is the constraint.
  • Electrolyser-equipment market estimates conflict on scope — India electrolyser market put at US$57m (2026) by one house and ~US$100m by another; a third puts it at US$620m by 2030. [CONFLICTING DATA].
  • 3-yr and last-year splits: [UNAVAILABLE] in this pass — base series too thin and scope-inconsistent.
Watch items
  • SIGHT Tranche-I electrolyser SCDs were 9–10 Aug 2026; Tranche-II 26-Mar-2027. As of Jul-2026, no SIGHT plant fully commissioned; several slipped to 2027. [LIVE]
  • ISTS transmission-charge waiver — 25 years, for projects commissioned on/before 31-Dec-2030.
  • Green-ammonia GAPA/GASA signed 31-Mar-2026 at ₹49.75–64.74/kg to 13 fertiliser units (724,000 t/yr).
  • Correction from the prior run: the GST cut on green H₂/electrolysers (18%→5%) is already in force — the 56th GST Council reduced electrolysers and green-hydrogen equipment to 5% w.e.f. 22-Sep-2025; it is not pending. [REPORTED-T1]
Coverage gaps
  • No NSE/BSE pure-play in membranes, catalysts, PTL or bipolar plates — the highest-import nodes are entirely unlisted.
  • Electrolyser manufacturing equipment and stack-IP licences sit with foreign/private firms (Nel, McPhy, thyssenkrupp nucera, Ohmium, John Cockerill).

05 Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunity dual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

Membranes (Nafion / Zirfon)PFSA and Zirfon diaphragms are proprietary, made by a handful of global firms; India's domestic value add here is ~0%. Needed at PEM & alkaline scale. Resilience: structural–years.

Opportunity

Indigenous membrane programmeMNRE/CEEW flag advanced membrane technology as a priority; a domestic PFSA/Zirfon alternative is a defensible white space — currently unlisted.

Risk

Iridium & platinum catalystsGlobal iridium supply is ~7.5 t/yr, a by-product of Pt mining (South Africa >60% of PGMs). Net-zero PEM build-out could need ~30% of global supply; shortages flagged as early as 2030. India has no reserves. Resilience: structural.

Opportunity

Low-loading & recyclingCutting Ir loading (target <0.1 mg Ir/W) and PGM recovery from end-of-life MEAs become their own value pools; alkaline/AEM routes sidestep Ir entirely.

Risk

Critical mineralsNickel, zirconium, titanium, molybdenum and rare earths are near-100% imported; China controls up to ~95% of some. ~1 t of nickel per MW of alkaline electrolyser. Resilience: years.

Opportunity

Process-here, import-oreImport the mineral, do compaction/sintering/coating in India — CEEW sees this adding ~10 pts of indigenisation; Ti PTL → stainless-steel substitution cuts cost too.

Risk

Stack IP & manufacturing equipmentStack technology is licensed from Nel, McPhy, thyssenkrupp nucera, HydrogenPro, Hystar; the R2R casting, coating and sintering tooling is imported. Resilience: years.

Opportunity

Assembly-led localisation is already happeningAdvait (30 MW, Mehsana), Waaree (Dungri), L&T (Hazira) are real; the next step is stack-component and tooling localisation.

Risk

Demand & cost gapSIGHT itself names demand creation the key bottleneck; green H₂ is ₹397/kg vs grey ₹150–200/kg. Awarded 862,000 t/yr but ~8,000 t/yr commissioned (Feb-2026). Resilience: months–years.

Opportunity

Blending mandate + export offtakeRefinery/fertiliser HPO mandates would aggregate demand; green-ammonia export (Reliance–Samsung C&T, US$3bn+) monetises early.

Deepest hidden import dependency: the electrolyser stack itself — membrane, catalyst and stack IP — not the balance of plant. BoP is 68–76% of cost and already ~45% domestic, so headline "localisation" flatters India: the value-dense, IP-heavy 24–32% of the machine is where dependence is structural. Green H₂ is ~65–70% import-dependent along the value chain.
06 Value pools & cost waterfall where value is captured · and which pool leaks to imports
Where value is captured
  • Balance of plant & integration — India's strongest, most localised pool (68–76% of electrolyser cost; ~45% domestic today).
  • Stack manufacturing — the contested prize; L&T, Reliance, Adani, Waaree, Advait, BHEL; mostly licensed IP.
  • Downstream molecules (NH₃, MeOH, SAF) — the export-value pool; Reliance's 15-yr green-ammonia SPA is the template.
  • RTC renewable power + EPC annuity — NTPC Green, Adani Green, Tata Power, JSW.
Who captures it (India-listed)
  • L&T NSE: LT — 300 MW electrolysers (McPhy licence, Hazira); IOCL Panipat 10 KTPA at ₹397/kg
  • Reliance Industries NSE: RELIANCE — 300 MW electrolysers; Jamnagar giga complex; US$3bn+ green-ammonia export SPA
  • Adani Enterprises NSE: ADANIENT — ANIL 198.5 MW + T2; 5 MW off-grid pilot; ~5.3 GW mfg pipeline
  • Waaree Energies NSE: WAAREEENER — 300 MW SIGHT; 1 GW Dungri plant (₹676 cr, FY27)
  • Advait Energy Transitions NSE: ADVAIT — 300 MW PLI; 30 MW assembly live (Mar-2026)
Pools that leak to imports
  • Membranes, catalysts (Ir/Pt/Ni), PTL and bipolar plates — no NSE/BSE pure-play.
  • Stack-IP licences and electrolyser manufacturing equipment — foreign/private.
  • Critical minerals (Ni, Zr, Ti, Mo, rare earths) — near-100% imported.
