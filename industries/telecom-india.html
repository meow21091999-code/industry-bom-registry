00 Digest short summary · full detail in the sections below
Snapshot

India's telecom network-infrastructure stack — the equipment, fibre, silicon, towers and services behind the world's second-largest subscriber base (~1.26 bn wireless, Dec-2025). Scope: India, NSE/BSE.

Process & equipment
  • Equipment is assembled in India (SMT/pick-and-place, RF test) but built around imported chipsets and optics.
  • Optical fibre runs preform → draw → cable; draw towers, preform lathes and cabling lines are the key machinery.
  • Towers are erected on site; site power (SMPS, batteries, DG, solar) is the largest operating cost.
Bottlenecks
  • Fibre preform ~70% imported (only STL integrated) — resilience: years.
  • Telecom chipsets / baseband + RF almost fully imported — structural.
  • RAN BOM only ~4% localised; optical transceivers >$500 mn/yr imported.
  • TEC/MTCTE certification and trusted-source (NSCS) gating; test gear imported.
Value pools
  • Preform captures ~70% of the optical-fibre chain's profit — largely leaks to imports.
  • Indian RAN/OFC assembly value is thin and competitive; equipment margin sits upstream in silicon.
  • Towers and services are domestic and annuity-like (Indus, Airtel) — value stays home.
  • Localisation opportunity: preform (HFCL/Finolex), RF front-end design, transceiver modules.
Watch items
  • Proposed second telecom PLI on non-electronic components (pending notification).
  • Anti-dumping duty on single-mode fibre (5 yrs from Aug-2023).
  • MTCTE Phase III/IV mandates; Right-of-Way Rules 2024 in force.
  • PLI tenure to FY27; Nokia/Ericsson seeking a five-year extension.
Coverage gaps

No listed pure-play: telecom chipsets (baseband/RF) and test & measurement/certification — nearest: domestic fabless + OSAT, and foreign T&M vendors.

05 Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunity dual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

Optical fibre preform~65–75% imported (China, Japan, Korea); only STL is fully backward-integrated. Preform holds ~70% of the profit pool, and only 6–10 companies worldwide make it. Resilience: years.

Opportunity

Second & third integrated playerHFCL’s ~₹580 cr preform plant, Finolex Phase-2 (to ~8 mn fkm) and Polycab’s pilot move India toward 2–3 integrated makers; anti-dumping duty on single-mode fibre protects the domestic industry.

Risk

Telecom chipsets — baseband & RFAlmost fully imported (~$4 bn/yr); no listed pure-play in India. The RF front-end is one of the highest-value parts of a 5G radio. Resilience: structural.

Opportunity

Fabless design + OSATDomestic fabless (Saankhya/Tejas, Silizium, Axiro, Signalchip) under DLI / C2S / TTDF, plus OSAT packaging at Kaynes Sanand — the 5G-modem-and-RF chipset mission is the open prize.

Risk

RAN localisation4G/5G RAN BOM only ~4% localised; switches 3%, GPON ONT 12%. Most of the radio is imported even when “made in India”. Resilience: months–years.

Opportunity

Non-electronic component PLIProposed PLI targets plastic, metal, antenna, RF-filter and connector localisation with rising year-2→5 targets; the BSNL indigenous stack proves the capability.

Risk

Optical transceivers>$500 mn/yr imported (China, Taiwan); high-speed 400G/800G optics concentrated abroad as data-centre demand surges. Resilience: months.

Opportunity

Module integration → designTejas and HFCL integrate metro/access optics today; the AI data-centre interconnect wave is a large, fast-growing domestic opportunity.

Risk

Tower energy & certificationDiesel and battery replacement are the tower cost battleground; and every product is gated by TEC/MTCTE & trusted-source (NSCS) approval — test gear is imported.

Opportunity

Solar + lithium + ESCoIndus has ~30,000 solar sites and cut diesel 6% YoY; the energy-management and battery-replacement cycle is a durable recurring market.

Deepest hidden import dependency: the RF front-end silicon and high-speed optical DSPs inside every radio, router and transport box — and, one layer up, the specialty fibre grades and precision ceramic ferrules that India still cannot make at scale.
06 Value pools & cost waterfall where value is captured · and which pool leaks to imports
Where value is captured
  • Preform captures ~70% of the optical-fibre chain's profit — largely leaks to imports.
  • Indian RAN/OFC assembly value is thin and competitive; equipment margin sits upstream in silicon.
  • Towers and services are domestic and annuity-like (Indus, Airtel) — value stays home.
  • Localisation opportunity: preform (HFCL/Finolex), RF front-end design, transceiver modules.
Who captures it (India-listed)
  • Tejas Networks NSE/BSE: TEJASNET — Supplied 4G/5G RAN for 100,000+ BSNL sites; FY25 revenue ₹8,923 cr
  • HFCL NSE/BSE: HFCL — 5G FWA CPE, Wi-Fi AP and routers; large 5G R&D team
  • ITI Limited NSE/BSE: ITI — Contract-manufactured ~8,633 BSNL 4G eNodeB sites for Tejas
  • Dixon Technologies NSE/BSE: DIXON — PLI-approved for Open-RAN (RU/DU/CU) and GPON ONT
  • Netweb Technologies NSE/BSE: NETWEB — PLI-approved telecom/networking products
  • Astra Microwave Products NSE/BSE: ASTRAMICRO — RF/microwave modules and antennas (largely defence)
  • Data Patterns (India) NSE/BSE: DATAPATTNS — Defence/telecom electronics and RF subsystems
Pools that leak to imports
  • No listed pure-play: telecom chipsets (baseband/RF) and test & measurement/certification — nearest: domestic fabless + OSAT, and foreign T&M vendors.
  • Bottleneck nodes: 5G / 4G RAN — Base Stations & Radios, Telecom Chipsets — Baseband & RF Front-End, Optical Fibre Preform, Optical Transceivers & Modules, Test & Measurement / TEC–MTCTE Certification.
