Snapshot

India’s telecom network-infrastructure stack — the equipment, fibre, silicon, towers and services behind the world’s second-largest subscriber base (1,348 mn telephone subscribers, Jun-2026). The demand side is monetising (industry ARPU ₹187.12; a 12–15% tariff hike expected within months), while the supply side stays import-heavy: roughly 60% import substitution at product level, but >80% of critical components sourced from China. Scope: India, NSE/BSE.

Process & equipment
  • Equipment is assembled in India (SMT/pick-and-place, RF test) but built around imported chipsets, RF front-ends and high-speed optics.
  • Optical fibre runs preform → draw → cable; draw towers, preform lathes (MCVD/OVD/VAD) and cabling lines are the key machinery — all being expanded now (HFCL, Finolex, STL).
  • Towers are erected on site; site power (SMPS, batteries, DG, solar) is the largest operating cost, with ~46,000 solarised sites at Indus.
Bottlenecks
  • Fibre preform: ~65–75% imported (Japan/China/Korea); only STL is fully backward-integrated, HFCL and Finolex are building. Resilience: years (shortening).
  • Telecom chipsets / baseband + RF: almost fully imported (~$4–5 bn/yr); no listed pure-play. Resilience: structural.
  • RAN BOM only ~4% localised; optical transceivers ~$0.6 bn/yr imported (USA/Taiwan/China).
  • High-end DCS/optics silicon and MTCTE/trusted-source (NSCS) gating; test gear imported.
Value pools
  • Preform captures ~70% of the optical-fibre chain’s profit — largely leaked to imports, now being pulled home by HFCL (§1,800 cr), Finolex and STL.
  • Indian RAN/OFC assembly value is thin and competitive; equipment margin sits upstream in silicon and photonics.
  • Towers and services are domestic and annuity-like (Indus, Airtel, Jio) — value stays home.
  • Localisation opportunity: preform & specialty fibre (HFCL/Finolex/STL), optical transceivers (Dixon–Gemtek, GX Quantum Photonics), RF front-end design (fabless + Kaynes OSAT).
Market & growth
  • India telecom & network equipment (TANE), scope: telecom/network equipment, India: $25 bn (FY25) → $50 bn (FY32), CAGR ~10% [REPORTED-T3, NITI Aayog / KPMG, Oct-2026]. Value (nominal); volume [UNAVAILABLE]. Growth led by physical build-out (5G densification, fibre/data-centre), so the volume mix is positive.
  • Mobile network traffic (scope: India mobile traffic): ~17% CAGR 2025→2030 vs operator revenue ~2.7% [REPORTED-T3, KPMG]. Value (nominal) vs volume — the traffic/revenue gap is the monetisation problem.
  • Last-year: telecom gross revenue ₹4,04,067 cr FY26 (+8.59%); AGR ₹3,34,659 cr (+10.44%) [REPORTED-T1, TRAI Yearly Indicators 2025-26].
  • [CONFLICTING DATA] on the equipment-market CAGR (different scopes): ~10% [NITI Aayog/KPMG], 10.41% [MarketsandData, $22.5 bn FY24→$49.7 bn FY32], 3.66% [IMARC, $25.4 bn 2025→$35.5 bn 2034].
Watch items
  • Telecom PLI 2.0 — successor to the ₹12,195 cr scheme (ends Mar-2027) at a proposed ₹6,000–7,000 cr, focused on components/design-led manufacturing; targeting Q1 FY28 launch (DoT/EFC, Oct-2026). [LIVE]
  • Jio Platforms IPO — RHP 14-Oct-2026, price band ₹1,065–1,119, issue ₹30,213 cr, opens 21-Oct, lists 28-Oct; capex cycle peaking. [LIVE]
  • Telecom Manufacturing Zone (TMZ) Gwalior — Asia’s first; ~₹5,500 cr, 24 companies, 170 acres; DoT proposed ~₹500 cr common testing lab. [CYCLICAL]
  • Anti-dumping: India’s ADD on single-mode fibre from China/Indonesia/Korea (5 yrs from Aug-2023) stays; China extended its own ADD on Indian SMOF for 5 yrs from 14-Aug-2026 — a new export barrier. [LIVE]
Coverage gaps
  • No listed pure-play: telecom chipsets (baseband/RF) and test & measurement/MTCTE certification — nearest are domestic fabless + Kaynes OSAT, and foreign T&M vendors. High-speed optical DSPs remain an import-only pool.

05Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunitydual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

Optical fibre preform~65–75% imported (Japan, China, Korea); only STL is fully backward-integrated. Preform holds ~70% of the profit pool, and only 6–10 companies worldwide make it. Global fibre is in shortage as AI data centres absorb supply, lifting fibre/preform prices 20–25%. Resilience: years (shortening).

Opportunity

Second & third integrated playerHFCL has approved a ~₹1,800 cr preform/fibre/cable programme (600 MT preform, 43.1 mn fkm fibre, 62 mn fkm cable by 2028); Finolex commissioned 100 MT (to 200 MT) preform and is doubling fibre/OFC to 8 mn fkm; STL remains fully integrated. Anti-dumping duty on single-mode fibre protects the domestic industry.

Risk

Telecom chipsets — baseband & RFAlmost fully imported; no listed pure-play in India. NITI Aayog reports >80% of critical components (4G/5G antennas, signal processors) come from China. The RF front-end is one of the highest-value parts of a 5G radio. Resilience: structural.

Opportunity

Fabless design + OSATDomestic fabless (Saankhya Labs/Tejas, Signalchip, WiSig, Aheesa) under DLI / C2S / TTDF (23 design projects approved by MeitY), plus commercial OSAT at Kaynes Semicon (Sanand, from Mar-2026) — the 5G-modem-and-RF chipset mission is the open prize.

Risk

RAN localisation4G/5G RAN BOM only ~4% localised; switches 3%, GPON ONT 12%; average domestic value addition ~27.8%. Most of the radio is imported even when “made in India”. Resilience: months–years.

Opportunity

PLI 2.0 & Telecom Manufacturing ZoneA proposed ₹6,000–7,000 cr PLI 2.0 targets components and design-led manufacturing; the Gwalior TMZ (~₹5,500 cr, 24 firms, 170 acres) and the BSNL indigenous stack prove the capability and create a component cluster.

Risk

Optical transceivers~$0.6 bn/yr imported (USA ~50%, Taiwan ~25%, China ~15%); high-speed 400G/800G optics concentrated abroad as data-centre demand surges. Resilience: months.

Opportunity

Module integration → photonicsDixon–Gemtek JV (60:40) for SFP/BOSA modules, GX Quantum Photonics’ ~₹500 cr Bhiwadi photonics fab (production from late-2026) and CloudPhotonix (Noida, up to 800G) are building India’s first transceiver base — the AI data-centre interconnect wave is a large, fast-growing domestic opportunity.

Risk

Tower energy & certificationDiesel and battery replacement are the tower cost battleground (battery supplies were disrupted by geopolitics in FY27 Q1); every product is gated by MTCTE & trusted-source (NSCS) approval — test gear is imported. Resilience: months.

Opportunity

Solar + lithium + ESCoIndus has ~46,000 solarised sites and cut diesel 13% YoY (Q1 FY27); Airtel solarised ~27,000 sites in two years. The energy-management, lithium-battery and testing-lab (§500 cr common testing lab proposed at TMZ) cycles are durable recurring markets.

Deepest hidden import dependency: the RF front-end silicon and high-speed optical DSPs inside every radio, router and transport box — and, one layer up, the specialty fibre grades, germanium dopant and precision optical sub-assemblies (laser dies, BOSA/TOSA/ROSA) that India still cannot make at scale.
06 Value pools & cost waterfall where value is captured · and which pool leaks to imports
Where value is captured
  • Preform captures ~70% of the optical-fibre chain’s profit — largely leaked to imports, now being pulled home (HFCL, Finolex, STL).
  • Indian RAN/OFC assembly value is thin and competitive; equipment margin sits upstream in silicon and photonics.
  • Towers and services are domestic and annuity-like (Indus, Airtel, Jio) — value stays home.
  • Localisation opportunity: preform/specialty fibre, optical transceivers (Dixon–Gemtek, GX), RF front-end design (fabless + OSAT).
Who captures it (India-listed)
  • HFCL NSE/BSE: HFCL — OF/OFC/preform (₹1,800 cr capex), 5G FWA CPE, routers; FY26 revenue ₹4,949 cr (+21.8%), PAT ₹329 cr (+90%); order book ₹26,665 cr (Jun-2026)
  • Tejas Networks NSE/BSE: TEJASNET — indigenous 4G/5G RAN, transport, optics; FY26 revenue ₹1,103 cr; Q2 FY27 ₹1,537 cr TCS LOI (18,685 BSNL sites)
  • Sterlite Technologies NSE/BSE: STLTECH — only fully integrated fibre maker (preform→cable); ~₹500 cr FY27 capex
  • Finolex Cables NSE/BSE: FINCABLES — preform 100→200 MT, fibre/OFC to 8 mn fkm
  • Dixon Technologies NSE/BSE: DIXON — Gemtek JV for optical transceivers/BOSA; 5G FWA, routers, backhaul
  • Indus Towers NSE/BSE: INDUSTOWER — 264,514 macro towers / 428,014 co-locations (Mar-2026); FY26 revenue ₹32,493 cr (+7.9%)
  • Bharti Airtel NSE/BSE: BHARTIARTL · Reliance (Jio) NSE/BSE: RELIANCE · Vodafone Idea NSE/BSE: IDEA — the operator annuity layer
  • Kaynes Technology NSE/BSE: KAYNES — Kaynes Semicon OSAT (Sanand)
Pools that leak to imports
  • No listed pure-play: telecom chipsets (baseband/RF) and test & measurement/MTCTE certification — nearest: domestic fabless + Kaynes OSAT, and foreign T&M vendors.
  • High-speed optical DSPs and laser dies; germanium dopant and specialty preform feedstock (mostly Japan).
  • Bottleneck nodes: 5G / 4G RAN — Base Stations & Radios, Telecom Chipsets — Baseband & RF Front-End, Optical Fibre Preform, Optical Transceivers & Modules, Test & Measurement / TEC–MTCTE Certification.
