Snapshot

Servo motors and drives translate a control signal into precise, repeatable motion — the motion core of robots, CNC machines and automated lines. India's market is ~US$450 mn and growing, but it is predominantly served by global OEMs; domestic players do licensed assembly and integration, not indigenous design.

Process & equipment
  • Motor: stator winding, lamination stacking, rotor/magnet assembly, balancing, encapsulation.
  • Drive: power-stage assembly (IGBT/MOSFET modules), control board (MCU/DSP), encoder interface, firmware.
  • Gating inputs: rare-earth magnets, encoder chips, power semiconductors, precision bearings.
Bottlenecks
  • Rare-earth magnets (~95% imported) and encoder chips (no Indian maker).
  • Power semiconductors (IGBT/MOSFET) — no domestic capability.
  • Design IP — local lines are licensed, not indigenous.
  • Skilled automation engineers in short supply.
Value pools
  • Motors: Bharat Bijlee (PM servo), CG Power (motors + drives), Sona Comstar (motors/actuators).
  • Drives: Bharat Bijlee (KEB), CG Power (Emotron), ABB India, Siemens.
  • The highest-value inputs (magnets, encoders, power silicon) leak to imports.
Market & growth
  • India servo & drives market US$452 mn (2025) → US$642 mn by 2034 — 3.8% CAGR [REPORTED: IMARC].
  • Import CAGR +16.4% over 2020–24, including +30% in 2023–24 alone — imports growing roughly four times faster than the domestic market [REPORTED].
  • That gap is the substitution runway; an India-volume series is [UNAVAILABLE] in this pass.
Watch items
  • Mitsubishi/Panasonic localisation moves; PLI for semiconductors, ACC and electronics.
  • REPM magnet scheme (₹7,280 cr) — upstream enabler.
  • Make-in-India drive to shift from assembly to design.
Coverage gaps

No NSE/BSE pure-play in servo drives as a product; power semiconductors, encoder chips and frameless torque motors have no listed Indian maker. Proxies: Bharat Bijlee, CG Power, Sona Comstar, ABB India, Siemens.

05 Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunity dual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

Rare-earth magnets~95% imported; the input to every servo motor. Resilience: years / structural.

Opportunity

REPM magnet scheme₹7,280 cr / 6,000 MTPA REPM scheme; Sona Comstar and motor makers form captive demand.

Risk

Encoder chips & power semiconductorsNo Indian maker of high-resolution encoder chips or drive-grade IGBT/MOSFET. Resilience: years.

Opportunity

Semiconductor localisationIndia Semiconductor Mission + OSAT ramp; the drive-grade device is the specific gap.

Risk

Licensed-not-designedLocal lines assemble foreign designs; the IP stays abroad. Resilience: years.

Opportunity

Design capability build-outThe route runs from the encoder and magnet up, not the finished drive down.

Risk

Skilled automation engineersManufacturers cite a shortage of automation engineers and high upfront capital. Resilience: months–years.

Opportunity

Training & engineering servicesIndia's engineering-services layer (Tata Elxsi, Cyient, KPIT) is a bridge to product design.

Risk

Import dependence at 60%+Over 60% of AC servo systems imported; a supplier or currency move re-prices the market. Resilience: months.

Opportunity

Servo localisationBharat Bijlee, CG Power scaling; Mitsubishi/Panasonic local assembly.

Deepest hidden dependency: the rare-earth magnet, encoder chip and power semiconductor inside the motor and drive. India can assemble a servo package; it cannot yet make the three inputs that define its precision.
06 Value pools & cost waterfall where value is captured · and which pool leaks to imports
Where value is captured
  • Motors: Bharat Bijlee (PM servo), CG Power, Sona Comstar.
  • Drives: Bharat Bijlee (KEB), CG Power (Emotron), ABB India, Siemens.
  • Global OEMs capture the design premium; Indian firms capture assembly/integration.
Who captures it (India-listed)
  • Bharat Bijlee — indigenously developed SynchroTorq PM servo motors, 4,000+ packages.
  • CG Power — LV/MV motors and Emotron drives.
  • ABB India / Siemens — drives and motion, largely foreign design.
Pools that leak to imports
  • Magnets, encoder chips, power semiconductors and frameless torque motors.
  • High-precision servo variants — still predominantly imported.
