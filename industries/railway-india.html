Snapshot

The fourth-largest network in the world (~68,000 route km, almost fully electrified) and the second-largest freight carrier at 1,670 MT in FY26. One buyer spends ₹2.93 lakh cr of FY27 capex on rolling stock, track, signalling and stations.

Process & equipment

Rolling stock is fabricated: car-body welding, bogie and wheelset build-up, brake and coupler fitment, electrical outfitting, testing. Wheels are forged on 10,000-t presses, heat-treated, machined and ultrasonically tested.

Track is a steel chain: 260 m rails from Bhilai, flash-butt welded, laid on concrete sleepers with elastic fastenings, tamped by track machines. Signalling is SIL-4 electronics plus towers and optical fibre.

Bottlenecks

Wheelsets — years. Imported for six decades; the new domestic JV reaches 80,000/yr from a standing start, each wheelset needing RDSO qualification.

RDSO / SIL-4 approval — years. Kavach has three approved makers, which is why one order can be ₹2,466 cr to a single firm.

Specialty bearings and propulsion electronics — months to years, and made here mostly inside MNC plants.

Value pools

Rolling-stock OEM is thin: 10-13% EBITDA, and revenue fell 14-26% in FY26 when wheelsets ran short.

Components earn more — Jupiter's wheelset business ran ~17% EBITDA against a 12.4% group margin.

The 35-year maintenance annuity is the richest contracted pool: Siemens (1,200 locos), Alstom (800), Wabtec (1,000), Kinet (120 trainsets).

Signalling is small (₹7,500 cr FY27) but concentrated and safety-gated; rails leak to nobody — SAIL supplies them.

05Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunitydual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

Wheelsets were the binding constraint of FY26 — resilience: yearsForged wheels were imported for six decades (UK, Czech Republic, Brazil, Romania, Japan, China, Ukraine, Russia) and IR bought ~80,000 a year worth ~₹520 cr in 2022-23 and ~₹900 cr in FY25. When supply broke, production broke with it: Jupiter's wagon dispatches fell 35% in nine months of FY26 and its revenue fell 26.4%; Titagarh dispatched 7,019 wagons against a 12,000-unit capacity. A wheelset is not a commodity purchase — it is a press-fitted, ultrasonically tested, RDSO-qualified assembly with a four-to-five-year life.

Opportunity

Three private plants are being built at once: Ramkrishna-Titagarh (2.28 lakh forged wheels/yr, commercial from Aug-2026), Jupiter's Odisha integrated wheelset plant (~1 lakh wheelsets/yr, partial by Mar-2027) and Texmaco-BVV at MoU stage. The fastest cash is not the wheel — it is wheelset machining, assembly and axle supply, which Jupiter already runs at ~2,000 sets/month at 17% EBITDA. Beyond that, the 20-year offtake contract structure (80,000 wheels/yr guaranteed) is the template for financing any other import-substitution node.

Risk

A single certification gate sets the pace of the whole safety market — resilience: yearsKavach runs on SIL-4 certification and RDSO specification approval; three vendors are approved (Medha, HBL, Kernex) and two more are in process. That is why a single CLW order can be ₹2,466 cr to one company, and why commissioning reached 2,633 route km by 31-Jul-2026 against a 21,794 RKm programme. Certification, not capital, is the constraint.

Opportunity

Certification capability is itself a market: SIL-4 product development, independent safety assessment, RFID tag supply, station data centres (1,009 built), telecom towers (1,721) and 11,547 km of optical-fibre cable. The next layer — moving block, ATS/ATO and centralised traffic control on the Kavach platform — is being built through the Kernex-BHEL JV, which is where a new entrant would have to partner rather than compete.

Risk

Propulsion electronics and specialty bearings sit inside foreign-owned plants — resilience: months to yearsTraction converters, IGBT modules, main transformers, CTRB and insulated traction-motor bearings are all made in India, but largely in plants owned by Siemens, ABB, Hitachi Energy, SKF, Timken, Schaeffler, Wabtec and Knorr-Bremse. Design authority and the highest-margin sub-components (semiconductor modules, ceramic-hybrid insulated bearings) stay abroad. Alstom's Madhepura plant is ~90% localised and still imports wheels and axles because no Indian source exists for that design and specification.

Opportunity

Titagarh's propulsion SBU (ABB technology transfer, integrated propulsion and TCMS test benches, ~₹500 cr order book) and a ~400-unit propulsion-system tender pipeline (~₹2,000 cr) are the first genuine Indian-owned entry. NBC is developing insulated and hybrid-ceramic traction-motor bearings for Vande Bharat and metro fleets. The realistic play is design-and-assemble with licensed power modules, not semiconductor fabrication.

Risk

Aluminium coach bodies and high-speed rail are the next capability gap — resilience: months (closing now)India's metro and mainline coaches are predominantly stainless steel. Aluminium bodies need extrusion, friction-stir welding and flat-pack fabrication, which Titagarh imported from Firema (Italy) until it installed the machinery; full self-sufficiency is guided for FY27 and early FY28. Seven high-speed corridors (~4,000 km, ~₹16 lakh cr of proposed investment) will be aluminium-bodied, and the two BEML high-speed prototypes are being equipped by Knorr-Bremse.

Opportunity

This is the one node where the gap is closing on a dated schedule rather than a hope: Titagarh expects end-to-end aluminium capability (extrusion onwards) by FY28 and already has the only Indian aluminium metro coach line (Pune, plus 12 repeat trainsets). Extrusion, FSW tooling and interior systems are the sub-nodes still to localise, and the high-speed corridor programme is the demand anchor that makes the investment rational.

Risk

Skilled trades and operating staff bind as tightly as materials — resilience: yearsKavach alone has required training ~94,000 people, including ~57,000 loco pilots and assistant pilots, at IRISET-designed courses. DFC throughput is limited by shortages of loco pilots and guards and by feeder-route congestion, not only by track capacity. Welding, NDT and functional-safety engineering for EN-standard car bodies are certification-gated trades that cannot be bought quickly.

Opportunity

Training and certification is a service market with no listed Indian pure-play: IR's own institutes, IRISET and OEM training programmes (Alstom has trained 22,000+ IR staff) absorb it. For a supplier, in-house certification capability is the entry ticket to EN 15085 welding and SIL-4 work; for an investor it is a reason the incumbents' positions are durable.

Deepest hidden import dependency: not the coach and not the locomotive — the wheel, the bearing and the power module. India fabricates car bodies, assembles trainsets and has localised locomotives to ~90%, but until 2026 every high-speed wheel was forged abroad, the highest-specification bearings are made in India only inside foreign-owned plants, and the traction converter's semiconductors are imported. The wheel is now closing on a dated schedule; the other two are not.
06Value pools & cost waterfallwhere value is captured · and which pool leaks to imports
Where value is captured
  • Rolling-stock OEM — competitive and thin: 10-13% EBITDA across the four listed makers, with FY26 revenue down 14-26% on wheelset shortage.
  • Components and wheelsets — better: Jupiter's wheelset business ran ~17% EBITDA against a 12.4% group margin.
  • 35-year service annuities — the richest contracted pool: Siemens (1,200 locos), Alstom (800), Wabtec (1,000), Kinet (120 trainsets) and Titagarh-BHEL (80) all bundle decades of maintenance.
  • Signalling — small revenue (₹7,500 cr FY27) but concentrated, safety-gated and priced on certification rather than metal.
  • Track materials — commodity pass-through; SAIL supplies the rails and captures volume, not margin.
Who captures it (India-listed)
  • Titagarh Rail Systems NSE: TITAGARH — FY26 revenue ₹3,143.6 cr, order book ₹27,540 cr incl. JV; passenger rail is now 81% of the rail backlog
  • Texmaco Rail NSE: TEXRAIL — ₹4,377 cr revenue; 96.4% of the freight-car book now private and export, which de-risks the IR tender cycle
  • Jupiter Wagons NSE: JUPITERWAGONS — ₹2,915.7 cr revenue; the deepest component play (wheelsets, brakes, containers, BESS)
  • Ramkrishna Forgings NSE: RKFORGE — lead partner of the only 20-year forged-wheel offtake contract in the country
  • Kernex / HBL / Quadrant NSE: KERNEX, HBLENGINE, QUADRANT — the Kavach oligopoly
Pools that leak to imports
  • Wheels and axles for the WAG-12B design (Alstom still sources from China and Ukraine); premium passenger wheels until the Chennai plant ramps.
  • Power semiconductors, some traction converters and main transformers; highest-spec bearings.
  • Aluminium flat packs and extrusion technology until FY28; design IP for brakes, couplers and doors (Wabtec, Knorr-Bremse, Faiveley).
  • Large forging presses, friction-stir welding lines and wheel-testing rigs — the machines that make the parts.
