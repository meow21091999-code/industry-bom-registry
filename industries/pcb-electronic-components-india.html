DGDigest — at a glanceshort summary; full detail below
Snapshot
  • Bare PCBs and the components that populate them — the substrate layer beneath India's ~USD 115 bn electronics production.
  • India consumes ~₹35,000 cr (USD 4.2 bn) of PCBs but makes only ~10–15% locally; the rest is imported, mainly from China, Taiwan and Korea.
Process & equipment
  • Bare-board line: CCL cut & inner-layer imaging → lamination → CNC drilling → PTH / plating → outer imaging & etching → solder mask → surface finish (ENIG / HASL) → electrical test.
  • Key machinery — CNC drillers, LDI / exposure, plating & etching lines, multilayer presses, AOI and flying-probe testers — is almost entirely imported (China, Taiwan, Italy, Germany, Japan).
  • Assembly (PCBA) is the domestic stronghold: SMT, reflow, AOI / X-ray and box-build.
Bottlenecks
  • Copper-clad laminate (CCL) & prepreg — 100% imported until 2026; India's first CCL plant (Wipro) starts 2026. Resilience: months→years.
  • Copper foil & glass fabric — imported; a thin domestic base. Resilience: years.
  • HDI / flex and ≥8-layer boards — imported; new ECMS capacity from FY27. Resilience: years.
  • PCB machinery & drills — imported; DGTR anti-dumping probe on Chinese PCB drills/routers (Sep 2025). Resilience: months.
  • Capacitors (MLCC / film) — ~20% domestic. Resilience: structural.
Value pools
  • Value sits upstream of assembly: laminate (~30% of PCB BOM), copper foil, HDI / flex fabrication and capital equipment.
  • Domestic bare-PCB fabrication is small and thin-margin but protected by the 30% anti-dumping duty (Mar 2024).
  • The ECMS ₹40,000 cr outlay is redirecting value into laminate, HDI and equipment — Amber, Kaynes, Syrma and Wipro are the main vehicles.
  • Listed pure-play capture is thin: BPL and Fine-Line are small; most PCB capacity is unlisted.
  • EMS / PCBA (Dixon, Kaynes, Syrma) is where listed exposure actually sits, but the board itself still leaks to imports.
Market & growth
  • India PCB demand ~₹35,000 cr FY25; ELCINA projects ~USD 14 bn by FY30 [REPORTED-T2].
  • Value CAGR ~15% (2025–2034) [CALCULATED] from IMARC sizes; volume CAGR [UNAVAILABLE].
  • Domestic bare-PCB output to rise from ~13% of demand (FY24) to ~31% (FY29) — Avendus [REPORTED-T2].
  • [CONFLICTING DATA]: imported share cited at 88% (ELCINA), ~90% (Ind-Ra), 85–90% (Jefferies) vs “45% met domestically” (Vaishnaw, Aug 2026) — scopes differ.
Watch items
  • ECMS 5th tranche, 17-Aug-2026: 106 projects approved, outlay raised to ₹40,000 cr. [LIVE]
  • Amber–Korea Circuit JV (₹3,250 cr) at YEIDA for HDI / flex + substrates; Kaynes Chennai PCB line due to start ~Sep 2026.
  • 30% anti-dumping duty on bare PCB (≤6-layer) runs to Mar 2029; ADD probe on PCB drills/routers open. [LIVE]
  • Customs: BCD on populated PCBA cut 20%→15% (Budget 2025); bare PCB BCD 0% plus the anti-dumping duty.
Coverage gaps
  • HDI, flex, CCL, copper foil, glass fabric and PCB-equipment nodes have no NSE/BSE pure-play — capacity is unlisted or foreign-owned.
  • Only small listed bare-PCB names (BPL, Fine-Line) and passives (Gujarat Poly, Shivalik) exist in the selected universe.

05 Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunity dual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

Copper-clad laminate (CCL) & prepreg100% imported until 2026; CCL is ~30% of a PCB's bill of materials and the single largest upstream gap. Resilience: months→years.

Opportunity

First domestic CCL plantsWipro Electronic Materials ₹500 cr (Karnataka, 2026, 6 mn sheets/yr); Amber CCL Mysuru (₹800 cr); MeitY reports 80% laminate self-sufficiency by Aug 2026.

Risk

HDI / flex & ≥8-layer boardsImported; the ≥8-layer and mobile boards are exempt from the anti-dumping duty, so high-value boards face no protection. Resilience: years.

Opportunity

ECMS HDI / flex build-outKaynes ₹4,995 cr Thoothukudi (74-layer / HDI / flex); Amber Ascent ₹991 cr Hosur + Ascent-K JV ₹3,215 cr (YEIDA); Syrma–Shinhyup JV.

Risk

PCB machinery & drillsCNC drillers, imaging, plating lines and presses are almost fully imported from China / Taiwan / Europe; a single delayed line gates a plant's ramp. Resilience: months.

Opportunity

Capital-goods ECMS tierJyoti CNC and Ind-Sphinx won ECMS capital-goods approvals (Aug 2026); DGTR opened an anti-dumping probe on Chinese PCB drills / routers (Sep 2025).

Risk

Passive componentsCapacitors are only ~20% domestic and connectors ~75%; MLCC and film capacitors remain foreign-concentrated. Resilience: structural.

Opportunity

Passive / electro-mech ECMS roundECMS now covers capacitors, connectors, relays, speakers and metallised film (SRF); listed proxies are Gujarat Poly (MLCC) and Shivalik Bimetal (shunt resistors).

Deepest hidden import dependency: the substrate beneath the substrate — electro-deposited copper foil and woven glass fabric, the two inputs to the copper-clad laminate. India has no PCB-grade ED-foil line and only two (foreign-owned) glass-fibre makers, so even a "localised" CCL plant imports its own feedstock.
06 Value pools & cost waterfall where value is captured · and which pool leaks to imports
Where value is captured
  • Upstream of the board: laminate (~30% of PCB BOM), copper foil and glass fabric.
  • HDI / flex fabrication — the highest-value board types, still imported.
  • Capital equipment — the deepest, least-localised pool.
  • PCBA / box-build — large but thin-margin domestic pool.
Who captures it (India-listed)
  • BPL NSE:BPL and Fine-Line BSE:512264 — small listed bare-PCB pure-plays.
  • Amber, Kaynes, Syrma — listed EMS building PCB / laminate capacity.
  • Gujarat Poly, Shivalik Bimetal, Salzer — listed passives / connectors.
  • Epigral, SRF, Hindalco, Jyoti CNC — listed material / equipment suppliers.
Pools that leak to imports
  • CCL / laminate, copper foil, glass fabric — the largest leak.
  • HDI / flex and ≥8-layer boards — no duty protection.
  • Capacitors and connectors — foreign-concentrated.
  • PCB manufacturing equipment — almost fully imported.
