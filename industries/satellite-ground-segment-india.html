Snapshot
  • The ground side: earth stations, gateways, VSAT terminals, ground RF/TT&C and GSaaS.
  • Policy-created demand: DoT's 20%-in-5-years ground-segment indigenisation mandate.
Process & equipment
  • Antenna fabrication, pedestal/tracking systems, RF-chain integration and gateway commissioning.
  • Field installation and CAMC across distributed VSAT networks.
Bottlenecks
  • Ground-segment localisation — high-end RF/modems imported (months).
  • High-end modems & baseband — foreign proprietary hardware (months).
Value pools
  • Gateway and VSAT integration captured domestically (L&T, BEL, Avantel, Nelco).
  • Service/annuity value sits with operators (Nelco ~40% VSAT share; Hughes 160k+ VSATs).
Watch items
  • Satcom spectrum allocation and commercial rollout.
  • The 5-year ground-segment indigenisation clock.
Coverage gaps
  • GSaaS is early — Avantel is the emerging listed play; Hughes India and Bhawani are unlisted.

05Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunitydual-readable
Risk

Ground-segment localisationDoT mandates 20% indigenisation in 5 years, yet high-end RF, modems and antennas are largely imported. Resilience: months.

Opportunity

Gateway & VSAT build-outNSIL rate-contract RFPs and L&T gateway execution are a recurring domestic integration market (BEL, Avantel, Nelco).

Risk

High-end modems & basebandDepends on foreign proprietary hardware (e.g. iDirect, Gilat). Resilience: months.

Opportunity

Domestic RF/basebandAstra Microwave, BEL and Avantel can substitute at the sub-system level as volumes build.
