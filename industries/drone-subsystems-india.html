Snapshot

A drone is an airframe, a flight controller/autopilot, ESCs, BLDC motors, propellers, avionics (IMU/GNSS/datalink), optics/gimbals and a battery. India assembles airframes and writes autopilot software well, but imports the propulsion and control-electronics core — ~50–60% of subsystem value.

Process & equipment
  • Propulsion: BLDC motor winding, magnet assembly, rotor balancing; ESC = power-stage PCBA + FOC firmware.
  • Avionics: flight-controller PCBA (MCU/IMU), RF datalink modules, gimbal/optics assembly.
  • Gating inputs: magnets, power MOSFETs, MEMS IMUs, camera sensors — imported.
Bottlenecks
  • Flight controllers/autopilot and ESCs — imported core.
  • BLDC motors and propellers — domestic entrants emerging but young.
  • Optics/gimbals and RF datalinks — largely imported.
  • Subsystem R&D — the target of ANRF MAHA Drones.
Value pools
  • OEM/assembly and DaaS: ideaForge, Zen, Paras, DroneAcharya.
  • Component localisation: Zen's Vector Technics is the early listed step.
  • Training/RPTO and DaaS are recurring pools.
Watch items
  • PLI 2.0 disbursement; Mission Drone Shakti facility timelines; ANRF demonstrators.
  • DGCA type certification and the shift from Drone Rules to an Act.
  • Export entry via SCOMET Category 5B.
Coverage gaps

No listed pure-play in drone motors, ESCs, flight controllers or optics. Zen (via Vector Technics) is the closest listed component exposure; ideaForge/DroneAcharya are OEM/assembly.

05 Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunity dual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

Flight controllers & autopilotThe drone's brain; largely imported or open-source-based. Resilience: months–years.

Opportunity

Indigenous autopilotZuppa's NavGati on C-DAC PCBs/processors shows the route; ANRF funds this.

Risk

ESCs & BLDC motorsPropulsion electronics and motors were ~imported; Indian entrants (Zepco, Flameback, Enercomp, Reflex, ZDrive) are young. Resilience: months–years.

Opportunity

Domestic propulsionZepco's 16 kW record motor and 100% Indian-content ESCs prove feasibility.

Risk

Optics & gimbalsSurveillance/defence drones need gimbals and cameras, largely imported. Resilience: years.

Opportunity

Optics localisationParas Defence's optics/defence-electronics base is the nearest listed capability.

Risk

Subsystem R&D gapPLI 1.0 rewarded assembly, not subsystems (40% DVA could still import 60%). Resilience: years.

Opportunity

PLI 2.0 / Mission Drone Shakti / ANRFPLI 2.0 measures total drone value; Mission Drone Shakti (₹10,000 cr) funds facilities; ANRF funds component R&D.

Risk

SkillsEmbedded, avionics, propulsion and DGCA-certified pilots in short supply. Resilience: months–years.

Opportunity

RPTOs & training244 RPTOs and growing; training is itself a value pool.

Deepest hidden dependency: the flight controller/autopilot silicon and the motor magnets. India can write the autonomy software and build the airframe; the control electronics and propulsion core still travel through import channels.
06 Value pools & cost waterfall where value is captured · and which pool leaks to imports
Where value is captured
  • OEM/assembly and DaaS: ideaForge, Zen, Paras, DroneAcharya.
  • Component localisation is the open pool; Zen's Vector Technics is the early listed step.
  • Training/RPTO and services are recurring.
Who captures it (India-listed)
  • ideaForge — pure-play surveillance/mapping drone OEM.
  • Zen Technologies — anti-drone + components (Vector Technics) + loitering munitions (TISA).
  • Paras Defence — optics/defence electronics + drone subsidiaries.
  • DroneAcharya — services, training and agri-drone manufacturing.
Pools that leak to imports
  • Flight controllers, ESCs, BLDC motors, optics/gimbals, RF datalinks.
  • Magnets and power MOSFETs inside them.
