Snapshot

Component MRO is India's 'missing middle' — the highest-value node it does not control. Aircraft may be checked and even engines overhauled in India, but the fuel systems, avionics LRUs, landing gear, APUs and hydraulics still fly abroad. It is the most independent-friendly segment (lower spares intensity, PMA/DER routes) and therefore the clearest localisation opportunity.

Process & equipment
  • Remove at a check → repair to the CMM → bench/functional test → release.
  • Gating inputs: OEM data, test benches, and the CAR-145 C-rating held.
  • Equipment: component test benches/ATE, autoclaves, plating lines, plasma spray, CMM, NDT.
Bottlenecks
  • ~100% of components go overseas — years.
  • OEM data/tooling licences for high-end LRUs — years.
  • Test equipment & PMA/DER capability gap — months–years.
  • Thin rotable pool → long TAT, weak PBH pricing — cyclical.
Value pools
  • Wheels & brakes and landing gear — high-volume, established shops.
  • Avionics LRU repair — Thales/LHT capturing, India under 30%.
  • APU overhaul — a named gap (Air India sends HGT1700 to Hamburg).
  • PMA/DER parts — 20–40% / 30–40% cost lever.
Market & growth

  • India MRO 15–20% domestic today; import dependence >60% for components/airframes/engines [REPORTED-T2].

05 Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunity dual-readable: every risk is a substitution play

Opportunity

OEM data & tooling licencesHigh-end LRUs, FADEC modules and composite repairs stay outside India without formal OEM partnerships. Resilience: years.

Opportunity

PMA / DER route20–40% / 30–40% cheaper; contestable without OEM data.

Risk

Test equipment & capability gapComponent test benches/ATE and deep C-ratings are scarce. Resilience: months–years.

Opportunity

Shared test facilitiesCommon-facility centres and OEM service-centre partnerships.

Risk

Thin rotable poolLong TAT and weak PBH pricing vs global pools. Resilience: cyclical.

Opportunity

Exchange/loan poolsBuilding a domestic rotable pool cuts TAT and wins 3rd-party work.

Deepest hidden dependency: OEM repair data and test equipment — not hangar space. Component MRO is the most contestable node India has (55% spares intensity, PMA/DER routes, 166 approved MROs), yet it runs at ~100% overseas. That gap is the clearest localisation market in Indian aviation maintenance.
