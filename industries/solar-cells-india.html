Snapshot

The wafer-to-cell conversion step — texturing, diffusion, passivation, metallisation and test. India's cell capacity is ~36.6 GW (Jun-2026) but only 16–18 GW is effective; silicon wafers are >99% imported; capex is ~₹600 cr/GW; TOPCon is 33% of capacity.

Process & equipment
  • Texturing, POCl₃/BBr₃ diffusion, passivation (Al₂O₃/SiN), screen-printed metallisation and co-firing.
  • Turnkey cell lines are imported; metallisation is ~24% of conversion cost.
Bottlenecks
  • Silicon wafers — >99% imported.
  • Silver paste, fine-mesh screens and dopants / process gases.
  • Cell-line machinery >90% foreign; ALMM-II demand timing.
Value pools
  • Cell OEMs — Premier, Waaree, Websol, Emmvee, Tata Power (TP Solar), Reliance.
  • Silver-paste and screen makers are foreign/unlisted; recycling is emerging.
Watch items
  • ALMM-II in force 1-Jun-2026.
  • TOPCon share rising; Reliance's integrated entry.
Coverage gaps
  • Wafers and paste/screens have no listed Indian pure-play.
01System OverviewCell fabrication turns an imported wafer into a current-producing device through a ~15–20 step sequence of wet chemistry, high-temperature diffusion, deposition and metallisation. Gold-bordered nodes below are the HIGH-priority subsystems carried into the drill-down tree — the wafer input, the deposition and metallisation steps, the cell-line machinery and the silver paste. This is the node where India's import dependence has moved to, not disappeared.

05Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunitydual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

Solar Cell FabricationImported / supply-concentrated node — imported.

Opportunity

Solar Cell Fabrication — localisation playListed incumbents — Premier Energies, Waaree Energies, Websol Energy System, Emmvee Photovoltaic Power, Tata Power (TP Solar), Reliance Industries — can deepen local capability at this node.

Risk

Incoming wafer>99% of India silicon wafers are imported (China ~97% of global supply). Domestic wafer capacity is ~2 GW and is the binding input constraint — the reason ALMM-III (2028) targets this stage.

Opportunity

Incoming wafer — localisation playListed incumbents — Tata Power, Waaree Energies, Reliance Industries — can deepen local capability at this node.

Risk

Ingot & polysiliconNO COVERAGE IN SELECTED UNIVERSE as a pure-play. India has ~2 GW of ingot-wafer and no commercial polysilicon production. Nearest listed: Reliance, Tata Power, Waaree — integrated plans only.

Opportunity

Ingot & polysilicon — localisation playNo NSE/BSE pure-play; domestic qualification of this node is the substitution play. [DERIVED]

Risk

Metallisation paste (Ag / Al)NO COVERAGE IN SELECTED UNIVERSE. Silver and aluminium pastes are largely imported; metallisation is the single largest direct cost after the wafer (~24% of cell-conversion cost).

Opportunity

Metallisation paste (Ag / Al) — localisation playNo NSE/BSE pure-play; domestic qualification of this node is the substitution play. [DERIVED]

Risk

Silver powder & high-purity silverNO COVERAGE. High-purity silver bars (≥99.9%) were moved to the Restricted import category by DGFT from 16 May 2026; paste / silver-powder suppliers are foreign (DKEM, Fusion, Solamet, DuPont, Heraeus).

Opportunity

Silver powder & high-purity silver — localisation playNo NSE/BSE pure-play; domestic qualification of this node is the substitution play. [DERIVED]

Risk

Process gases (silane, NH₃, POCl₃, BBr₃)Specialty cell process gases are substantially imported. Linde India is a listed supplier of industrial / specialty gases but not a cell-gas pure-play.

Opportunity

Process gases (silane, NH₃, POCl₃, BBr₃) — localisation playListed incumbents — Linde India — can deepen local capability at this node.

Risk

Cell-line machineryNO COVERAGE. Turnkey cell lines come from Jinchen (SSE: 603396, China), Singulus (Germany, via the Jinchen JV), Linton (China, for Websol) and SVCS; >90% of cell-line capex is foreign.

Opportunity

Cell-line machinery — localisation playNo NSE/BSE pure-play; domestic qualification of this node is the substitution play. [DERIVED]

Risk

Diffusion / PECVD / ALD toolsNO COVERAGE. Deposition and diffusion tools are foreign; PVD-based TOPCon (e.g. VON ARDENNE) is a rising, tandem-upgradable alternative.

Opportunity

Diffusion / PECVD / ALD tools — localisation playNo NSE/BSE pure-play; domestic qualification of this node is the substitution play. [DERIVED]

Risk

Screen printers & firing furnacesNO COVERAGE. Metallisation tools (printers, dryers, fast-firing furnaces) are dominated by foreign OEMs.

Opportunity

Screen printers & firing furnaces — localisation playNo NSE/BSE pure-play; domestic qualification of this node is the substitution play. [DERIVED]

Where value sits: Cell OEMs — Premier, Waaree, Websol, Emmvee, Tata Power (TP Solar), Reliance. Silver-paste and screen makers are foreign/unlisted; recycling is emerging.
