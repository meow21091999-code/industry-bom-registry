DGDigest — at a glanceA short summary of the analysis. Structure and inputs are on the tree; process, value pools and watch items are summarised here.
Snapshot

The wafer-to-cell conversion step — texturing, diffusion, passivation, metallisation, firing and test. India has built 37.8 GW of ALMM-listed cell capacity (8-Oct-2026) but only ~16–18 GW is effective; silicon wafers are >99% imported; capex is ~₹470 cr/GW; TOPCon has displaced PERC as the default new line. Import dependence has moved up the chain, not disappeared.

Process & equipment
  • Texturing, POCl₃/BBr₃ diffusion, Al₂O₃/SiN passivation, screen-printed metallisation and co-firing — a ~15–20 step wet-chemistry + high-temperature sequence.
  • Turnkey cell lines are imported (>90% of capex), mostly Chinese OEMs; metallisation is the largest direct cost after the wafer (~24% of conversion cost).
Bottlenecks
  • Silicon wafers — >99% imported; India wafer capacity ~2 GW.
  • Silver paste and high-purity silver — ~90% imported; silver-bar imports restricted since 16-May-2026.
  • Cell-line machinery — >90% foreign; process gases and fine-mesh screens largely imported.
Value pools
  • Cell OEMs are the largest listed pool — Premier, Waaree, Websol, Emmvee, Tata Power (TP Solar), Reliance.
  • Premier is India’s largest cell maker (10.6 GW after the 7 GW Naidupeta line, Sep-2026); Waaree holds 5.4 GW.
  • Wafers/polysilicon and paste/screens have no listed Indian pure-play — the localisation prize.
Watch items
  • ALMM List-II in force 1-Jun-2026; net-metering/open-access exemption ends 31-Dec-2026.
  • ALMM-III (ingots/wafers) targeted 1-Jun-2028; US AD ~123% / CVD ~126% on Indian cells.
Coverage gaps
  • Wafers, ingot/polysilicon, silver paste, screens and cell-line equipment have no listed Indian pure-play; nearest exposure is via integrated OEMs.

05Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunitydual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

Incoming wafer>99% of India’s silicon wafers are imported (China ~97% of global output); domestic wafer capacity is ~2 GW — the binding input constraint. Resilience: years / structural.

Opportunity

Wafer localisation — ALMM-III (1-Jun-2028)Waaree’s 10 GW ingot-wafer complex at Nagpur (₹6,200 cr) and Tata Power’s 10 GW plan (₹6,500 cr) lead a ~100 GW announced pipeline; the mandate triggers once ≥3 units and ≥15 GW are enlisted.

Risk

Metallisation paste (Ag / Al)Silver paste is ~90% import-dependent (China ~90–92% of global production); India has near-zero domestic paste. Silver-bar imports (≥99.9%, HS 71069221/29) moved to “Restricted” on 16-May-2026 and now need DGFT authorisation. Resilience: months–years.

Opportunity

Paste & silver thriftingSilver-paste imports hit US$184m in FY26 (+64.5% YoY, ~65% from China); a domestic paste/refined-silver play is the open white space, and copper-metallisation is the substitution race (see the Silver Paste drill-down).

Risk

Cell-line machinery>90% of cell-line capex is foreign — turnkey lines and process tools (diffusion, PECVD/LPCVD, ALD, printers, firing furnaces) come mainly from Chinese OEMs, now under export pressure. Resilience: years.

Opportunity

Domestic equipment & consumablesAn unlisted white space — wet-bench, PECVD, printers, screens, ribbon. RENA/centrotherm (Germany) and China S.C serve India today; no NSE/BSE pure-play exists.

Risk

Process gases & wet chemicalsCell-grade silane, ammonia, POCl₃/BBr₃ and high-purity wet chemicals (KOH, HF, HNO₃) are substantially imported; supply is concentrated and qualification is line-specific. Resilience: months.

Opportunity

Industrial-gas localisationLinde India is the only listed gas exposure; local bulk-gas and on-site nitrogen plants (e.g. Praxair/Linde) are the substitution route.

Risk

ALMM-II demand timingList-II is live (1-Jun-2026) at 37.8 GW enlisted but only ~16–18 GW effective; module lines have been under-fed, and the 31-Dec-2026 net-metering/open-access exemption creates a demand pull-forward cliff. Resilience: months–years.

Opportunity

Effective-capacity rampPremier’s 7 GW Naidupeta TOPCon line (Sep-2026) and Waaree’s 10 GW Unn plant are converting enlisted into operating capacity — the value pool that stays with the domestic builder.

Risk

Trade exposureUS preliminary AD ~123% / CVD ~126% on Indian cells (final order 26-Oct-2026) squeezes exports, while China’s WTO challenge (Dec-2025) contests India’s tariff/PLI stack. Resilience: structural.

Opportunity

Domestic-demand anchoringALMM-II plus 44 GW of annual domestic demand makes the home market the anchor; EU FTA (concluded 27-Jan-2026) is the diversification route.

Where value sits: Cell OEMs — Premier, Waaree, Websol, Emmvee, Tata Power (TP Solar), Reliance. Silver-paste and screen makers are foreign/unlisted; recycling is emerging.
06 Value pools & cost waterfall where value is captured · and which pool leaks to imports
Where value is captured
  • Cell conversion is the largest listed value pool — Premier, Waaree, Websol, Emmvee, Tata Power (TP Solar), Reliance.
  • The wafer dominates cell cost but is imported, so its value leaks upstream to China.
  • Silver paste, screens and cell-line equipment are foreign/unlisted — value leaks.
  • Recycling and silver recovery are emerging, largely informal.
Who captures it (India-listed)
  • Premier Energies NSE: PREMIERENE — India’s largest cell maker: 10.6 GW after the 7 GW TOPCon Naidupeta line (Sep-2026); targeting ~25.8% efficiency
  • Waaree Energies NSE: WAAREEENER — 5.4 GW cell, scaling to 15.4 GW; 10 GW cell plant at Unn, Gujarat; 10 GW ingot-wafer at Nagpur
  • Websol Energy System NSE: WEBELSOLAR — 1.2 GW → 1.35 GW; brownfield PERC→TOPCon upgrade at Falta SEZ, West Bengal
  • Emmvee Photovoltaic Power NSE: EMMVEE — 2,153 MW TOPCon cell on ALMM List-II; 1.4 GW TOPCon line ramped with German equipment
  • Tata Power (TP Solar) NSE: TATAPOWER — 4,480 MW cell at Tirunelveli (ALMM-II); small share of consolidated revenue [INFERENCE]
  • Reliance Industries NSE: RELIANCE — 1,238 MW HJT cell at Jamnagar (ALMM-II); New Energy segment, minor to group [INFERENCE]
Pools that leak to imports
  • Wafers, polysilicon, silver paste, screens and cell-line equipment have no listed Indian pure-play.
  • Bottleneck nodes: Incoming wafer, Ingot & polysilicon, Metallisation paste (Ag / Al), Silver powder & high-purity silver, Process gases, Cell-line machinery, Diffusion / PECVD / ALD tools, Screen printers & firing furnaces.
