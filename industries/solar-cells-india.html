Snapshot

The wafer-to-cell conversion step — texturing, diffusion, passivation, metallisation and test. India's cell capacity is ~36.6 GW (Jun-2026) but only 16–18 GW is effective; silicon wafers are >99% imported; capex is ~₹600 cr/GW; TOPCon is 33% of capacity.

Process & equipment
  • Texturing, POCl₃/BBr₃ diffusion, passivation (Al₂O₃/SiN), screen-printed metallisation and co-firing.
  • Turnkey cell lines are imported; metallisation is ~24% of conversion cost.
Bottlenecks
  • Silicon wafers — >99% imported.
  • Silver paste, fine-mesh screens and dopants / process gases.
  • Cell-line machinery >90% foreign; ALMM-II demand timing.
Value pools
  • Cell OEMs — Premier, Waaree, Websol, Emmvee, Tata Power (TP Solar), Reliance.
  • Silver-paste and screen makers are foreign/unlisted; recycling is emerging.
Watch items
  • ALMM-II in force 1-Jun-2026.
  • TOPCon share rising; Reliance's integrated entry.
Coverage gaps
  • Wafers and paste/screens have no listed Indian pure-play.
01System overview

Cell fabrication turns an imported wafer into a current-producing device through a ~15–20 step sequence of wet chemistry, high-temperature diffusion, deposition and metallisation. Gold-bordered nodes below are the HIGH-priority subsystems carried into the drill-down tree — the wafer input, the deposition and metallisation steps, the cell-line machinery and the silver paste. This is the node where India's import dependence has moved to, not disappeared.

02Value-chain ladder

Raw material → aftermarket, with the Tier-4 → OEM supplier bands. The value inversion is sharp: the wafer dominates cell cost and is imported, while the highest domestic value-add sits at the cell-conversion step itself — which is exactly what ALMM List-II has tried to localise.

Tier 4 · Raw
Polysilicon · silicon metal · quartz · silver · aluminium · copper China ~93% polysilicon · ~97% wafers
Tier 3 · Specialty
Silicon wafers · silver / aluminium paste · fine-mesh screens · POCl₃ / BBr₃ dopants · silane & ammonia · high-purity silver powder
Tier 2 · Component
The solar cell — wafer-to-cell conversion (texture → diffusion → passivation → metallisation → test)
Tier 1 · Cell OEMs
Premier · Waaree · Websol · Emmvee · Tata Power (TP Solar) · Reliance · Avaada (unlisted) · ReNew (Nasdaq)
OEM / Downstream
Module makers and IPP / developers — the demand layer that ALMM-II channels toward domestic cells ALMM-II in force 1-Jun-2026
Aftermarket
Repowering · module / cell recycling · silver recovery emerging, largely informal
