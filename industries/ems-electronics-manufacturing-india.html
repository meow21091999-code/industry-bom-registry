DGDigest — at a glanceshort summary; full detail below
Snapshot
  • India's EMS sector is ~USD 40–45 bn (FY25), projected to exceed USD 150 bn by FY30 (KPMG, Jun 2026).
  • It exports finished electronics (~USD 38.6 bn FY25) but imports components (~USD 36.8 bn) — scale without depth.
Process & equipment
  • SMT line: solder-paste printer → SPI → pick-and-place → multi-zone reflow; AOI and X-ray on hidden joints; ICT/FCT.
  • Box-build adds enclosures, harness, sub-assembly and final assembly; high-reliability builds add burn-in.
  • Traceability via per-board serial and MES; automotive lines run IATF 16949.
Bottlenecks
  • Imported components (ICs, passives, sub-assemblies) — ICs alone ~USD 23.8 bn of imports. Resilience: structural.
  • Bare PCB (multi-layer/HDI) — ~90% imported; CCL is the upstream gap. Resilience: structural.
  • Display & camera modules — largely imported; first ECMS plants cover ~15–20% of demand.
  • Low domestic value addition — mobile DVA ~23% vs a 35–40% target.
Value pools
  • Mobile & IT-hardware assembly is the largest pool, at thin margin (~3.8%).
  • High-mix EMS (industrial/auto/defence/med-tech) carries higher margin (Kaynes ~15%, Avalon ~36% gross).
  • Component / backward integration is the next margin pool (display, camera, PCB, battery).
  • Consumer-durables ODM (AC, appliances) is a large domestic pool.
Watch items
  • ECMS outlay raised to ₹40,000 cr; commitments ₹1.15 lakh cr as of Dec 2025.
  • Customer concentration: Dixon top-3 = 60% of FY25 revenue.
  • US tariffs and China export controls add supply-chain risk.
  • Chinese rivals underbidding export tenders compressed EMS gross margins ~150–200 bps since 2024.
Coverage gaps
  • Large EMS capacity is unlisted/foreign (Foxconn, Tata Electronics, Pegatron, Wistron, Zetwerk).
  • Battery-pack, charger, connector and enclosure pure-plays are largely unlisted or captive.
  • Segment-level margins for enclosures, connectors and chargers are not separately disclosed — [UNAVAILABLE].

05 Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunity dual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

Imported components (ICs, passives)Electronic component imports ~USD 36.8 bn in FY25, ~40% from China; ICs alone ~USD 23.8 bn. Resilience: structural.

Opportunity

ECMS + ISM + PLI roundsNew JVs with Korea/Taiwan/Japan suppliers aim to localise components and sub-assemblies.

Risk

Bare PCB (multi-layer / HDI)India imports ~90% of PCB consumption; CCL laminate is overwhelmingly from China. Resilience: structural.

Opportunity

ECMS PCB projects30% anti-dumping duty on ≤6-layer PCBs lifted utilisation; Amber, Kaynes and Syrma are building PCB/HDI capacity.

Risk

Display & camera modulesPanels/touchscreens ~4.4% of the import basket; camera and display modules largely imported.

Opportunity

Module localisationDixon-HKC display JV; Kaynes camera-module sub-assembly; first ECMS plants to cover ~15–20% of demand.

Risk

Low domestic value additionMobile manufacturing DVA rose from ~15% to ~23% against a 35–40% target.

Opportunity

Backward integrationDixon, Kaynes, Syrma and Amber investing in components, PCBs and modules.

Deepest hidden import dependency: the component and substrate layer beneath the assembly — integrated circuits (~USD 23.8 bn imported) and bare PCBs (~90% imported). India assembles the box; it imports what goes inside it.
06 Value pools & cost waterfall where value is captured · and which pool leaks to imports
Where value is captured
  • Mobile & IT-hardware assembly — largest pool, thin margin (~3.8%).
  • High-mix EMS (industrial/auto/defence/med-tech) — higher margin.
  • Component / backward integration — the next margin pool.
  • Consumer-durables ODM (AC, appliances) — large domestic pool.
Who captures it (India-listed)
  • Dixon Technologies NSE:DIXON — FY25 revenue ₹38,880 cr
  • Syrma SGS NSE:SYRMA — FY25 revenue ₹3,836 cr

  • ICs and passives — ~USD 23.8 bn IC imports.
  • Bare PCB and CCL laminate — ~90% imported.
  • Display and camera modules — largely imported.
