Snapshot

Optical fibre cable — cabling fibre into land/duct, FTTH, ADSS and OPGW constructions. India is a large maker and a net exporter; the binding constraint is the upstream glass preform. Scope: India, NSE/BSE. Re-analysed 2026-10-23 (delta pass over the 2026-10-08 entry).

Process & equipment
  • Fibre is buffered, stranded around FRP/aramid strength members with water-blocking yarn, sheathed in PE/PVC/LSZH and armoured; ribbon (IBR) and micro-duct constructions for data centres.
  • Cabling / SZ-stranding lines and OTDR / PMD / CD test gear are the key equipment — all imported, with no Indian pure-play.
Bottlenecks
  • Preform — ~65–75% imported; resilience: years, shortening as STL/HFCL/Finolex build.
  • Aramid yarn / strength members — no Indian maker (Teijin, DuPont); FRP is domestic.
  • Fibre price spike — 2.8–5× 2025; global supply deficit ~180 mn fkm in 2026, to persist to ~2028.
  • BharatNet Phase III fixed-price squeeze — STL/Polycab invoked force majeure as cable prices doubled.
  • Cabling machinery & test gear imported (Europe / USA / Japan); Chinese overcapacity & dumping pressure.
Value pools
  • Cable assembly is competitive and thin-margin; value sits in preform/fibre above it.
  • India's export-hub role adds value — exports +158% YoY to $483 mn in Apr–Aug FY27.
  • HFCL and STL are moving up into high-count IBR / data-centre interconnect; Vindhya is doubling down on speciality cable.
  • Localisation: FRP/aramid strength members, cabling machinery, preform.
Market & growth
  • India OFC market (scope: optical fibre cable, India): ~$550 mn (2025) → >$1 bn (2031), CAGR ~11% [REPORTED-T3, tele.net, Aug-2026]. Value (nominal); a sibling scope (cable + accessories) reads ~$620 mn (2025) → ~$1.76 bn (2035) at ~11% [REPORTED-T3, Expert Market Research, Sep-2026].
  • Last-year: India optical-fibre exports +158% YoY to $483 mn in Apr–Aug FY27 (US >half at $266.7 mn; China $52.4 mn) [REPORTED-T1, Ministry of Commerce, Oct-2026]. India fibre/OFC capacity ~100 mn fkm/yr vs ~46 mn consumed.
  • Growth is demand-led (AI data centres, FTTH, 5G backhaul, BharatNet Phase III); data centres ~18–20% of global fibre demand in 2026, rising to 30–35% in 2027. Price/mix is strongly positive — data-centre fibre runs above telco fibre.
  • [CONFLICTING DATA] on other scopes: India OFC $1.73 bn 2024→$7.50 bn 2032 (20.1%, Maximize); India optic-fibre-cable & accessories $4.8 bn 2026→$12.99 bn 2035 (11.7%, MarkWide); India fiber-optics $517 mn 2025→$1,196 mn 2034 (9.8%, IMARC). Scopes differ; do not reconcile.

05 Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunity dual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

Preform & bare fibre~65–75% of preform is imported (China, Japan); domestic capacity meets only ~25–35% of demand, and preform captures ~60–70% of the value-chain profit pool. The expansion cycle is 18–24 months. [REPORTED]

Opportunity

Domestic preform integrationSTL is fully integrated (₹3,000 cr capex FY27-29); Finolex has commissioned captive preform (100→200 MT); HFCL is building 600 MT/yr by Oct-2028 (₹1,250 cr; total planned ~₹1,800 cr). [REPORTED]

Risk

Strength members & FRP/aramidAramid yarn has no Indian maker (Teijin/DuPont); FRP capacity is concentrated and was named among cost-pressure inputs in BharatNet Phase-III. HDPE rose ₹90→₹175/kg; jelly ₹135→₹250. [REPORTED]

Opportunity

Backward integrationAksh runs the world’s largest single FRP-rod facility (~2 mn km/yr); HFCL (Hosur, via HTL) and Polycab (Halol) make FRP/ARP rods and IGFR yarns in-house. [REPORTED]

Risk

Fibre price spike & BharatNet fixed-price squeezeSpot fibre is 2.8–5× 2025 levels; the CRUofpi index ran 78.6 (Mar-2024) → 263.0 (Mar-2026), the sharpest rise since 2017. Under fixed-price 10-year BharatNet contracts, STL and Polycab invoked force majeure as cable prices nearly doubled. [REPORTED]

Opportunity

Multi-year volume certainty₹65,000 cr programme across 16 packages; HFCL won ₹2,666 cr from RVNL (UP-West, Oct-2026), on top of ₹8,100 cr of BharatNet-3 wins; Polycab ₹6,447 cr, STL+Dilip Buildcon ₹2,631 cr, ITI ₹6,900 cr. [REPORTED]

Risk

Chinese overcapacity & dumpingChina’s fibre capacity (~500 mn km) dwarfs its demand and export pricing creates structural pressure; sub-standard fibre has entered as raw material. India’s ADD on SMOF covers only finished fibre, not preform. [REPORTED]

Opportunity

Tariff & localisation tailwindIndia’s ADD on SMOF (CN/KR/ID) runs to Aug-2028; domestic-preference in BharatNet/5G procurement and a proposed “Trusted Fibre” policy favour Indian makers. [REPORTED]

Risk

Machinery & test equipmentCabling / SZ-stranding lines and OTDR / PMD / CD test gear are imported (Austria, USA, Belgium, Switzerland, Japan) with no Indian pure-play. [INFERENCE]

Opportunity

Capacity-expansion pullThe ₹1,800 cr+ industry capex wave (HFCL, STL, Finolex, VTL) is a direct demand signal for upstream machinery localisation; SIMCO and Machine & Controls build some lines in India. [INFERENCE]

Risk

AI data-centre demand skewData centres are ~18–20% of world fibre demand in 2026, heading to 30–35% in 2027; domestic telco capex stays lumpy and ARPU-sensitive. [REPORTED]

Opportunity

High-fibre-count premiumIBR / ribbon cables command premium margins — HFCL’s 3456-fibre micro-duct IBR (capacity 1.73 → 19.01 mn fkm) and STL’s 864-fibre ribbon target this segment; HFCL’s OFC+DCI order book is ₹18,500 cr, ~70% hyperscalers. [REPORTED]

Deepest hidden import dependency: the glass preform — the semiconductor-grade glass rod from which every fibre is drawn — plus the germanium tetrachloride (GeCl₄) and helium that go into it. Even with HFCL and Finolex building preform lines, India remains a net importer of preform, and it is the one input whose expansion cannot be dialled up to meet a demand spike.

  • Vindhya Telelinks NSE/BSE: VINDHYATEL — MP Birla group; FY26 revenue ₹3,593 cr; order book ~₹5,226 cr (Mar-2026); speciality OFC 0.5→3.0 mn FKM/yr by Mar-2027; absorbing Birla Cable [REPORTED]
