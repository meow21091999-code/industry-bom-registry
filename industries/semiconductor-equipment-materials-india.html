DGDigest — at a glanceshort summary; full detail below
Snapshot
  • The upstream layer of the chip industry: the tools that pattern a wafer and the materials it consumes — not the chips themselves.
  • India's position is near-zero. Every process tool, wafer, photoresist, mask and most gases and wet chemicals are imported; Semicon 2.0 creates a dedicated pillar to change that.
Process & equipment
  • Front-end spine: lithography → deposition → etch → implant → diffusion → CMP → clean → metrology, repeated 400–1,400 times per wafer.
  • Sub-fab spine: vacuum, abatement, gas cabinets, chillers and ultrapure water — unglamorous and completely imported.
  • Back-end spine: dicing, die attach, wire bond, moulding, singulation, ATE — the tools India's new OSATs have all bought abroad.
  • Materials are consumables, not capex: they recur every wafer, which is why qualification, not capital, is the barrier.
Bottlenecks
  • Lithography — ASML sole EUV supplier; DUV export-controlled. Resilience: STRUCTURAL.
  • Photoresist, photomask blanks and 300mm silicon wafers — three-to-five supplier oligopolies, 2–3 year requalification. Resilience: YEARS.
  • Specialty gases and high-purity wet chemicals — 12–22 of 50+ gases made locally; Fujifilm's first plant lands FY2028. Resilience: MONTHS–YEARS.
  • Packaging materials — leadframe, bonding wire, moulding compound, substrate: no qualified Indian producer. Resilience: MONTHS–YEARS.
  • Skills — orbital welders for 5N/6N gas lines: 200–300 needed per project, trained from scratch. Resilience: YEARS.
Value pools
  • Value sits with the tool OEM and with proprietary consumables that recur per wafer — slurry, resist, quartzware, targets, chamber parts.
  • India's current capture is the commoditised slice: civils, cleanroom fit-out, cryogenic tanks, gas supply, installation labour.
  • The realistic entry is tool contract manufacturing and precision sub-assemblies — ASM Technologies' model, now funded by an ECMS approval.
  • Localisation alone does not cut cost: NITI Aayog found Indian arms of global suppliers price at import parity, so the saving arrives only at genuine Indian scale.
  • The largest single material line item is the leadframe at ~35–40% of OSAT material cost — the highest-value localisation target in the back end.

Watch items

  • 27 Nov 2026 — China's US-specific gallium / germanium / antimony suspension expires.

05 Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunity dual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

LithographyASML is the sole EUV supplier and the DUV leader; tools reach India only through export licensing. No domestic path. Resilience: STRUCTURAL.

Opportunity

None at tool levelIndia can only build the support layer — optics cleaning, stages, metrology — not the scanner.

Risk

Photoresist & ancillariesJSR, TOK, Shin-Etsu, Sumitomo and Fujifilm hold the qualified supply; requalification runs 2–3 years per layer. Resilience: YEARS.

Opportunity

Local formulation with a technology partnerSumitomo's Tata MoU and Fujifilm's Dholera plant are the only live routes; both start with imported raw materials.

Risk

Photomask blanks & 300mm wafersHoya/AGC/Shin-Etsu make the blanks; five suppliers make most 300mm wafers. Neither exists in India at any scale. Resilience: STRUCTURAL.

Opportunity

Nothing before 2030Both need crystal growth or ultra-precision polishing capability India does not have; a JV with technology transfer is the only route.

Risk

Specialty gases12–22 of 50+ gases are made locally; 20–25 will still be imported after INOX Air Products' expansion. Resilience: MONTHS–YEARS.

Opportunity

Gas purification and the Dholera hubINOX Air Products' ~₹500 cr specialty-gas hub plus Linde's Dholera plant are the build; purification capacity, not gas molecules, is the gap.

Risk

High-purity wet chemicalsNo qualified Indian producer; Fujifilm's first plant targets FY2028, and NITI Aayog found local arms of global suppliers price at import parity. Resilience: MONTHS.

Opportunity

Genuinely Indian chemical manufactureIndia's specialty-chemical base can supply the plant; the barrier is sub-ppb purification and fab qualification, not chemistry.

Risk

Packaging materialsLeadframe ~95% imported at ~35–40% of material cost; bonding wire 100%; moulding compound ~90%; organic substrate 100%. Resilience: MONTHS–YEARS.

Opportunity

Leadframe stamping & platingThe highest-value localisation target in the back end; copper strip and precision stamping already exist in India, qualification does not.

Risk

Qualified skillsA single project needs 200–300 orbital welders for 5N/6N gas lines; material qualification takes 9–18 months and a new vendor plant three years. Resilience: YEARS.

Opportunity

Training plus bonded warehousesINOX India's skill centre is the template; a bonded stocking hub turns a 75–90 day import exposure into 7–14 days without needing the factory.

06 Value pools & cost waterfall where value is captured · and which pool leaks to imports
Where value is captured
  • Tool OEM margin — the largest pool in the chain, protected by patents and process know-how rather than materials.
  • Recurring consumables — resist, slurry, quartzware, targets and chamber parts are billed per wafer, so they compound with fab utilisation.
  • Qualification itself — once a material is designed into a process, it is rarely displaced; that is the moat, not the chemistry.
What India captures today
  • Civils, cleanroom fit-out, cryogenic tanks, gas supply, UPW and installation labour — the low-margin, commoditised end.
  • Tool contract manufacturing is the one higher-value foothold, and it is small: ASM Technologies' whole FY26 revenue is ₹528.5 cr.
  • Material value leaks almost entirely: material is ~45–50% of OSAT cost and 60–70% of that is imported.
The localisation trap
  • NITI Aayog (May 2026) found Indian arms of global suppliers price at import parity, tariffs included — so a "made in India" component can cost what the import costs.
  • Cost competitiveness arrives only when manufacture is genuinely Indian and at scale, which is why the leadframe (the biggest single line item) matters more than headline fab announcements.
