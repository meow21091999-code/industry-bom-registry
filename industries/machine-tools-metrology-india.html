Snapshot

Machine tools are the ‘mother industry’ — the machines that make everything else. India produces ~₹17,000 cr of them but consumes ~₹36,000 cr, importing the difference (~60%). The precision tiers that matter for robotics — gear grinding and metrology — are the most import-dependent of all.

Process & equipment
  • Machines: CNC lathes, VMCs, five-axis machining centres, gear hobbing/grinding, EDMs, grinding machines.
  • Sub-systems: CNC controllers, spindles, precision bearings, linear guides, ball screws, encoders.
  • Metrology: CMMs, gear testers, surface/roundness testers, laser interferometers.
Bottlenecks
  • CNC controllers (Fanuc/Siemens/Mitsubishi) — proprietary, imported.
  • Gear/thread grinding machines — ultra-precision fully imported.
  • CMMs/metrology — ZEISS/Mitutoyo dominated.
  • Spindles and precision bearings — imported/high-grade.
Value pools
  • CNC machines: Jyoti CNC and other domestic builders.
  • Cutting tools & abrasives: Kennametal India, Carborundum Universal, Grindwell Norton.
  • Bearings: Schaeffler, SKF, Timken; cages: Harsha Engineers.
  • The controller/spindle/metrology pools leak to imports.
Market & growth
  • India machine-tool consumption was ₹36,419 cr in FY26, up ~15% on ₹31,781 cr in FY25, while domestic production rose ~19% to ₹17,387 cr and imports 16% to ₹21,628 cr — imports are ~59% of consumption [REPORTED-T2: IMTMA revised estimates, 2026]. Scope: metal-cutting and metal-forming machine tools only; metrology (CMMs, gear testers) sits outside the IMTMA series.
  • Longer run: production ₹4,230 cr in FY15 → ₹17,387 cr in FY26, a ~13.7% CAGR [REPORTED-T2: IMTMA Director General, 2026; CALCULATED for the CAGR]. An earlier IMTMA estimate put FY26 production at ₹16,478 cr (+13%); the revised figure is used here [CONFLICTING DATA].
  • Growth is value-led, not volume-led — the mix is shifting toward 5-axis, grinding and automation; Q1 FY27 production was +26% and consumption +20% YoY [REPORTED-T2: IMTMA Q1 FY27 release]. India ranks 9th in global production and 4th in consumption [REPORTED-T2: Gardner / CECIMO survey].
  • Drivers: roughly ₹21,600 cr of import substitution sits inside FY26 consumption; 29 capital-goods projects sanctioned to Mar-2026; defence, semiconductor, EV and railway capex pulling advanced CNC and gear grinding.
Watch items
  • Capital-goods scheme (29 projects) and import-substitution push.
  • Jan-2026 rescission of the 2024 machinery regulation.
  • Defence and semiconductor demand pulling advanced CNC.
Coverage gaps

CNC controllers, gear-grinding machines, CMMs/metrology and spindles have no listed Indian maker at robotics grade. Listed proxies: Jyoti CNC (machines), Kennametal/Carborundum/Grindwell (tools/abrasives), Schaeffler/SKF/Timken (bearings).

05 Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunity dual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

CNC controllersFanuc/Siemens/Mitsubishi proprietary controllers sit atop most machines; imported. Resilience: years / structural.

Opportunity

Controller developmentThe deepest software-hardware gap; a long-horizon national capability.

Risk

Gear & thread grinding machinesUltra-precision gear grinding is Germany/Japan-dominated (Klingelnberg, Gleason, Nidec, Höfler); these make the reducers. Resilience: years / structural.

Opportunity

Machine-tool build-outWithout domestic gear grinding, no reducer plan can qualify.

Risk

Metrology (CMMs)ZEISS/Mitutoyo dominate; without metrology no gear can be certified. Resilience: years.

Opportunity

Metrology capabilityA qualification-gated niche; CMTI and capital-goods projects target it.

Risk

Spindles & precision bearingsHigh-speed spindles and precision bearings are imported/high-grade. Resilience: years.

Opportunity

Component localisationBearings partly domestic (Schaeffler, SKF, Timken); spindles are the gap.

Risk

Import share ~60%Imports are ~59–65% of consumption and exceed domestic production. Resilience: years.

Opportunity

Import substitution₹20,000+ cr import pool; 29 capital-goods projects sanctioned.

Deepest hidden dependency: the CNC controller and the ultra-precision gear grinder. Every ‘localised’ robot reducer, gear and screw ultimately rests on imported machines, controllers and metrology — the true root of India's robotics import dependence.
