Snapshot
  • The instruments that give a satellite its function: comms, TT&C/transponders, EO and navigation payloads.
  • Payload electronics and RF are the deepest import dependency after solar cells and ADCS.
Process & equipment
  • RF/microwave fabrication and screening (ISRO-certified), MMIC die handling, wire bonding.
  • Optical fabrication and precision diamond turning for EO optics.
Bottlenecks
  • TWTA / transponder amplifiers — historically imported (years).
  • Atomic clocks — 5 of 11 NavIC satellites failed (structural).
  • High-grade optics & detectors — not domestically available (years).
Value pools
  • Payload build and screening captured domestically (Astra Microwave, Centum, Data Patterns, BEL, Paras).
  • Highest-value instruments (atomic clocks, high-grade optics) still leak abroad.
Watch items
  • Indigenous TWTA and clock qualification.
  • EO-PPP (Pixxel-led, ~₹1,200 cr) payload localisation.
Coverage gaps
  • No NSE/BSE pure-play for navigation payloads / atomic clocks.

05Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunitydual-readable
Risk

TWTA / transponder amplifiersHistorically imported from a few global suppliers — a supply-chain vulnerability. Resilience: years.

Opportunity

Indigenous TWTATDS-01 validated an indigenous TWTA — a firewall against technology-denial regimes.

Risk

Atomic clocks (NavIC)Imported clocks failed on 5 of 11 satellites. Resilience: structural.

Opportunity

Indigenous rubidium clocksDemonstrated on TDS-01; qualifying a supplier de-risks NavIC.

Risk

Optics & detectorsHigh-grade optics and detectors of certain specs not available domestically. Resilience: years.

Opportunity

Precision optics manufacturingIndian firms (e.g. Eon) have replicated hand-polished telescope optics with diamond turning; a domestic qualification path.
