Snapshot

Telecom towers & passive infrastructure — macro towers, IBS and site power that host operator radios; an annuity-like domestic business. Scope: India, NSE/BSE.

Process & equipment
  • Towers are fabricated (lattice/monopole) and erected on site with foundations.
  • Site power (SMPS, batteries, DG, solar) is the largest operating cost; remote monitoring/IoT manage energy.
Bottlenecks
  • Steel and monopole fabrication.
  • Energy — the biggest operating cost (diesel/grid).
  • Tenancy-ratio decline on consolidation.
  • Battery chemistry transition (VRLA → lithium; imported cells); Right-of-Way and permitting.
Value pools
  • Domestic and annuity-like — value stays home (Indus, Bharti).
  • Energy management (ESCo) is a recurring market.
  • Battery replacement is a durable cycle.
  • Consolidation gives pricing power.
Watch items
  • Right-of-Way Rules 2024.
  • 5G loading and VI rollout.
  • Lithium-cell localisation.
  • Tower consolidation / M&A.
Coverage gaps

No listed pure-play: tower fabrication, SMPS, DG, cooling, IBS equipment — nearest: Indus/Bharti Hexacom (tower cos), Exide/Amara Raja (batteries).

05 Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunity dual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

Steel & monopole fabricationSteel price volatility and a fragmented fabrication base feed directly into per-tower capex (~₹12–15 lakh) and schedule. [REPORTED]

Opportunity

Domestic structure capacitySalasar Techno is scaling telecom towers and monopoles (211,000 MTPA) with lighter Ramboll-designed towers (~20% more efficient). [REPORTED]

Risk

Energy is the biggest operating costDiesel dependence and an unreliable grid keep power and fuel a large share of per-tenant revenue (Indus power & fuel ~₹23.7k/tenant/month in 4QFY25). [REPORTED]

Opportunity

Green energy & the ESCo modelIndus added 15,000+ solar sites in FY25 (base ~30,000) and cut diesel ~6% YoY; ESCo shifts energy capex to opex. [REPORTED]

Risk

Tenancy-ratio declineConsolidation cut the industry tenancy ratio to 1.63 (from 1.78 in FY23) and net tower adds fell to ~25,000 in FY25, the lowest since FY18. [REPORTED]

Opportunity

5G loading & densificationLoading tenancies, Vodafone Idea 4G/5G rollout and IBS / small cells re-open growth even as macro adds slow. [INFERENCE]

Risk

Battery chemistry transitionThe VRLA-to-lithium pivot exposes a dependency on imported lithium cells, with China material restrictions a live risk. [REPORTED]

Opportunity

Domestic lithium cellsAmara Raja crossed 1 GWh of lithium telecom deployment and is building a Telangana cell plant; Exide is scaling Li-ion — localising the cell, not just the pack. [REPORTED]

Risk

Right-of-Way & permittingSite acquisition and historically state-variable RoW charges and approval timelines delayed rollout. [REPORTED]

Opportunity

RoW Rules 2024Uniform charges (₹10,000/tower), deemed approval in 67 days and small cells on street furniture, in force from 01-Jan-2025, should accelerate deployment. [REPORTED]

Deepest hidden import dependency: the lithium cell and the power-semiconductor (not the pack, not the rectifier) — plus diesel fuel logistics. India assembles batteries and DC power plants domestically, but the strategic layers (LFP cells, rectifier/IGBT silicon) remain import-led.
