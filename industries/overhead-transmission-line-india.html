Snapshot

The physical transmission line itself — conductors, towers, insulators, hardware and OPGW, foundations, right-of-way and erection. The NEP 2022–32 outlay is ₹9.16 L cr; the ≥220 kV network is 4,96,372 ckm (Aug-2025), against a 42% FY25 line-add shortfall.

Process & equipment
  • Stranded conductor drawing, tower fabrication and galvanising, insulator firing, hardware fittings and OPGW, then stringing and erection EPC.
  • Conductor-grade rod and some tower inputs are import-linked.
Bottlenecks
  • Aluminium / EC-grade wire-rod supply and tower fabrication capacity.
  • Insulator and OPGW imports; right-of-way and erection labour.
  • FY25 line-add fell 42% short of target.
Value pools
  • Line-materials integrators and turnkey EPC — KEC, KPIL, L&T, Transrail.
  • Asset owners — PGCIL, Adani Energy Solutions, IndiGrid — earn a 35-yr TSA / TBCB annuity.
  • Aftermarket reconductoring (APAR).
Watch items
  • ISTS capex of ~₹1 L cr for FY26–27.
  • FY25 line-add shortfall of 42%.
  • TBCB pipeline.
Coverage gaps
  • Insulators and OPGW fibre are import-linked; no listed pure-play insulator maker.
