Snapshot

India’s robotics ecosystem spans four families — industrial robots, AMR/AGV, drones/UAV and humanoid & service robots — over a shared core of actuators, drives, sensors, power and software. India is the world’s 6th-largest robot installer (9,120 units, 2024) with a stock of ~52,600, but imports the core: reducers, magnets, encoders and power semiconductors. No dedicated robotics PLI exists; the National Strategy on Robotics is still a draft.

Process & equipment
  • Robot build = cast/forge/machine housings → wind stators → assemble actuator (motor + reducer + encoder + brake) → fit controller → integrate cell (fixtures, EOAT, vision) → EOL test.
  • Core process equipment is imported: 5-axis CNC, gear/thread grinding (Klingelnberg, Gleason, Nidec, Höfler), vacuum/atmosphere heat-treat furnaces, magnet presses and CMMs (ZEISS, Mitutoyo).
  • Drone and cobot assembly is lighter — PCB SMT, composite lay-up and DGCA/ISO test rigs.
Bottlenecks
  • Reducers (harmonic/RV) — effectively 100% imported; no robotics-grade domestic maker.
  • Rare-earth NdFeB magnets — ~95% imported; first Indian plant targeted Q1 2028.
  • Power electronics (IGBT/MOSFET/MCU) and encoders/torque sensors — no Indian capability.
  • Precision machine tools & metrology — fully imported; they make the reducers.
Value pools
  • Value sits upstream in reducers, motors, sensors, magnets and control silicon — the pools India imports.
  • Indian assembly/integration is competitive and thin-margin; ARAPL is the only listed robotics pure-play.
  • Software/autonomy and service (RaaS, AMC, retrofits) are the fastest-growing domestic capture layer.
  • Drones: four listed players; ~50–60% of subsystem value still imported.
Watch items
  • Drone PLI 2.0 finalised early 2026; Mission Drone Shakti (₹10,000 cr) proposed in Budget 2026.
  • National Strategy on Robotics still un-adopted; TAG review Feb-2026.
  • Electronics PLI lapses 2026 — IFR flags a possible 2026 robot-installation pause.
Coverage gaps

No NSE/BSE pure-play in reducers, NdFeB magnets, encoders/torque sensors, power semiconductors, LiDAR, precision machine tools or drone flight-controllers. Best proxies are emerging suppliers (Sona Comstar, Bharat Bijlee, MTAR, Schaeffler, KSH) and unlisted specialists (context tier).

05 Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunity dual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

Harmonic / strain-wave reducersEffectively 100% imported — no large-scale domestic robotics-grade producer. A 50–100 Nm unit lands at ₹35,000–₹1,20,000 (2026); lead times 12–24 weeks. Every robot joint needs one. Resilience: years / structural.

Opportunity

Domestic reducer industry from zeroChina’s Leaderdrive scale (206k units 2023 → 1.59 mn planned 2027) shows the shape; India’s humanoid programme would need reducer volumes exceeding any single Chinese supplier — a first-mover prize.

Risk

Rare-earth NdFeB magnets~95% imported; China holds ~94% of sintered output and 87–91% of rare-earth processing. Every servo motor needs magnets. First Indian plant targeted Q1 2028. Resilience: years / structural.

Opportunity

Domestic magnet capacityNational rare-earth/magnet scheme + motor makers (Bharat Bijlee, CG Power, Sona Comstar) create a captive domestic demand base; the magnet plant is the missing link.

Risk

Servo motors, drives & power silicon>60% of AC servo systems imported; ~34% domestic motor capability; power electronics (IGBT/MOSFET) and control MCU/DSP have no Indian capability. India does licensed assembly, not indigenous design. Resilience: months–years.

Opportunity

Servo & power-electronics localisationBharat Bijlee (SynchroTorq PM servo, 4,000+ packages) and CG Power (motors + Emotron drives) are scaling; India Semiconductor Mission could anchor power-semi. The design, not the assembly, is the gap.

Risk

Encoders, torque sensors & LiDARNo Indian manufacturer of robotics-grade encoders or joint torque sensors disclosed; the dominant encoder chip is made in Austria. LiDAR and depth sensors are imported. Resilience: years.

Opportunity

Precision sensing entryHigh-value, low-volume, qualification-gated — a natural niche for Indian precision-electronics firms once machine-tool and metrology capability matures.

Risk

Precision machine tools & metrologyThe gear/thread grinders (Klingelnberg, Gleason, Nidec, Höfler) and CMMs (ZEISS, Mitutoyo) that make reducers, screws and gears are fully imported — the deepest hidden dependency in a “localised” robot. Resilience: years / structural.

Opportunity

Machine-tool & metrology build-outWithout domestic gear grinding and metrology, no reducer plan can qualify. MTAR’s defence/space-grade screws are a beachhead; volume is unproven.

Risk

Drones — subsystem imports~50–60% of drone subsystem value is imported (flight controllers, ESCs, BLDC motors, optics); complete-drone imports are banned, so much “domestic” output is assembly. Resilience: months–years.

Opportunity

Component indigenisation cascadePLI 2.0, Mission Drone Shakti (₹10,000 cr proposed) and ANRF MAHA Drones (component R&D) target exactly these subsystems; Zen’s Vector Technics (51%) is an early listed step.

Risk

Skills & standardsScarce engineers in AI, actuators, control and integration; no unified testing/standards framework (a non-tariff export barrier). Capacity can be bought; qualified process engineers cannot.

Opportunity

Training & accredited labsRPTO/robotics training and BIS/ISO-aligned test labs carry durable scarcity value; a National Robotics Mission would fund both.

Deepest hidden import dependency: the precision machine tools and metrology that make the reducers, screws and gears — and the magnet and power-semiconductor supply behind the motors. India’s robotics position is “strong at the edges, empty at the core”: integration, software and assembly are real, but every high-value joint, magnet and encoder is imported.
06 Value pools & cost waterfall where value is captured · and which pool leaks to imports
Where value is captured
  • Upstream: reducers, motors, encoders/sensors, magnets and control silicon — the pools India imports.
  • Downstream: application engineering, vision, software and service (RaaS, AMC, retrofit) — the domestic capture layer.
  • Assembly of arms/cells is competitive and thin-margin.
  • Drones: airframe/assembly domestic, subsystems imported.
Who captures it (India-listed)
  • ARAPL NSE: AFFORDABLE — India’s first listed robotics co; order book >₹140 cr (Nov-2025); AMR brand Humro (RaaS, leasing).
  • Bharat Bijlee NSE: BBL — indigenously developed SynchroTorq PM servo motors (4,000+ packages).
  • CG Power NSE: CGPOWER — motors, drives (Emotron), automation.
  • Sona Comstar NSE: SONACOMS — ₹62.6 cr robotics-components capex; NEURA Robotics partnership.
  • Zen / ideaForge / Paras / DroneAcharya — the four listed drone-sector names.
Pools that leak to imports
  • Reducers, NdFeB magnets, encoders/torque sensors, power semiconductors, LiDAR and precision machine tools have no listed Indian pure-play.
  • Bottleneck nodes: harmonic/RV reducers, servo motors, drives, power electronics, encoders, magnets, LiDAR, drone flight-controllers, machine tools.
