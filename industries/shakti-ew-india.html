Snapshot
  • Shakti is the Indian Navy's indigenous capital-warship EW suite — DRDO/DLRL-designed, BEL-built — giving wideband ESM (intercept, direction-finding, radar fingerprinting) and DRFM-based ECM jamming.
  • Part of DRDO's seven-system Samudrika family (ship-borne Shakti, Nayan, Tushar; air-borne Sarang, Sarakshi, Sarvadhari, Nikash). Fitted to P-15B destroyers, P-17A frigates, Talwar-class and INS Vikrant.
Process & equipment
  • RF/digital LRUs are SMT-assembled, die-attached and wire-bonded in cleanrooms; antennas and arrays are precision-machined and hermetically sealed.
  • Spine: MMIC/HEMT fab (GaN-on-SiC) → T/R-module and microwave-module assembly → LRU build → array integration → EMC/EMI + environmental qualification → sea trials & Navy/DGQA acceptance.
  • Key machinery: MOCVD reactors, RF wafer fabs, pick-and-place/SMT lines, wire/die bonders, RF anechoic & EMI/EMC chambers, vector network analysers.
Bottlenecks
  • GaN/GaAs MMIC and SiC substrate — ~75% imported; DRDO GAETEC output is limited. Resilience: years.
  • RF test equipment, BAW/SAW filters, specialty gases, MOCVD reactors — 90–100% imported. Resilience: years.
  • FPGA/DSP and high-speed ADC/DAC — imported (US/foreign). Resilience: months–years.
  • Cognitive-EW software — India at TRL 4–5 vs China 6–7. Resilience: years.
Value pools
  • Prime integration is the largest pool and is captured by BEL (listed) as DRDO's ToT partner — ~85% of system value is captured domestically at integration.
  • RF subsystems and T/R modules are the fastest-climbing private pool (Astra Microwave, Centum, Data Patterns, Apollo Micro).
  • Software/cognitive-EW is the highest-margin, fastest-appreciating pool (BEL, Data Patterns).
  • The pools that leak to imports are the deepest ones: GaN MMIC, RF chips, FPGA, test gear, laminates, connectors.
  • Localisation opportunity sits at the MMIC/fab and cognitive-software layers — where no listed pure-play exists yet.
Market & growth
  • India EW market: ₹3,200 cr (FY24) → ₹9,200 cr (FY35) committed baseline; ₹15,000–22,000 cr under upside scenarios. [REPORTED-T3 / SCENARIO]
  • India defence-electronics: ~$6.85 bn (2024) → ~$11.35 bn (2032), ~6.2% CAGR; EW ~22.6% of it (2024). [REPORTED-T2 / CALCULATED]
  • EW systems 5-yr CAGR 8–10% (industry est.) [REPORTED-T3]; a rival estimate puts EW at $923 mn (2025) → $1.47 bn (2035), ~4.8% CAGR [CONFLICTING DATA].
  • Value is nominal; no separate physical/volume series is published for EW — volume [UNAVAILABLE]. Growth is driven by budget/mix, not unit count.
Watch items
  • Oct-2026: single-tender RFP to BEL for a Shakti system to replace Ellora Mk-II on a Shivalik-class frigate. [LIVE]
  • BEL flagged Shakti Phase IV and Shatrughat / Samghat EW solutions as near-term orders (May-2026 call). [LIVE]

Coverage gaps
  • No NSE/BSE pure-play for GaN/GaAs MMIC, FPGA/ADC silicon, RF test equipment, MIL connectors, RF laminates, radomes/mechanical or hermetic packaging — exposure is via unlisted DRDO fabs, startups and foreign-listed OEMs.

05Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunitydual-readable
Risk

GaN/GaAs MMIC & SiC substrateThe RF chip inside every T/R module and jammer amplifier is ~75% imported; SiC substrate ~95%, MOCVD reactors ~100%. DRDO's GAETEC output (~100 wafers/month) is far below need. Resilience: years.

Opportunity

Indigenous GaN fab scale-upDRDO has cracked GaN-on-SiC design; scaling yield to 1,000 wafers/month unlocks Uttam, Virupaksha and naval EW alike. Capturable via DRDO ToT + private fabs (unlisted today).

Risk

RF test equipment & BAW/SAW filtersVector network analysers, spectrum analysers, EMC chambers and filters are ~90–100% imported — the deepest hidden import dependency in a 'localised' suite. Resilience: years.

Opportunity

Test & filter localisationEvery EW/radar line needs this gear; a greenfield Indian RF-test and BAW/SAW-filter play has no incumbent. High entry barriers, captive demand.

Risk

Cognitive-EW softwareIndian programmes sit at TRL 4–5 against China's 6–7; threat libraries and adaptive algorithms are the decisive gap by 2028–2030. Resilience: years.

Opportunity

Pragyashakti integrator roleThe tri-service framework moves value from hardware to algorithms; whoever wins the integrator role captures a decade of spending (BEL, Data Patterns positioned).

Risk

Procurement velocity & certificationAcceptance-to-delivery averages ~9.5 years (2.4× peers); DGQA/Navy acceptance and sea trials gate output. Resilience: months–years.

Opportunity

Faster, iterative acceptanceCompressing the cycle toward ~4 years is the single highest-leverage reform — it lifts every listed subsystem supplier's revenue conversion.

Where value is captured
  • Prime integration is the largest pool — ~85% of system value captured domestically at integration.
  • RF subsystems / T/R modules are the fastest-climbing private pool.
  • Software & cognitive-EW is the highest-margin, fastest-appreciating pool.
  • Materials/fab sit upstream and are the deepest import-substitution plays.
Who captures it (India-listed)
  • Prime: BEL (DRDO ToT partner for Shakti; naval EW, CMS).
  • RF/subsystems: Astra Microwave, Centum, Data Patterns, Apollo Micro.
  • Software/EW suites: BEL, Data Patterns.
  • Unlisted but decisive: DRDO/DLRL, GAETEC/SSPL, AGNIT, Polymatech.
Pools that leak to imports
  • GaN/GaAs MMIC and SiC substrate — ~75–95% imported.
  • FPGA/DSP and high-speed ADC/DAC — imported.
  • RF test gear, BAW/SAW filters, specialty gases — ~90–100% imported.
