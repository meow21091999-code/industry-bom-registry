Snapshot

The PWR/VVER steam generator — the primary-to-secondary heat exchanger that turns reactor heat into steam. A VVER SG weighs ~340 t, carries ~130 km of Inconel 690 tube (~11,000 U-bend tubes) and takes ~2 years to fabricate melt-to-despatch; a unit uses four SGs.

Process & equipment
  • Seamless Inconel 690 U-bend tubing is the critical input, made by only a few global mills.
  • Shells and tube sheets are nuclear-grade SA-508/SA-533 forgings from a single heavy-forging shop (LTSSHF).
  • Tube-to-tubesheet welding and full NDT gate the build.
Bottlenecks
  • Inconel 690 SG tube is imported (Sandvik, Valinox and peers).
  • One nuclear-grade heavy-forging shop in South-East Asia.
  • Qualification and the ~2-year fabrication cycle.
Value pools
  • SG fabrication — L&T, BHEL, Walchandnagar, Godrej.
  • Inconel 690 tube and nuclear-grade forgings are the scarce upstream pools.
  • Fleet-mode demand (10×700 MWe plus SMRs) supports scale-up.
Watch items
  • NPCIL ₹747 cr SG order (2018, GHAVP plus end shields).
  • Fleet-mode SG orders.
  • SMR adoption.
Coverage gaps
  • Inconel 690 seamless U-bend tube has no Indian producer; nuclear-grade heavy forgings sit with one listed player (L&T).

05 Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunity dual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

Inconel 690 tube is importedThe qualified seamless U-bend SG tube is made only by Sandvik (Sweden), Valinox/Vallourec (France) and Sumitomo (Japan). India has no qualified maker. Resilience: years (certification-gated).

Opportunity

Indigenous SG tube qualificationMIDHANI already casts Superni 690 mill forms; a qualified seamless-tube line would close the single deepest import gap in the vessel.

Risk

One heavy-forging shopLTSSHF is the only nuclear-grade heavy-forging facility in South-East Asia — and its credit profile is weak (CRISIL ‘D’ on the NPCIL loan). A single point of failure for every SG. Resilience: years.

Opportunity

Forging scale-up / second shopFleet-mode demand (10×700 MWe + SMRs) justifies a second qualified forging source and an LTSSHF recapitalisation.

Risk

Certification-gated vendor baseClass-1 nuclear qualification (ASME N/NPT, AERB) takes years; only a handful of fabricators are qualified. Resilience: years.

Opportunity

N-stamp incumbents scaleL&T (first Indian ASME N-stamp, US-NRC-audited) and BHEL capture a widening order book as fleet mode ramps.

Risk

Fabrication machinery importedThe ultra-heavy forging press, welding automation, heat-treatment furnaces and heavy CNC are foreign-built. Resilience: months–years.

Opportunity

Machine-tool localisationA domestic heavy-machine-tool and welding-automation base would de-risk the entire heavy-fabrication chain.

Risk

Skilled tradesClass-1 welders, tube-to-tubesheet welding operators, NDT inspectors and QA engineers bind tighter than materials and cannot be bought quickly.

Opportunity

Training pipelinesBHEL / L&T / Walchandnagar qualification pipelines are a durable moat for incumbents.

Risk

Owner & design are unlistedDesign (BARC), ownership (NPCIL) and the fuel cycle sit inside DAE entities with no NSE/BSE listing. Resilience: structural.

Opportunity

SHANTI Act 2025Graded liability, a statutory AERB and private participation open SPV models for the next tranche of reactors.

Deepest hidden dependency: the SG is India’s most mature nuclear-equipment node — L&T and BHEL have built it for decades and the heavy forgings are now domestic — yet the two highest-value inputs, the Inconel 690 tube and the fabrication machinery, are still imported, and the owner (NPCIL) and designer (BARC) are unlisted. The fabrication is investable; the material and the IP are not.
06 Value pools & cost waterfall where value is captured · and which pool leaks to imports
Where value is captured
  • SG fabrication — L&T, BHEL, Walchandnagar, Godrej.
  • Inconel 690 tube and nuclear-grade forgings are the scarce upstream pools.
  • Fleet-mode demand (10×700 MWe plus SMRs) supports scale-up.
Who captures it (India-listed)
  • Larsen & Toubro NSE: LT — Shell, heads and tubesheet fabrication and assembly at Hazira; the dominant Indian SG…
  • BHEL NSE: BHEL — SG shells and assemblies at Trichy; nuclear SGs supplied since 1976
  • MIDHANI NSE: MIDHANI — Special stainless-steel plates (e.g. 304L NAG) and alloy mill forms for the DAE
  • ISGEC Heavy Engineering NSE: ISGEC — Heat-exchanger / pressure-vessel vendor on the NPCIL procurement list
  • Ador Welding NSE: ADORWELD — Welding consumables; nuclear-grade Alloy 690 overlay qualification not evidenced
  • MTAR Technologies NSE: MTAR — Precision components for refurbishment / maintenance of operating reactors
Pools that leak to imports
  • Inconel 690 seamless U-bend tube has no Indian producer; nuclear-grade heavy forgings sit with one listed player (L&T).
  • Bottleneck nodes: Heavy Forgings & Special Steels, Inconel 690 Tube Bundle, Heavy Machinery, Forming & Furnaces, Welding Consumables & Cladding, SG Level & Pressure Instrumentation.
