DDigestshort summary · full detail below
Snapshot
  • The spacecraft platform: bus, structure/thermal, power, ADCS, avionics, propulsion, AIT and materials.
  • ~50–55% of satellite content is imported, concentrated in these subsystems.
Process & equipment
  • Cleanroom AIT and environmental testing (TVAC, vibration); most qualification facilities stay with ISRO.
  • Precision machining, composites and deployable-mechanism manufacture dominate the equipment base.
Bottlenecks
  • Space-grade solar cells — 70–80% imported (years).
  • ADCS, star trackers, rad-hard electronics — imported (years).
  • Atomic clocks — 5 of 11 NavIC satellites failed (structural).
Value pools
  • Leaks upstream to imported solar cells, ADCS and rad-hard silicon.
  • Domestic capture: structures/mechanisms (L&T), electronics (Data Patterns, BEL, Centum), materials (MIDHANI), propulsion (MTAR).
Watch items
  • IN-SPACe technology transfers to industry.
  • Domestic solar-cell and ADCS qualification timelines.
Coverage gaps
  • No NSE/BSE pure-play for solar cells, ADCS, atomic clocks or qualification test infrastructure.

05Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunitydual-readable
Risk

Space-grade solar cells (III-V)70–80% imported; India has zero gallium and germanium refining capacity. Resilience: years.

Opportunity

MOCVD epitaxy + array qualificationA five-year ISRO offtake could seed domestic cell and anti-radiation-coating capacity (BEL, Dhruva Solis+).

Risk

ADCS & precision sensorsStar trackers, reaction wheels, gyros and rad-hard OBCs imported. Resilience: years.

Opportunity

Five-year subsystem ordersPlace 5-year orders with 2–3 Indian firms per category (gyros, star trackers, reaction wheels, atomic clocks, OBCs).

Risk

Atomic clocks (NavIC)Imported first-gen clocks failed on 5 of 11 satellites. Resilience: structural.

Opportunity

Indigenous rubidium clocksClocks demoed on TDS-01 (ISRO / CSIR-CEERI); qualifying a domestic supplier is a strategic prize.
