Snapshot

The PCS is the bidirectional inverter connecting the battery DC bus to the grid — ~15% of BESS capex, and the second-largest value pool. India assembles string PCS, but the power devices and grid-forming control IP are foreign.

Process & equipment
  • Topologies: 2-/3-level (NPC/ANPC) on a 1000–1500 V DC bus; central, string or hybrid architectures.
  • Key build: IGBT/SiC modules, gate drivers, magnetics/inductors, DC-link film capacitors, real-time DSP/MCU, isolation transformer.
  • Assembly is localisable (Delta, ABB, Statcon, Ador); the silicon is not.
Bottlenecks
  • No Indian IGBT/SiC power-device fab; CG Semi is OSAT only.
  • Grid-forming control IP (VSM, droop, black-start) is held abroad.
  • Magnetics materials and DC-link film capacitors are largely imported.
  • Central PCS >1 MW and SiC platforms still imported (~2–3 years from localisation).
Value pools
  • PCS hardware — Hitachi Energy India, ABB India, CG Power, Servotech, Waaree, Vikram, Premier.
  • Power semiconductors — the pool that leaks entirely; CG Semi (CGPOWER) is the only listed OSAT exposure.
  • Grid-forming firmware — foreign IP today, a localisation frontier.
  • Inverter-duty transformers & switchgear — a domestic oligopoly (CG Power, Hitachi Energy).
Watch items
  • 1 Jul 2027 — CEA draft: all BESS PCS must have grid-forming control.
  • MNRE draft (May-2026) — ≥60% localisation of PCS/EMS for ≥100 MW grid-forming BESS from 2027.
  • CG Semi G2 OSAT (Oct–Nov 2027); Tata–PSMC Dholera first silicon ~Dec-2026 (logic, not power).
  • Six Chinese firms pre-approved to JV with Indian makers under the MNRE draft.
Coverage gaps
  • No listed pure-play for IGBT/SiC devices or grid-forming control IP; Ador, Statcon, Wattpower, Delta-India are unlisted.

05 Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunity dual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

No Indian power-semiconductor fabEvery IGBT/SiC device in an Indian PCS is imported; CG Semi is OSAT assembly/test; Dholera makes logic, not power; a fab is 3–5+ years and US$1–5 bn away. Resilience: critical.

Opportunity

OSAT today, fab tomorrowCG Semi's Sanand OSAT (Jul-2026) is the first step; the India Semiconductor Mission and power-device demand make a domestic device line the logical next node.

Risk

Grid-forming control IPGFM (VSM, droop, synthetic inertia, black-start) IP is held by SMA, GE Vernova, Sungrow and Tesla; Indian competence is nascent. Resilience: high.

Opportunity

The CEA mandate creates a marketMaking grid-forming PCS mandatory from Jul-2027 creates a captive domestic market for whoever builds the control stack locally.

Risk

Magnetics & film capacitorsMagnetics materials and DC-link film capacitors are largely imported; domestic quality/scale is a work in progress. Resilience: medium-high.

Opportunity

Passive localisationRising PCS volume plus the ≥60% localisation draft pull passive-component and magnetics manufacturing into India.

Risk

Central PCS & silicon-import costCentral inverters >1 MW and SiC platforms are imported; long-term BESS duty deferment can disadvantage domestic integrators. Resilience: high.

Opportunity

Localisation policyThe ≥60% PCS/EMS localisation draft and the 20% DCR route procurement to domestic builders; six Chinese firms may only participate via JVs.

Deepest hidden dependency: the power semiconductor itself — India can assemble a PCS cabinet but makes none of the IGBT/SiC devices or the grid-forming control IP inside it, so the two highest-value, hardest-to-copy layers remain entirely foreign.
