Snapshot

The power conversion system is the bidirectional inverter between a battery DC bus and the grid — 11–22% of BESS cost depending on scope, and the node where India's storage build-out meets its semiconductor gap. India assembles PCS cabinets up to 1.25 MW; every power device, gate driver, film capacitor and utility-scale plant controller inside them is imported.

Process & equipment
  • Topologies are 2- and 3-level (NPC/ANPC) on a 1000–1500 V DC bus, in central, string or hybrid architecture; SiC is entering at the string end.
  • The build is device stacks, gate drivers, magnetics, DC-link film capacitors, control boards, cooling and enclosure — then factory test at full power into a resistive or grid-simulator load.
  • Localisable steps are cabinet assembly, magnetics winding, harness and integration; the semiconductor front end is not.
  • Key machinery: SMT lines, wave/selective solder, busbar fabrication, coil winding, potting, environmental chambers and a MW-class test bay.
Bottlenecks
  • Power devices — 100% imported, no Indian IGBT or SiC fab; SiC module lead times run 12–16 weeks. Resilience: years.
  • Grid-forming control at plant scale — Indian capability exists at 1–2 MW unit size, unproven at 100 MW+ plant level. Months to years.
  • DC-link film capacitors and nanocrystalline cores — imported, small value, long qualification. Months.
  • Utility-scale price competition — Chinese inverters held 85.5% of central-inverter shipments in Q1 2026. Structural on cost.
  • Certification and test capacity — BIS/BEE compliance plus grid-forming validation; the test bays are few. Months.
Value pools
  • Power devices capture the most operating profit and leak entirely abroad — Infineon, onsemi, Mitsubishi, Fuji, ST, Rohm.
  • Grid-forming firmware and plant EMS are the highest-return pool per rupee of capital; India's capability here is unlisted.
  • PCS assembly is domestic, contested and thin — Hitachi Energy India explicitly guides BESS balance-of-plant margins below its portfolio average.
  • Inverter-duty transformers and MV switchgear are the part of the AC block India makes well, and the listed names (CG Power, Hitachi Energy, Shilchar, Voltamp) earn real margin there.
  • The 40% DCR is a price umbrella for whoever can document domestic content first — and a compliance cost for everyone else.
  • Power-module OSAT is a small pool today: CG Semi G1 is 300 million units a year of assembly and test, with G2 production late 2028–2029.

  • 15-Oct-2026 — CEA draft consultation closed (extended from 04-Oct); the final regulation has not been notified.
  • 01-Jul-2027 — CEA draft would require grid-forming control on all BESS PCS and on 15% of inverters in new RE plants.
  • 01-Apr-2027 — CEA BESS technical standards take effect: PCS efficiency ≥95%, AC-AC round-trip ≥70%.
  • Mid-2027 — CG Semi G2 qualification; SiCSem's SiC fab and Tata–PSMC's Dholera fab remain under construction.
Coverage gaps
  • No listed exposure to power devices, gate drivers, DC-link film capacitors or grid-forming control IP; CG Semi is OSAT only.
  • Delta Electronics India, Statcon Energiaa, Ador Digatron, Wattpower and Enertech — the firms actually building central-class PCS in India — are unlisted.

05 Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunity dual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

No power-device fabIndia makes none of the 1200–1700 V IGBT or SiC modules inside a PCS; 100% of power valves and about 90% of wide-bandgap devices are imported. CG Semi is assembly and test. Resilience: years.

Opportunity

OSAT today, fab tomorrowFive ISM units are in commercial operation and Semicon 2.0 carries ₹1,27,500 crore. The step from OSAT to a discrete power line is smaller than to a logic fab, and CDIL already runs Si and SiC discrete production at Mohali.

Risk

Utility-scale price competitionChinese suppliers held 85.5% of central-inverter shipments in Q1 2026, and Indian-made battery components cost 18–61% more than imported equivalents. On an unsubsidised bid, the imported PCS wins on price. Resilience: structural.

Opportunity

The 40% DCR is a price umbrellaThe Nov-2026 VGF scheme makes 40% domestic content a condition of support on 30 GWh of BESS. Localising the 60% of PCS value that is not silicon — magnetics, busbars, sheet metal, harness, assembly labour — is the fastest route to a compliant bill of materials.

Risk

Grid-forming at plant scaleThe CEA draft would require grid-forming control on every BESS PCS from 01-Jul-2027, and the CEA advisory already asks for stability at short-circuit ratios at or below 2.0 with response inside 5–30 ms. Indian capability is proven at 1–2 MW units, not at 100 MW plants. Resilience: months to years.

Opportunity

A mandate that creates the marketIf the draft is notified, every BESS PCS sold in India needs compliant firmware and a validation report — a services and IP pool with almost no capex. Statcon and Ador hold unit-level capability; the plant controller is the open position.

Risk

Passives and coresDC-link polypropylene film capacitors and nanocrystalline or ferrite cores are imported, small in value but long in qualification; a substituted part re-opens the PCS type test. Resilience: months.

Opportunity

A ₹100–200 crore localisationA 1000–1500 V DC-link film line and a wound-core line are small-ticket projects by fab standards, and both feed inverters, EV chargers and drives at once, not only BESS.

Risk

Certification and test capacityBIS and BEE compliance plus the CEA BESS standards from 01-Apr-2027 (PCS efficiency ≥95%, AC-AC round-trip ≥70%) require MW-class test bays and grid simulators; few exist in India and queues stretch lead times. Resilience: months.

Opportunity

Test bay as a serviceA shared grid-forming validation facility would serve every PCS vendor in the country and is the sort of asset a listed BoS player could host at a regulated return.

A PCS assembly line is a ₹100–300 crore asset, not a US$1–5 bn fab, and it can be run on imported silicon — which is precisely why entry is easy and returns are competed away. The capital barrier sits one layer up, at the device line.

Two live migrations run at once: silicon IGBT to SiC MOSFET, and grid-following to grid-forming control. India follows on both, with unit-level grid-forming capability and no device capability. SiC lead times of 12–16 weeks mean the migration is gated by supply, not by demand.
