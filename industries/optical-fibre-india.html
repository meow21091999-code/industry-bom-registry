Snapshot

Optical fibre (drawing) — turning the glass preform into 125 µm fibre. India has world-scale draw capacity (~100 mn fkm, ~2× its own consumption) but depends on imported preform for the glass. Scope: India, NSE/BSE. Re-analysed 2026-10-22 (delta pass over the 2026-10-08 entry).

Process & equipment
  • A draw tower and ~2,000 °C furnace pull fibre from preform; dual-layer acrylate coating, proof testing and colouring/ribbonising follow inline.
  • Draw towers, furnaces and winders are imported (Rosendahl Nextrom, SG Controls) or locally assembled (SIMCO, Machine & Controls); consumables are UV resins, inks, helium and germanium-doped preform.
Bottlenecks
  • Preform — ~65–75% imported; resilience: years, shortening as STL/HFCL/Finolex build.
  • Germanium / GeCl₄ export controls (China >90% of refining; ban to US/Japan suspended only to Nov-2026).
  • Helium — China’s Jul-2026 export ban plus Qatar/Hormuz disruption; India almost fully import-dependent.
  • Fibre price spike — 2.8–5× 2025; global supply deficit ~180 mn fkm in 2026, to persist to ~2028.
  • Draw-tower equipment and ceramic ferrules remain import-only.
Value pools
  • Value concentrates in preform, not in drawing — but the up-cycle has repriced fibre upward.
  • STL is the only fully integrated Indian player; HFCL becomes the second by Oct-2028, Finolex the third.
  • Fibre drawing is competitive and structurally lower-margin than preform.
  • Localisation: integrated fibre, specialty grades, ferrules.
Market & growth
  • India OFC market (scope: cable, India): ~$550 mn (2025) → >$1 bn (2031), CAGR ~11% [REPORTED-T3, tele.net, Aug-2026]. Value (nominal); global OFC ~$13 bn → $23 bn (~10%).
  • Last-year: India optical-fibre exports +158% YoY to $483 mn in Apr–Aug FY27 (US >half at $266.7 mn) [REPORTED-T1, Ministry of Commerce, Oct-2026]. India fibre demand seen roughly tripling to ~60 mn km/yr.
  • Growth is demand-led (AI data centres, FTTH, 5G backhaul, BharatNet Phase III); data centres ~18–20% of global fibre demand in 2026, rising to 30–35% in 2027.
  • [CONFLICTING DATA] on other scopes: India fibre-optics $323.9 mn 2024→$866.1 mn 2029 (21.7%, MarketsandMarkets); India OFC ~$550 mn 2025→~$1 bn 2031 (~11%, tele.net). Price/mix is strongly positive as data-centre fibre runs above telco fibre.
Watch items
  • Germanium export-ban suspension (US-specific: 2024 No. 46 Art. 2, suspended by 2025 No. 72) runs to 27-Nov-2026; MOFCOM’s 28-Sep-2026 statement extended the wider Kuala Lumpur arrangement to 10-Jan-2027 but names neither announcement — the extension is unconfirmed. The licence regime persists either way. [LIVE]
  • China’s Jul-2026 helium export ban (Hormuz/Qatar trigger) — India imports almost all its helium. [LIVE]
  • India’s ADD on SMOF to Aug-2028; China extended its own ADD on Indian SMOF to Aug-2031. [CYCLICAL]
  • Aksh Optifibre admitted to CIRP (NCLT Jaipur, 19-Jun-2026); resolution window to ~Dec-2026. [LIVE]
  • BharatNet Phase III (₹65,000 cr) — STL/Polycab invoked force majeure on the fibre cost spike. [LIVE]
Coverage gaps

No listed pure-play for draw-tower equipment, ceramic ferrules, coating resins/process gases or fibre test equipment — nearest: STL / HFCL / Finolex (fibre), foreign equipment makers.

05 Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunity dual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

Preform — the upstream choke point~65–75% of preform is imported (China, Japan); domestic capacity meets only ~25–35% of demand, and ~60–70% of the value-chain profit pool sits in this one step.

Opportunity

India’s second & third integrated playersSTL is already integrated (₹3,000 cr capex FY27-29); Finolex has commissioned captive preform (100→200 MT) and HFCL is building 600 MT/yr by Oct-2028 (₹1,800 cr total capex).

Risk

Fibre price spike & global deficitSpot fibre prices are 2.8–5× their 2025 levels; the CRUofpi index ran 78.6 (Mar-2024) to 263.0 (Mar-2026), the sharpest rise since 2017. A ~180 mn fkm global shortfall in 2026 is expected to persist to ~2028.

Opportunity

Integrated players win the queueIn a glass shortage, whoever makes their own preform fills orders others cannot; STL’s utilisation is ~70% and HFCL’s fibre lines are near ~100%, with order books booked into 2027.

Risk

Germanium & helium — two input shocks at onceChina holds >90% of germanium refining and has run it under export licence since Aug-2023 (ban to the US/Japan suspended only to Nov-2026); it also imposed a helium export ban in Jul-2026 after Qatar/Hormuz disruption. India imports almost all its helium.

Opportunity

Diversify & recycleGermany, the US and Kazakhstan have smaller germanium capacity; new ex-China projects target +95 t by 2030. Fibre-scrap recycling and India’s Critical Minerals Mission are the medium-term substitutes; helium recovery/recycling is the near-term lever.

Risk

Draw-tower equipmentTowers, induction furnaces and winders are imported (Rosendahl Nextrom, SG Controls); there is no Indian listed equipment pure-play and new preform/draw capacity carries an 18–24-month lead time.

Opportunity

Local equipment buildSIMCO and Machine & Controls build towers, colouring lines and FRP lines in India — an under-owned, pick-and-shovel play on the fibre capex cycle.

Risk

Precision ceramic ferrulesSub-micron zirconia ferrules are dominated by China and Japan; India largely grinds imported blanks, so the connector end of the chain stays import-dependent.

Opportunity

Make-in-India ferrulesDomestic firms are moving from grinding to full powder→sinter→grind manufacturing — a small but strategically placed import-substitution niche.

Risk

Capacity vs consumption~100 mn fkm of capacity against ~46 mn fkm of domestic use makes India export-dependent and exposed to the global fibre price cycle and to dumping — hence the anti-dumping duty regime.

Opportunity

Global AI demand cycleAI data centres, 5G densification and BharatNet 2.0 have fibre makers sold out; India’s fibre exports surged +158% YoY to $483 mn in Apr–Aug FY27, with the US taking more than half.

Risk

Consumables & specialty gradesUV coating resin, colouring inks and process gases are imported; G.657 and specialty grades are harder to draw, and consistency and yield separate winners from the pack.

Opportunity

ADD protection & premium mixThe ADD (Aug-2023) covers bend-insensitive G.657, and the data-centre mix (G.657.A2, hollow-core, high-count) carries a steep price premium — a tailwind for domestic drawers.

Deepest hidden import dependency: the preform glass itself — India draws world-scale fibre from imported glass, and the germanium tetrachloride (GeCl₄) and helium that go into it. A step that generates over 60% of the value-chain profit pool is controlled by fewer than ten firms worldwide, and only one Indian company is fully backward-integrated into it.
06 Value pools & cost waterfall where value is captured · and which pool leaks to imports
Where value is captured
  • Value concentrates in preform (~60–70%), not in drawing — but the 2026 up-cycle has repriced drawn fibre sharply upward.
  • STL is the only fully integrated Indian player; HFCL becomes the second by Oct-2028, Finolex the third.
  • Fibre drawing is competitive and structurally lower-margin than preform.
  • Localisation: integrated fibre, specialty grades, ferrules.
Who captures it (India-listed)
  • Sterlite Technologies (STL) NSE/BSE: STLTECH — Only fully backward-integrated fibre drawer (own preform); ₹3,000 cr capex FY27-29, capacity +50%, ~70% utilisation, ₹20,000 cr FY29 revenue target [REPORTED]
  • HFCL NSE/BSE: HFCL — Fibre 28 → 43.1 mn fkm (Jul-2028); ₹28,911 cr order book (Q2 FY27); ₹1,800 cr capex [REPORTED]
  • Finolex Cables NSE/BSE: FINCABLES — Fibre-draw 4 → 8 mn fkm; preform 100 → 200 MT; comm-cable revenue +62% YoY in Q1 FY27 [REPORTED]
  • Aksh Optifibre NSE/BSE: AKSHOPTFBR — Draws fibre and makes FRP rods at Bhiwadi; admitted to CIRP 19-Jun-2026 [REPORTED]
  • Universal Cables NSE/BSE: UNIVCABLES — Birla Furukawa JV (fibre; imports preform from Furukawa Japan) [REPORTED]
  • Polycab India NSE/BSE: POLYCAB — OFC vertical at Halol [REPORTED]
  • Linde India NSE/BSE: LINDEINDIA — Industrial-gas major; plausible supplier to fibre lines, no fibre-specific contract disclosed [INFERENCE]
Pools that leak to imports
  • No listed pure-play for draw-tower equipment, ceramic ferrules, coating resins/process gases or fibre test equipment — nearest: STL/HFCL/Finolex (fibre), foreign equipment makers.
  • Bottleneck nodes: Optical Fibre Preform (Glass), Bare Fibre Drawing (125 µm), Draw-Tower Equipment & Furnace, Specialty / Bend-Insensitive Fibre (G.657), Precision Ceramic Ferrules.
