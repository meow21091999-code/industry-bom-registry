Snapshot

Grid-scale battery energy storage (BESS) in India — cells to containerised systems, PCS, BMS/EMS, grid interface, EPC and the developer layer. The operating base reached ≈12.6–14.5 GWh by Sep-2026 against a 198 GWh pipeline and a 236 GWh requirement by FY32; cells remain ~90% imported.

Process & equipment
  • Cell making runs coating → calendering → stacking → formation → aging. India had ≈60 GWh of pack assembly and almost no cells at the start of FY27; Exide’s 6 GWh phase-1 cell line was commissioned in Sep-2026 and Reliance’s 40 GWh Jamnagar plant is in advanced commissioning.
  • Cell-line machinery — electrode coaters, stackers, formation cabinets, laser welders, HV test rigs — is imported, largely from Chinese and Korean OEMs; 85 categories of it became duty-free until 31-Mar-2029.
  • Container and rack assembly is where India’s live value-add sits: Pace Digitek doubled to 5 GWh in Aug-2026 and commissioned in-house container fabrication.
Bottlenecks
  • Cells & cell materials — FY26 imports ₹41,667 cr (+64%); Q1 FY27 ₹15,721 cr (+94%); >90% of cathode and anode active material processed in China. Resilience: years.
  • PLI delivery — 1.4 GWh commissioned of 40 GWh awarded; Ola has cut its plan to 5 GWh by FY29; first incentive disbursal is FY28. Years.
  • Cell-making equipment — Chinese export controls were cited by the Ministry of Heavy Industries as a cause of delay; the duty exemption to 2029 is the countermeasure.
  • PCS & power semiconductors — 100% grid-forming PCS now mandatory for BESS of 50 MW and above, and no Indian IGBT/SiC fab exists. Months to years.
  • Project economics — battery prices hardened through 2026; Crisil flags 12 GWh of under-construction capacity with weak returns and 8–9 GWh at delay risk.
Value pools
  • Cells are 40–55% of system cost and still leak abroad; the domestic pool only forms in 2027–28 (Exide, Reliance, Amara Raja, Ola, and the unlisted Agratas).
  • Pack and container assembly is the live domestic pool — low capex, ≈6–7% operating margin on assembly (Amara Raja), and the layer the 20% domestic-content floor is met on.
  • Grid interface stays with a domestic oligopoly (Hitachi Energy, CG Power, GE Vernova, Siemens Energy) and gains from the grid-forming mandate.
  • Developer/BOO is a 15-year BESPA annuity: Adani Green holds 6.63 GWh, over half the national fleet; NTPC Green, Oriana, JSW and Tata Power follow.
  • EMS software is the highest-return pool and counts toward domestic content — with no listed vehicle.
  • Recycling is incentive-backed under the National Critical Mineral Mission; specialists Attero and Lohum are unlisted.
Market & growth

Watch items

  • CEA grid-forming advisory in force since 10-Jul-2026 — 100% GFM PCS for BESS ≥50 MW; bids concluded by 10-Aug-2026 exempt.
  • CEA co-location draft: comments closed 15-Oct-2026, final notification pending, applies to plants commissioned after 1-Jul-2027.
  • Local content: 20% floor notified Dec-2025 and counted against EMS software; a 40% proposal is under consultation but not notified.
Coverage gaps
  • No listed pure-play for BMS, EMS software, power semiconductors, thermal management or fire suppression; cell materials reachable only through upstream chemicals.

05 Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunity dual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

Cells & cell materialsCell imports reached ₹41,667 cr in FY26 (+64%) and ₹15,721 cr in Q1 FY27 alone (+94%); over 90% of cathode and anode active material is processed in China, and electrolyte is the only input Indian cell makers buy locally. Resilience: years.

Opportunity

The cell pool opens in 2027–28Exide commissioned 6 GWh at Bengaluru in Sep-2026, Reliance’s 40 GWh Jamnagar plant is in advanced commissioning, Amara Raja starts commercial cells in Jan-Feb 2027, and Agratas (unlisted) targets 20 GWh at Sanand in early 2027 — the first real domestic substitution window.

Risk

PLI deliveryOf 40 GWh awarded, 1.4 GWh is commissioned, all of it Ola’s; Ola has since cut its plan to ≈5 GWh by FY29; Rajesh Exports lost its extension amid a Sebi probe; no beneficiary has claimed an incentive. Resilience: years.

Opportunity

A 10 GWh tender, re-weighted to capabilityBids for the remaining 10 GWh of grid-scale ACC capacity close on 10-Nov-2026 after an extension from 13-Oct, with technical scoring at 80% and 70% of that on domestic value addition — the round in which Exide, Amara Raja and Waaree are expected to bid.

Risk

Cell-plant equipment & process know-howCoaters, stackers, formation cabinets and laser welders come from Chinese and Korean OEMs; the Ministry of Heavy Industries told Parliament that Chinese export controls on cell-making equipment and materials were a direct cause of missed PLI timelines. Resilience: structural on availability, months on cost.

Opportunity

Duty-free capital goods to 202985 categories of battery-making capital equipment now enter duty-free until 31-Mar-2029 against 7.5–15% earlier, while finished cells still attract 20% BCD — the tariff structure deliberately favours importing the plant rather than the product.

Risk

PCS, power semiconductors & grid-formingPCS is 10–18% of cost and largely imported, there is no Indian IGBT or SiC fab, and from 10-Jul-2026 every BESS of 50 MW and above must run 100% grid-forming PCS with EMT studies and black-start demonstration at commissioning. Resilience: months for the equipment, years for the domestic device.

Opportunity

A certification moat, not a price fightGrid-forming control is hard to buy off the shelf; whoever qualifies it first in India captures the PCS pool as Chinese vendors are pushed out of the compliant segment. CG Semi’s OSAT is the first rung toward a domestic device line.

Risk

Project economics & executionBattery prices hardened through 2026 against tariffs bid in 2025: Crisil puts 12 GWh of under-construction capacity at weak-return risk and 8–9 GWh at delay risk, and G R Infraprojects terminated an NTPC BESS contract in Sep-2026. Under VGF scheme 1, only 0.5 GWh of 12.7 GWh awarded is commissioned. Resilience: months.

Opportunity

Execution is the differentiator — and it pricesPace Digitek converted a 5 GWh line into a ₹10,735 cr executable order book at a 19.6% Q2 EBITDA margin while weaker sponsors slipped; a consolidating EPC/integrator tier is exactly what a fragmented auction market rewards.

Risk

Skills, testing & certification capacityCell-process engineers are scarce, EMT and black-start studies need specialist simulation capability, and accredited-lab slots gate every PLI claim and every BMS import. None of this can be bought quickly at any price.

Opportunity

The qualification layerNABL/ARAI-accredited testing, grid-simulation engineering and process depth carry durable pricing power as the domestic-content floor rises from 20% toward the proposed 40%.

Deepest hidden import dependency: not the cell but the machine that makes it. Cell-plant equipment — electrode coaters, calenders, stackers, formation and aging cabinets, laser welders, HV test rigs and the dry rooms around them — is dominated by Chinese and Korean OEMs and has been directly named by the government as a cause of PLI slippage. Duty-free import to 2029 lowers the cost of that dependency without reducing it.
06 Value pools & cost waterfall where value is captured · and which pool leaks to imports
Where value is captured
  • Cells (40–55% of system cost) leak abroad; the domestic pool forms only in 2027–28 as Exide, Reliance, Amara Raja and Ola ramp.
  • Pack, rack and container assembly is the live domestic pool — low capex, ≈6–7% operating margin on assembly (Amara Raja), and the layer the 20% domestic-content floor is met on.
  • Grid interface (transformers, switchgear, cables) is a domestic oligopoly with pricing power, and grid-forming retrofits add volume without new entrants.
  • Developer/BOO is a 15-year BESPA annuity; Adani Green’s 6.63 GWh is over half the national fleet and the layer where scale has already concentrated.
  • EMS software counts toward domestic content and carries the highest ROIC in the chain — with no listed vehicle to buy it through.
  • Recycling is incentive-backed under the National Critical Mineral Mission; the specialists are unlisted.
Who captures it (India-listed)
  • Assembly and integration: Pace Digitek (₹8,528 cr energy order book, 19.6% Q2 EBITDA), Waaree, Premier, Vikram, Amara Raja.
  • Grid interface: Hitachi Energy India, CG Power, GE Vernova T&D, Siemens Energy, Polycab, KEI.
  • Annuity: Adani Green, NTPC Green, NTPC, JSW Energy, Oriana, Tata Power, IndiGrid.
  • Upstream chemistry, the only listed route into materials: HSCL, Neogen, Tata Chemicals.
Pools that leak to imports
  • Cells, cell materials, BMS (≈90%), PCS and all power semiconductors. In FY26 that leak was ₹41,667 cr of cells, ≈84% of it from China.
Value capture — which node keeps the money
  • Li-ion cells — 3.7 / 5 (≈74 / 100).

  • Power semiconductors (IGBT / SiC) — 3.5 / 5 (≈70 / 100).
