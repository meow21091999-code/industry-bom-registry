Snapshot

Grid-scale battery energy storage (BESS) in India — cells to containerised systems, PCS, EMS, grid interface, EPC and the developer layer. India has ~10–14 GWh commissioned against a 47 GW / 236 GWh need by 2031-32; cells are ~90% imported.

Process & equipment
  • Cell making is a coating→calendering→stacking→formation line; India mostly assembles cells into modules, racks and containers.
  • Key machinery — electrode coaters, stackers, formation cabinets, laser welders — is imported, largely Chinese.
  • Container & pack assembly is India’s live value-add (Waaree, Pace Digitek, Premier, Vikram).
Bottlenecks
  • Cells & cell materials — ~90% imported; China controls 97–99% of anode.
  • BMS (~90% imported) — now behind a UL 1973 Ed.3 gate.
  • PCS & power semiconductors — imported power electronics.
  • Battery price rebound in 2026 vs tariffs struck on lower costs (Crisil: 8–9 GWh delay risk).
Value pools
  • Cells (40–55% of cost) leak to imports — Ola, Exide, Amara Raja, Reliance, Waaree are building.
  • Pack/container assembly, EMS software and TMS are the pools that stay domestic — and meet the 20% DCR.
  • Grid interface (transformers, switchgear, cables) is a domestic oligopoly (Hitachi, CG, GE Vernova).
  • Developer/BOO annuity (12–15 yr BESPA) sits with NTPC Green, Tata Power, Adani, JSW.
  • Recycling is a new incentive-backed pool under the National Critical Mineral Mission.
Watch items
  • CEA draft (Sep-2026): mandatory grid-forming PCS + 10% co-located storage for RE plants from Jul-2027.
  • MNRE draft (May-2026): ≥60% localisation of PCS/EMS for ≥100 MW grid-forming BESS tenders from 2027.
  • 20% domestic-content rule on VGF BESS; rising to 40% and beyond.
  • PLI-ACC disbursements expected from FY28; customs-duty exemption extended to Mar-2029.
Coverage gaps
  • No listed pure-play for BMS, EMS, power semiconductors, thermal management or fire suppression.

05 Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunity dual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

Cells & cell materials~90% of cells and nearly all cathode, anode, separator and electrolyte are imported; China controls 97–99% of anode and has used export controls on graphite (2023) and LFP technology (Jul-2025). Resilience: years.

Opportunity

Domestic gigafactoriesOla, Exide (EESL), Amara Raja, Reliance (Jamnagar, 40→120 GWh) and Waaree are building; PLI-ACC disbursements begin FY28; a PLI for cell materials is being framed.

Risk

BMS & control electronics~90% of BMS is imported — a safety-critical, cybersecurity-sensitive layer — and now gated by MNRE’s UL 1973 Ed.3 + ISO 15118-20 import rule (Sep-2026).

Opportunity

Indigenous BMS & EMSMNRE now mandates India-developed EMS software; private players (Xbattery, Maxwell, Kalkitech) are scaling — an unlisted pure-play gap a listing could fill.

Risk

PCS & power semiconductorsPCS is 10–18% of cost and largely imported; there is no Indian power-semiconductor fab for high-power IGBT / SiC devices.

Opportunity

Localising power electronicsHitachi Energy, CG Power, Waaree, Vikram and Premier are building PCS capability; CG Semi (OSAT, Sanand) is the route toward a domestic device line.

Risk

Project economics & battery-price reboundBattery prices rebounded in 2026 while many tariffs were struck on lower costs; Crisil flags 8–9 GWh of the pipeline at delay risk, and a 5 GWh cell plant runs at negative EBITDA.

Opportunity

Downstream is where the money isWood Mackenzie: near-term value sits in containers, EMS, SCADA and packs — exactly the 20% DCR-compliant layers Indian firms already own.

Risk

Skilled manpower & certificationCell-process engineers are scarce; Chinese-technician visa delays and accredited-lab test slots slow every ramp — people and test slots cannot be bought.

Opportunity

Qualification moatNABL/ARAI-accredited testing and process-engineering depth carry durable pricing power as DCR rises toward 40% and beyond.

Deepest hidden import dependency: the cell-line machinery itself — electrode coaters, stackers, formation cabinets, laser welders and high-voltage test rigs — is dominated by Chinese and Korean equipment OEMs and is subject to Chinese export-control measures, making it the true gate on how fast India can localise cells.
