Snapshot

Optical transceivers & modules — the pluggable 100G/400G/800G optics in every transport port, and the fastest-growing, most import-dependent part of the optical stack. Scope: India, NSE/BSE.

Process & equipment
  • Optical engines are assembled from imported DSPs, laser dies and PICs; TO-can assembly, active alignment, burn-in and high-speed test are the key steps.
  • High-speed test and qualification equipment is imported.
Bottlenecks
  • Optical DSP / retimer ASICs (Broadcom/Marvell/Credo; no Indian design).
  • Light sources EML/VCSEL/DFB (InP/GaAs die; no Indian fab) and silicon-photonics PIC engines.
  • Module integration is only final assembly; high-speed test imported.
Value pools
  • Value sits in the DSPs, laser dies and PICs — all imported.
  • India's value-add is final assembly.
  • The AI data-centre wave is the big domestic opportunity.
  • Localisation path: module integration → design.
Watch items
  • India data-centre capacity ramp (950 → 2,500 MW by 2032).
  • 800G adoption.
  • Transceiver duty changes.
  • Tejas / HFCL module-integration progress.
Coverage gaps

No listed pure-play across DSP, laser dies, PIC, TO-can and test — nearest: Tejas/HFCL (module integration).

05 Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunity dual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

Optical DSP & retimer ASICsNo Indian design or fab of PAM4 DSPs; 400G/800G engines rely on Broadcom, Marvell and Credo silicon. Export controls and allocation risk sit here.

Opportunity

Fabless DSP designDesign-led PLI plus India's chip-design talent base make a fabless optical-DSP play the highest-value localisation frontier.

Risk

Light sources — EML / VCSEL / DFBInP/GaAs laser-die fabrication is absent in India; dies come from Coherent, Lumentum, Broadcom, Sumitomo. The single hardest layer to localise.

Opportunity

Compound-semiconductor seedIndia's emerging compound-semi / OSAT push could seed laser-die packaging and test even before wafer fab.

Risk

Module integration is final assemblyIndia's present value-add is integrating imported optical engines, lasers and ICs into housings — thin margin, low IP.

Opportunity

Integration → designTejas and HFCL scale from assembly toward design-led IP; HFCL's data-centre connectivity plant (₹215 cr, 2.7 lakh assemblies/yr, by Sep-2027) deepens the assembly base.

Risk

High-speed test & qualification800G and coherent test capacity is limited in India; buyers rely on overseas labs, stretching qualification cycles and time-to-deploy.

Opportunity

Local test & burn-in as a serviceQualification-gated labs carry durable scarcity value and are a cheaper entry point than fab.

Risk

Import dependence & concentrationOver 60% of demand (85–90% by value at 100G+) is imported, China-dominant; duty changes and geopolitics move landed cost directly.

Opportunity

Import substitutionHigh-count cable, connector and preform lines (HFCL preform ~₹580–600 cr; STL hollow-core fibre) plus PLI-backed assembly target the substitutable layers.

Deepest hidden import dependency: the InP/GaAs laser dies and the PAM4 DSP silicon inside every 400G/800G module — the two chips India neither designs nor fabs today. Module assembly can localise; these two layers cannot, in the current window.
