DG Digest — at a glance short summary; full detail below
Snapshot

India is the world’s sixth-largest chemicals producer — a ~US$220 billion sector PIB projects at US$300 billion by 2030 — and still structurally short of the intermediates it consumes.

Process & equipment
  • A process industry: naphtha, gas or salt → cracker, reformer or chlor-alkali → intermediates → grades; specialty and fine chemistry is batch.
  • Cracker, reformer and phenol technology is licensed (Linde, Lummus, KBR, Technip); reactors and cold boxes are imported, and Indian EPC builds the shell.
Bottlenecks
  • Propylene / ethylene availability gates everything; Deepak bought propylene at higher prices through H1 FY27. Years.
  • PVC: ~66% of FY26 demand imported; EDC/VCM, not capital, is the constraint. Years.
  • Methanol (over 3 MMT) and acetic acid (~1.2 MMT) imported, Iran ~52% of methanol import value. Months–years.
  • Catalysts and licensed technology almost fully imported; the Chinese surplus is structural, not cyclical.
Value pools
  • Integrated upstream (RIL, IOCL, GAIL, OPaL) captures the feedstock-to-polymer spread.
  • Phenol / acetone re-rated in 2026 on dislocation and protection; Adperma’s 345 kt lands into it in Q4 FY27.
  • Specialty and fine chemistry is the durable export pool: SRF chemicals +25%, Navin Fluorine +57%.
  • PVC is the weakest pool: commodity, remedy-protected, still loss-making at Chemplast Sanmar.
  • Solvents remain the largest leak; catalysts and process technology the highest-margin one India does not hold.
Market & growth
  • Value (nominal, 5-yr): 11.6% CAGR, ₹9.62 → ₹15.14 lakh crore, FY19–FY24. [CALCULATED]
  • Volume: 2.8% CAGR FY16–FY25; last year +6.3%; 3-year 0.7%. [CALCULATED]
  • Value grew ~4x faster than volume — mostly price and mix.
  • FY26 value of output [UNAVAILABLE]; US$300 billion by 2030 is a projection. [SCENARIO]
Watch items
  • PVC: US$83.72–143.82/t countervailing duty recommended 26-Oct-2026, unnotified; minimum import price lapses ~24-Jan-2027. [LIVE]
  • Phenol: case AD(OI)-11/2026 has no findings; Adperma targets output in Q4 FY27. [LIVE]
  • BHAVYA Rasayan: state park proposals due 30-Nov-2026. [LIVE]
Coverage gaps

No NSE/BSE pure-play in styrene, acrylonitrile, MDI/TDI or catalysts; Adperma and OPaL are unlisted, so the phenol re-rating is investable only via Deepak Nitrite.

05 Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunity dual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

Propylene / ethylene availabilityOlefins cannot be easily imported as hazardous gases, and domestic propylene supply was disturbed for six months: Deepak Nitrite had to buy from non-traditional sources at higher prices through H1 FY27 and ran its 400 kt phenol chain slightly below nameplate. Resilience: years.

Opportunity

Cracker build-outIOCL Paradip (₹61,077 cr, 2029) and the RIL and Adani additions are the import-substitution play — with EPC and equipment localisation alongside. Every new cracker also de-bottlenecks an existing downstream chain.

Risk

PVC / EDC-VCM~66% of FY26 PVC demand was imported (~3.0 MMT on 4.55 MMT) against domestic capacity of only ~1.6 MMT. EDC availability, not capital, caps expansion — which is why Adani’s Mundra complex uses the coal-to-calcium-carbide route. Resilience: years.

Opportunity

Backward-integrated PVCAdani Mundra 1 MMTPA phase one (₹26,000 cr, 60%+ spent, commissioning FY28), RIL to 1.5 MMT, Chemplast’s 331 kt S-PVC — with ~3,000 kt of new capacity claimed for 2026-27. Import dependence is the clearest single number to move.

Risk

Phenol-acetone flipping from deficit to surplusAdperma’s 345 kt phenol and 215 kt acetone land in Q4 FY27 into a ~600 kt market, with Deepak Chem Tech adding 300 kt by FY28. The import-substitution trade risks turning into a domestic price war within four quarters. Resilience: months.

Opportunity

Downstream integrationValue migrates from the molecule to the derivative: Deepak already consumes 60–65% of its acetone captively in IPA, MIBK and MIBC, and Adperma is evaluating downstream phenol-acetone projects. The derivative layer is where the new capacity has to go.

Risk

Methanol & acetic-acid importsOver 3 MMT of methanol and ~1.2 MMT of acetic acid are imported, with Iran alone supplying ~52% of methanol import value, because domestic gas is not competitively priced. Resilience: months–years.

Opportunity

Coal-to-methanol & park-based capacityCoal-based methanol and new solvent capacity target the leak; the BHAVYA parks (₹3,030 cr, proposals due 30-Nov-2026) fund exactly the common utilities — steam, solvent recovery, effluent treatment — that make small solvent plants viable.

Risk

Fluorspar → HFChina-concentrated feedstock feeds every fluorochemical and refrigerant; non-integrated users are exposed to both price and export policy. Resilience: months–years.

Opportunity

Integrated fluorochemistryCaptive HF is the moat — SRF, Gujarat Fluorochemicals and Navin Fluorine hold it and export from it. SRF’s fluorochemicals delivered robust refrigerant volumes with positive pricing momentum in Q2 FY27.

Risk

Chinese overcapacityBeijing’s anti-involution campaign upgrades and prices up rather than closing: a 2028 efficiency deadline for sub-scale naphtha crackers, with 6–7 MMt/y of ethylene still being added. The 2026 price relief came from freight, war-related dislocation and trade remedies, not from a supply cut. Resilience: structural.

Opportunity

Trade-remedy and dislocation windowThe S-PVC MIP and recommended CVD, the phenol anti-dumping case and the Madras High Court’s provisional assessment of paste-PVC imports give domestic capacity a protected window — which is exactly when Adperma, Mundra and Paradip need to commission.

Deepest hidden dependency: the feedstock and the intermediate middle. Crude, naphtha and gas are imported, and the intermediate layer (methanol, acetic acid, EDC/VCM, styrene, acrylonitrile, catalysts, licensed technology) is either imported or thinly domestic — and the capital to close it sits with a handful of diversified majors. The two biggest new intermediate assets, Adperma’s phenol train and Mundra’s PVC, sit in an unlisted subsidiary and a conglomerate. India’s investable edge is at the two ends: integrated upstream majors and export-led specialty chemistry.
06 Value pools & cost waterfall where value is captured · and which pool leaks to imports
Where value is captured
  • Integrated upstream captures the feedstock-to-polymer spread — capital-heavy, cyclical, oligopolistic. Q2 FY27 showed the leverage: higher crude, ethylene and freight lifted PVC realisations even as volumes stayed soft.
  • Phenol / acetone is the 2026 re-rating: record phenolics EBIT at Deepak on import dislocation, freight and duty protection — and the pool most exposed to the 345 kt Adperma train landing in Q4 FY27.
  • Specialty & fine chemistry is the durable, export-led pool and the China+1 winner (SRF, Navin, Vinati, Aarti, Deepak).
  • PVC is commodity and remedy-protected — the most import-exposed polymer and still loss-making at PAT for Chemplast in Q2 FY27.
  • Solvents are thin-margin and import-led: the largest value leak. Catalysts and licensed technology are the highest-margin leak of all.
Who captures it (India-listed)
  • Reliance Industries NSE: RELIANCE — the integrated O2C chain: olefins, aromatics, polymers, polyester
  • Deepak Nitrite NSE: DEEPAKNTR — largest listed phenol, acetone and IPA producer; the import-substitution core
  • SRF NSE: SRF — fluorochemistry, agro/pharma intermediates, refrigerants
  • Navin Fluorine NSE: NAVINFLUOR — the fastest-growing specialty and CDMO pool in the quarter
  • Epigral NSE: EPIGRAL — chlor-alkali derivatives and India’s largest CPVC resin capacity
  • Chemplast Sanmar NSE: CHEMPLASTS — largest paste-PVC producer; still loss-making in commodity S-PVC
  • Linde India NSE: LINDEINDIA — the only listed industrial-gases pure-play feeding every subsystem
Pools that leak to imports
  • Methanol, acetic acid, acetone, MEK, acetonitrile and DMF — the solvent layer. Acetone’s leak narrows as Adperma adds 215 kt.
  • Styrene, acrylonitrile, MDI/TDI and polycarbonate — no domestic capacity; no listed pure-play.
  • Polymerisation and reforming catalysts, and cracker / reformer process technology — the highest value-add India does not hold.
  • PVC resin — ~3.0 MMT a year, the single largest polymer leak, and the one with a funded domestic answer.
