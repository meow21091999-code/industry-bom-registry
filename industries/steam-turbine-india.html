05Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunitydual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

Large rotor forgings are importedThe HP/IP rotor forging (CrMoV, 9–12% Cr and the Ni-based AUSC rotor) is dominated by Japan Steel Works, Doosan and China First Heavy. India’s own heavy-forging base is real but not yet a frontier substitute. Resilience: years.

Opportunity

Scale the domestic rotor-forging chainLTSHF and Bharat Forge’s 16,000-t press plus MIDHANI’s superalloys are the platform; supercritical and fleet-mode demand justifies qualifying an indigenous large-rotor line.

Risk

AUSC superalloy not yet commercialIndia’s AUSC programme (310 bar / 710°C, Alloy 617(M) rotors, Alloy 625 castings) has finished R&D but not reached commercial deployment; large Ni-alloy forgings are globally immature. Resilience: years.

Opportunity

AUSC materials & dissimilar-weld rotorsAn 800 MW AUSC demo with indigenous Ni-alloy forging and dissimilar-metal-weld capability lifts efficiency and opens an export material market.

Risk

Hot-stage blading is a superalloy itemThe highest-temperature blade rows need Ni-based superalloy, an emerging capability in India; the rest of the blading is domestic. Resilience: months–years.

Opportunity

Blade superalloy qualificationMIDHANI superalloy mill forms plus domestic blade shops could close the hot-stage blade gap.

Risk

Heavy fabrication machinery importedThe forging press, heavy multi-axis CNC, rotor-balancing and overspeed rigs and heat-treatment furnaces are foreign-built. Resilience: months–years.

Opportunity

Heavy machine-tool localisationA domestic heavy-machine-tool and balancing-rig base would de-risk the whole turbine chain.

Risk

Skilled tradesPrecision blading machinists, rotor-balancing and NDT engineers and turbine-commissioning engineers bind tighter than materials.

Opportunity

Incumbent training moatsBHEL, L&T and Siemens Energy India qualification pipelines are a durable barrier to entry.

Deepest hidden dependency: India machines and assembles the turbine well, but the two highest-value inputs — the large rotor forging and the balancing/heavy-machining equipment — are imported. The fabrication is investable; the frontier material is not.
