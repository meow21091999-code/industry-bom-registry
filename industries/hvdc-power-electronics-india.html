05 Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunity dual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

Converter valves & control codeA 3-player global oligopoly (Hitachi / GE / Siemens) owns the valve and the proprietary control software; Indian assembly is not sovereignty. Resilience: years.

Opportunity

Domestic valve linesBHEL commissioned India's first indigenous ±800 kV converter station; GE Vernova is building an LCC/VSC valve line in Chennai (early-2027); local-content mandate 30% → 60% by Mar-2035.

Risk

Power semiconductorsNo Indian fab for high-power thyristors / IGBT / SiC; ~60–65% of converter silicon imported; a Chinese export control would bite directly.

Opportunity

OSAT today, fab tomorrowCG Power's CG Semi OSAT (Sanand, ₹7,600 cr) is live; the India Semiconductor Mission is the route to a domestic power-device line.

Risk

Converter transformersCustom-built, individually tested, 18–36 month lead times; only a handful of Indian plants are qualified for HVDC-grade units.

Opportunity

A widening Indian clubTARIL became the first Indian-origin private maker to win an HVDC converter-transformer order (Dec-2025); BHEL, Hitachi and CG hold capability.

Risk

HVDC cables (XLPE DC)The DC-rated XLPE insulation compound and the joints/terminations have no domestic production; heavy import reliance.

Opportunity

Greenfield cable plantsSterlite Electric (Vadodara, up to 400–550 kV HVDC/HVAC), KEI (full-spectrum HVDC by FY27) and Polycab (EHV by 2027) are building the domestic base.

Risk

Skilled manpower & test capacityHVDC commissioning engineers and HV type-test bays are named binding constraints; capacity can be bought, people and test slots cannot.

Opportunity

Qualification moatScarce, qualification-gated roles and NABL/CPRI test slots carry durable pricing power.

Deepest hidden import dependency: the machine tools, vacuum/oil-processing plants and HV test bays used to build HVDC converter transformers and valves themselves — procurement lead times of 2–4 years for high-end manufacturing systems, and effectively the true gate on how fast India can localise.
