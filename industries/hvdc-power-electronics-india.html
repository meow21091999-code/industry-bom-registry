Snapshot

HVDC converter stations and grid power electronics — the AC↔DC interface that moves bulk renewable power long-distance. India's HVDC base is ~19,375 ckm / 33,500 MW, with a 32.3 GW HVDC pipeline in the NEP (14.5 GW under implementation) and +15,432 ckm / +32,250 MW planned FY28–32. Converter-station content is ~100% imported.

Process & equipment
  • Converter transformers are custom-built and individually type-tested (18–36 month lead); valves use high-power thyristors/IGBTs, increasingly SiC; control & protection is proprietary software.
  • HVDC cables need DC-rated XLPE compound and pre-moulded joints — neither made in India.
Bottlenecks
  • Converter valves & control code — a 3-player global oligopoly.
  • Power semiconductors — no Indian high-power fab; SiC localisation just starting.
  • Converter transformers — 18–36 month leads.
  • EHV bushings/insulators — 2–3 global suppliers (Chinese import relief to Jul-2028).
  • XLPE-DC cables — no domestic compound; commissioning engineers and HV test bays bind.
Value pools
  • Converter stations — BHEL+Hitachi executing Khavda–Nagpur and Bhadla–Fatehpur; GE Vernova won Barmer-II LCC terminals (Sep-2026).
  • Valves — Hitachi Chennai + GE Vernova Chennai line (2027).
  • Converter transformers — TARIL first Indian-origin private order (Dec-2025).
  • Cables — Sterlite, KEI, Polycab building; power-device OSAT (CG Semi live).
Watch items
  • MoP phased local-content order (30-Apr-2026).
  • Chinese component import relief (Jul-2026).
  • Khavda–Nagpur poles (Nov-2028 / May-2029).
  • SiC MOSFET indigenisation drive (Sep-2026).
Coverage gaps
  • No domestic high-power semiconductor fab; XLPE-DC compound and accessories are foreign.

05 Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunity dual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

Converter valves & control codeA 3-player global oligopoly (Hitachi / GE / Siemens) owns the valve and the proprietary control software; Indian assembly is not sovereignty. Resilience: years.

Opportunity

Domestic valve linesBHEL supplies thyristor valves from Bengaluru for Khavda–Nagpur and Bhadla–Fatehpur; Hitachi Energy assembles HVDC valves in Chennai and is adding a line at Chengalpattu (₹1,000 cr, Jul-2026); GE Vernova is building an LCC/VSC valve line in Chennai (early-2027). Local content 30% → 60% by FY35.

Risk

Power semiconductorsNo Indian fab for high-power thyristors / IGBT / SiC; ~60–65% of converter silicon imported; a Chinese export control would bite directly.

Opportunity

OSAT today, SiC fab nextCG Semi OSAT (Sanand) began commercial production Jul-2026; SiCSem's ₹2,067 cr SiC fab (Odisha) signed with ISM in May-2026; CDIL is expanding high-power discrete capacity; MeitY + MoP + POWERGRID launched an indigenous SiC-MOSFET drive for VSC-HVDC (Sep-2026, 36-month development).

Risk

Converter transformersCustom-built, individually tested, 18–36 month lead times; only a handful of Indian plants are qualified for HVDC-grade units. Global OEM order books are full to 2030.

Opportunity

A widening Indian clubTARIL became the first Indian-origin private maker to win an HVDC converter-transformer order (₹53.33 cr, Dec-2025) and is scaling 40,000 → 75,000 MVA; BHEL, Hitachi and CG hold capability.

Risk

HVDC cables (XLPE DC)The DC-rated XLPE insulation compound and the joints/terminations have no domestic production; heavy import reliance, and no domestic subsea cable capability.

Opportunity

Greenfield cable plantsSterlite Electric (Vadodara, up to 400–550 kV HVDC/HVAC), KEI (Sanand Phase-II EHV by Mar-2027; HVDC-ready, full spectrum by FY27) and Polycab (EHV by 2027) are building the domestic base.

Risk

EHV bushings, insulators & CRGOBushings have only 2–3 suppliers worldwide; CRGO electrical steel runs a ~30% national shortfall. These gate converter-transformer and switchgear output.

Opportunity

Import relief + localisationThe Centre granted a two-year exemption (Jul-2026) for select Chinese transformer-component suppliers (bushings, insulators) with India plants, to hold schedules; TARIL and others are backward-integrating CRGO processing.

Risk

Skilled manpower & test capacityHVDC commissioning engineers and HV type-test bays (CPRI-led) are named binding constraints; capacity can be bought, people and test slots cannot.

Opportunity

Qualification moatScarce, qualification-gated roles and NABL/CPRI test slots carry durable pricing power; Hitachi's GTIC and GE Vernova's Noida lab deepen the talent base.

Deepest hidden import dependency: the machine tools, vacuum/oil-processing plants and HV test bays used to build HVDC converter transformers and valves themselves — procurement lead times of 2–4 years for high-end manufacturing systems, and effectively the true gate on how fast India can localise.
06 Value pools & cost waterfall where value is captured · and which pool leaks to imports
Where value is captured
  • Converter stations — BHEL+Hitachi executing Khavda–Nagpur and Bhadla–Fatehpur; GE Vernova won Barmer-II LCC terminals (Sep-2026).
  • Valves — Hitachi Chennai assembly + new Chengalpattu line; GE Vernova Chennai line (early-2027).
  • Converter transformers — TARIL first Indian-origin private order (Dec-2025); capacity 40,000 → 75,000 MVA.
  • Cables — Sterlite, KEI and Polycab building; power-device OSAT (CG Semi, Sanand) live.
Who captures it (India-listed)
  • Hitachi Energy India NSE/BSE: POWERINDIA — Executed over half of India's HVDC links; co-supplying Khavda–Nagpur; Q1 FY27 order backlog ₹32,222 cr
  • GE Vernova T&D India NSE/BSE: GVT&D — ~23% of India operational HVDC; won Barmer-II 6 GW LCC terminals (Sep-2026); KPS III VSC (Dec-2025)
  • Siemens Energy India NSE/BSE: ENRIN — One of three qualified HVDC suppliers in India; first VSC link
  • BHEL NSE/BSE: BHEL — Commissioned India's first indigenous ±800 kV station; thyristor valves from Bengaluru
  • Transformers & Rectifiers (India) NSE/BSE: TARIL — First Indian-origin private HVDC converter-transformer order (₹53.3 cr, Dec-2025)
  • CG Power NSE/BSE: CGPOWER — Power transformers & reactors up to 765 kV; CG Semi OSAT live
Pools that leak to imports
  • No domestic high-power semiconductor fab; XLPE-DC compound and accessories are foreign; EHV bushings largely imported.
  • Bottleneck nodes: Converter Valves (Thyristor / IGBT / MMC), Power Semiconductors (Thyristor / IGBT / SiC), Converter Transformers & Reactors, Control & Protection Systems (MACH / SCADA), HVDC Cables (MI / XLPE DC), HVDC-grade XLPE DC Compound & Accessories.
