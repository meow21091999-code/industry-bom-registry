DDigestshort summary · full detail below
Snapshot
  • Defence electro-optics & thermal imaging (EO/IR) is the sensing layer of the modern battlefield: hand-held thermal imagers, weapon and tank sights, naval EO/IRST, airborne/UAV payloads, missile IIR seekers and counter-drone optics.
  • India's military EO/IR market is ~$1.19 bn (2026), heading to ~$1.78 bn by 2030 (10.4% CAGR); the wider defence-sensor market is ~$1–1.5 bn, ~80% imported. BEL (with BELOP) and DRDO (IRDE/CHESS/DEAL/SSPL) are the design-and-production spine; a private layer (Data Patterns, Paras, Ideaforge, Zen) is climbing.
  • The catch: the two deepest nodes — the IR detector/FPA and the germanium IR optics — are the most import-bound, so system value sits downstream of the chokepoint.
Process & equipment
  • Spine: detector material growth (MCT/InSb/microbolometer) → FPA fabrication & indium-bump hybridisation to a ROIC → dewar + cryocooler (IDDCA) → diamond-turned, coated IR optics → LRU/electronics assembly in cleanrooms → integration → blackbody/MTF/thermal-vacuum qualification → DGQA acceptance.
  • Key machinery: MBE/MOCVD reactors, wafer fabs, single-point diamond-turning lathes, magnetron-sputter/ion-beam coaters, vacuum dewars, flip-chip/wire bonders, cryocooler assembly, cleanrooms, blackbody + collimator + MTF benches and thermal-vacuum chambers.
  • Night vision runs a parallel spine: photocathode deposition → microchannel-plate and fibre-optic assembly → tube vacuum processing (BELOP, Pune).
Bottlenecks
  • IR detector / FPA fabrication — cooled MCT/InSb and uncooled microbolometer dies are ~100% imported; DRDO SSPL/SCL output is limited. Resilience: years.
  • Germanium IR optics — ~100% imported, China-controlled, price ~5× since 2023. Resilience: years (chalcogenide substitution is the emerging escape).
  • Cryocoolers (Stirling/JT) — imported from Israel/Europe. Resilience: years.
  • ROIC/readout silicon and high-speed FPGA/DSP — imported. Resilience: months–years.
  • Test & calibration gear (blackbodies, collimators, MTF benches, diamond-turning machines) — ~90–100% imported. Resilience: years.
Value pools
  • Prime integration and system engineering is the largest domestic pool and is captured by BEL (listed) as DRDO's productionisation partner; night-vision tubes add a near-monopoly pool via BELOP.
  • IR optics and opto-mechanical assembly is a climbing private pool (Paras Defence) — and the node most exposed to the germanium chokepoint.
  • Readout/processing electronics and software/AI fusion is the highest-margin, fastest-appreciating pool (Data Patterns, BEL, Zen).
  • The pools that leak to imports are the deepest: detector/FPA dies, germanium and chalcogenide optical blanks, cryocoolers, ROIC silicon and test gear.
  • Localisation opportunity sits at the detector/fab, IR-optics-material and cryocooler layers — where no listed pure-play exists today.
Market & growth
  • India military EO/IR: $1.04 bn (2025) → $1.19 bn (2026) → $1.78 bn (2030); headline 5-yr CAGR 10.4% (2026–2030, source) or ~11.4% (2025–2030, [CALCULATED]). Value (nominal); volume [UNAVAILABLE]. [REPORTED-T2]
  • Last-year growth 2025→2026: +14.4% [CALCULATED]. 3-yr CAGR: [UNAVAILABLE] — the sourced series carries no 2023 base.
  • Defence-sensor market (broader scope): ~$1–1.5 bn today → $3.5–4 bn by 2030. [REPORTED-T2, different scope — not additive]
  • Growth is value/mix-led (more UAV payloads, vehicle awareness, counter-drone, seekers) rather than unit-count-led; India captures ~30% of sensor value domestically today, targeted toward ~$1 bn by 2030.
  • Rival-scope datapoint: the wider Indian surveillance market is put at ~$4.3 bn → >$15 bn by 2029 [REPORTED-T2, includes civil/commercial].

Coverage gaps

05Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunitydual-readable
Risk

IR detector & focal-plane arrayThe FPA — cooled MCT/InSb or an uncooled microbolometer die — is the single highest-value part of any EO/IR system and is ~100% imported; DRDO SSPL and SCL output is lab-scale. Resilience: years.

Opportunity

Indigenous microbolometer FPA fabAn iDEX challenge already calls for indigenous microbolometer FPAs and dewar-detector-cooler assemblies; a domestic FPA line unlocks every sight, payload and seeker at once. No listed pure-play yet.

Risk

Germanium IR opticsGermanium is the standard LWIR lens material; India is ~100% import-dependent, China controls supply, and the price has ~5×'d since 2023 with >40-week lead times. Resilience: years.

Opportunity

Germanium-free & chalcogenide opticsChalcogenide glass replaces germanium in LWIR lenses and is easier to make domestically — EON Space Labs' germanium-free payload (2026) is the template; NCMM backing is the catalyst. Capturable by optics houses (Paras).

Risk

Cryocoolers & ROIC siliconStirling/JT micro-coolers and the ROIC readout silicon behind cooled detectors are imported, gating every high-performance (cooled) system. Resilience: years.

Opportunity

Cooler & readout localisationA domestic micro-cooler and ROIC line would lift the cooled-detector ceiling for tanks, naval EO/IRST and seekers — high entry barriers, captive demand, no incumbent.

Risk

Test, calibration & optical-fab equipmentBlackbodies, collimators, MTF/MRTD benches, thermal-vacuum chambers and single-point diamond-turning lathes are ~90–100% imported — the deepest hidden dependency in a 'localised' product. Resilience: years.

Opportunity

Metrology & optical-fab toolingEvery EO/IR line needs this gear; a greenfield Indian optical-fabrication and EO/IR-metrology play has no incumbent. High barriers, but it is the 'mother' dependency of the whole chain.

Risk

Export controls (SCOMET / Wassenaar)SCOMET and Wassenaar end-use controls limit export of high-resolution thermal and multispectral EO/IR gear, constraining Indian OEMs' export growth even as domestic capability rises. Resilience: months–years (policy).

Opportunity

Export-compliance & certification playA unified EO/IR test/qualification framework and export-compliance capability would let Indian OEMs convert domestic wins into exports — Tonbo already earns ~65% of revenue abroad.

06Value pools & cost waterfallwhere value is captured · and which pool leaks to imports
Where value is captured
  • Prime integration & system engineering is the largest domestic pool — captured by BEL as DRDO's productionisation partner.
  • Night-vision image-intensifier tubes are a near-monopoly pool (BELOP).
  • IR optics / opto-mechanical assembly is a climbing private pool (Paras) and the node most exposed to germanium.
  • Readout/processing electronics & software/AI fusion is the highest-margin pool (Data Patterns, BEL, Zen).
Who captures it (India-listed)
  • Prime & tubes: BEL (with BELOP).
  • IR optics: Paras Defence.
  • Processing & software: Data Patterns, Centum, Apollo Micro.
  • Payload/platform & counter-drone: Ideaforge, Zen, BDL, HAL, L&T.
  • Unlisted but decisive: DRDO (IRDE/CHESS/DEAL/SSPL), Tonbo Imaging, EON Space Labs, Tempsens/Pyrosens.
Pools that leak to imports
  • IR detector / FPA dies — ~100% imported.
  • Germanium IR-optical blanks — ~100% imported.
  • Cryocoolers and ROIC silicon — imported.
  • Test & calibration gear and diamond-turning machines — ~90–100% imported.
