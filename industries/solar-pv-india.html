05 Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunity dual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

Solar cells (ALMM-II live)ALMM List-II for cells in force since 1-Jun-2026; ~one-third of 140 SME module makers halted; 6–8 month waits for domestic cells; domestic-cell modules cost ~3–4¢/W more. Resilience: months–years.

Opportunity

Cell capacity rampThe single biggest listed value pool — Premier, Websol, Waaree, Emmvee, plus Reliance's integrated entry.

Risk

Wafers & polysilicon~100% polysilicon and >90% wafer import; China curbs equipment and wafer technology exports. Resilience: years / structural.

Opportunity

ALMM-III (2028) + polysilicon PLIFirst-mover scarcity premium — Tata Power (10 GW plan), Reliance, Waaree, Indosol.

Risk

Cell-line machinery>90% of cell-line capex is foreign; turnkey lines supplied mainly by Chinese OEMs now under export pressure. Resilience: years.

Opportunity

Domestic equipment & consumablesAn unlisted white space — screen printers, PECVD, pastes, ribbon, screens.

Risk

Solar glass & ancillaries~70% of glass demand imported; anti-dumping duties on China/Vietnam glass; encapsulant, backsheet and frames largely imported.

Opportunity

Localisation waveBorosil expansion; Vishakha Renewables IPO (Adani-backed); captive integration by module OEMs.

Risk

InvertersChinese suppliers (Sungrow, Sineng, TBEA) dominate; no Indian pure-play listed. Data-sovereignty and BIS/BEE norms tightening.

Opportunity

Domestic power electronicsWaaree Power (4.15 GW string inverters); a listed entry point remains the gap.

Risk

Skilled manpowerCell and wafer process know-how is scarce; capacity can be bought, process engineers cannot.

Opportunity

Training + JV routeSlowest to fix, but qualification-gated roles carry durable scarcity value.

Deepest hidden import dependency: the turnkey cell-line and polysilicon/wafer equipment — and the technology licence behind it. China added solar manufacturing equipment and wafer technology to its export-control catalogue and is restricting equipment sales and technical support to India, which is the real reason domestic cell plants ramp slower than policy expects.
