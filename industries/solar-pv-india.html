Snapshot

India's solar PV chain runs polysilicon → wafer → cell → module → BoM → EPC/developer. Installed solar reached 171 GW by 30-Sep-2026 (+44.6 GW in FY26). India has localised module and cell assembly but not wafers or polysilicon; ALMM module capacity is ~213 GW against ALMM-II cell capacity of 37.8 GW (~16–18 GW effective).

Process & equipment
  • Module assembly — stringing, lay-up, lamination, framing, flash/EL test — is largely domestic.
  • Cell lines run texturing, diffusion, passivation, metallisation and firing on imported turnkey equipment, mostly Chinese OEMs.
  • Wafering and polysilicon (CVD / float-zone) are effectively absent; a dedicated polysilicon PLI/VGF scheme is being prepared.
Bottlenecks
  • Cells — ALMM List-II live since 1-Jun-2026; 6–8 month waits; ~one-third of SME module makers halted.
  • Wafers & polysilicon — ~100% and >90% imported; China curbs equipment and wafer technology.
  • Cell-line machinery — >90% foreign. Solar glass ~70% imported; inverters Chinese-led (Sungrow ~26%).
Value pools
  • Cell manufacturing is the largest listed pool — Premier, Waaree, Websol, Emmvee, Reliance.
  • Module assembly is competitive and thin-margin.
  • Polysilicon/wafer is the open prize under ALMM-III (1-Jun-2028) and the polysilicon VGF.
  • The 25-yr EPC/developer annuity sits with Adani Green, NTPC Green, Tata Power.
Watch items
  • ALMM List-II in force 1-Jun-2026; net-metering/open-access exemption to 31-Dec-2026; ALMM-III (wafers) mandate 1-Jun-2028.
  • PLI — ₹24,000 cr, none disbursed; polysilicon VGF mooted.
  • US AD/CVD ~123%/126% on Indian cells/modules — ITC final 19-Oct-2026.
Coverage gaps
  • Polysilicon and wafering have no listed Indian pure-play; cell-line equipment, silver paste and encapsulant/frame/backsheet makers are foreign, unlisted or pre-IPO (Vishakha).

05 Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunity dual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

Solar cells (ALMM-II live)ALMM List-II for cells in force since 1-Jun-2026; enlisted capacity 37.8 GW (12th rev, 8-Oct-2026) but only ~16–18 GW effective; ~one-third of ~140 SME module makers halted; 6–8 month waits; domestic-cell modules cost ~3–4¢/W more. Resilience: months–years.

Opportunity

Cell capacity rampThe single biggest listed value pool — Premier (3.6→10.6 GW), Waaree (5.4 GW), Websol, Emmvee, plus Reliance's integrated entry.

Risk

Wafers & polysilicon~100% polysilicon and >90% wafer import; China curbs equipment and wafer technology exports. India wafer capacity ~2 GW. Resilience: years / structural.

Opportunity

ALMM-III (1-Jun-2028) + polysilicon VGFFirst-mover scarcity premium — Waaree (10 GW ingot-wafer Nagpur), Tata Power (10 GW plan), Reliance, Indosol; a polysilicon VGF of ₹7,000–8,000 cr (target 35–40 GW) is under consideration.

Risk

Cell-line machinery>90% of cell-line capex is foreign; turnkey lines supplied mainly by Chinese OEMs now under export pressure. Resilience: years.

Opportunity

Domestic equipment & consumablesAn unlisted white space — screen printers, PECVD, pastes, ribbon, screens.

Risk

Solar glass & ancillaries~70% of glass demand imported; domestic ~2,600 TPD vs ~11,000 TPD demand; anti-dumping duties on China/Vietnam and CVD on Malaysia glass; encapsulant, backsheet and frames largely imported.

Opportunity

Localisation waveBorosil expansion to 1,600 TPD (₹1,100 cr, Mar-2027); Vishakha Renewables DRHP (₹1,250 cr, Adani-backed) for glass/frames/encapsulant/backsheet.

Risk

InvertersChinese suppliers (Sungrow ~26%, Sineng, TBEA, Hopewind) dominate; no Indian pure-play listed. Data-sovereignty and BIS/BEE norms tightening.

Opportunity

Domestic power electronicsWaaree Power (4.15 GW string inverters, Oct-2026); a listed entry point remains the gap.

Risk

Export-market trade exposureUS imposed preliminary CVD ~126% and AD ~123% on Indian cells/modules in 2026 (final ITC injury ruling due 19-Oct-2026); China filed a WTO challenge to India's tariff regime and PLI in May 2026. Resilience: years / structural.

Opportunity

China+1 / non-Chinese supplyUS and EU buyers seeking non-Chinese wafers and modules; Indian upstream (wafer/poly) could export trade-compliant material — Indosol is targeting US cell lines.

Risk

Skilled manpowerCell and wafer process know-how is scarce; capacity can be bought, process engineers cannot.

Opportunity

Training + JV routeSlowest to fix, but qualification-gated roles carry durable scarcity value.

Deepest hidden import dependency: the turnkey cell-line and polysilicon/wafer equipment — and the technology licence behind it. China added solar manufacturing equipment and wafer technology to its export-control catalogue (from 2023) and, per Reuters (Apr-2026) and industry reporting (Jul-2026), is restricting equipment sales and technical support to India — the real reason domestic cell plants ramp slower than policy expects.
06 Value pools & cost waterfall where value is captured · and which pool leaks to imports
Where value is captured
  • Cell manufacturing is the largest listed pool — Premier, Waaree, Websol, Emmvee, Reliance.
  • Module assembly is competitive and thin-margin, though FY26 margins were strong (Waaree EBITDA 22.3%).
  • Polysilicon/wafer is the open prize under ALMM-III (1-Jun-2028) and the polysilicon VGF.
  • The 25-yr EPC/developer annuity sits with Adani Green, NTPC Green, Tata Power.
Who captures it (India-listed)
  • Premier Energies NSE: PREMIERENE — Integrated cell+module; FY26 revenue ₹7,824 cr, PAT ₹1,510 cr; 11.1 GW module / 3.6 GW cell
  • Emmvee Photovoltaic Power NSE: EMMVEE — TOPCon cell+module; FY26 revenue ₹5,050 cr, PAT ₹1,082 cr
  • Vikram Solar NSE: VIKRAMSOLR — Module + EPC pure-play; FY26 revenue ₹4,861 cr, PAT ₹470 cr
  • Saatvik Green Energy NSE: SAATVIK — Module pure-play; FY26 revenue ₹4,548 cr, PAT ₹357 cr
  • Websol Energy System NSE: WEBELSOLAR — Integrated cell+module, Falta SEZ, West Bengal
  • Insolation Energy NSE: INSOLATION — Jaipur-based module maker
  • Alpex Solar NSE: ALPEXSOLAR — Module pure-play
Pools that leak to imports
  • Polysilicon and wafering have no listed Indian pure-play; cell-line equipment and paste makers are foreign or unlisted.
  • Bottleneck nodes: Module Assembly, Solar Cell Fabrication, Ingots & Wafers, Polysilicon, Solar Glass, Encapsulant (EVA / POE), Silver Paste & Ribbon, Inverters, Cell / Module Production Equipment.
