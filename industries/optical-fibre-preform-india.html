Snapshot

Optical fibre preform — the ultra-pure silica glass rod drawn into fibre. It is the deepest upstream layer of the optical-fibre chain and holds roughly 60–70% of a finished cable's value. Scope: India, NSE/BSE. Re-analysed 2026-10-21 (delta pass over the 2026-10-08 entry).

Process & equipment
  • Deposition (MCVD/OVD/VAD/PCVD) builds doped silica layers into a rod; glassworking, lathes and draw towers are the key (largely imported) equipment.
  • Feedstock is high-purity silica/SiCl₄ with GeCl₄ core doping; cleanroom-grade glassworking is required.
  • STL now runs a green-hydrogen preform furnace at Aurangabad (Project Photon Leap, >200 TPA H₂), cutting plant emissions ~30%.
Bottlenecks
  • Preform import dependence (~65–75%); resilience: years, shortening as STL/HFCL/Finolex build.
  • Germanium / GeCl₄ export controls — China >60% of supply (>90% of refining); licence regime since Aug-2023; the ban to the US/Japan is suspended only through Nov-2026. Prices ~9–10× 2023.
  • Preform price spike: ~$55/kg (mid-2025) → $120–140/kg (mid-2026) → HFCL cites ~$300/kg in Q2 FY27; the global supply deficit is expected to persist to ~2028.
  • Deposition technology/equipment concentrated in 6–10 firms; no listed Indian exposure.
Value pools
  • Preform captures ~60–70% of the chain's value — largely leaked to imports, now being pulled home (STL ₹3,000 cr, HFCL ₹1,800 cr, Finolex).
  • STL captures it domestically as the only integrated player; HFCL becomes the second by Oct-2028, Finolex the third.
  • Import-substitution prize ~$300–400 mn/yr; India fibre exports surged +158% YoY to $483 mn in Apr–Aug FY27.
  • Deposition equipment is a hidden second import layer.
Market & growth
  • India preform market (scope: preform supplied to domestic drawers, landed cost): ~$320–380 mn (2026) → $850–1,050 mn (2035), CAGR ~12–15% [REPORTED-T3, IndexBox, May-2026]. Value (nominal); volume 55–65 mn fkm-equiv → 140–170 mn fkm.
  • Last-year: +14–16% YoY (2026 vs 2025) [REPORTED-T3]. 3-year horizon not separately reported [UNAVAILABLE].
  • Exports: India optical-fibre/bundle/cable exports +158% YoY to $483 mn in Apr–Aug FY27 (US >half at $266.7 mn) [REPORTED-T1, Ministry of Commerce, Oct-2026].
  • [CONFLICTING DATA] on other scopes: OFC & accessories $4.8 bn 2026→$12.99 bn 2035 (11.7%, MarkWide); India fibre-optics $323.9 mn 2024→$866.1 mn 2029 (21.7%, MarketsandMarkets); India OFC ~$550 mn 2025→~$1 bn 2031 (~11%, tele.net). Growth is demand-led (BharatNet Phase III, data centres, FTTH), with price/mix positive as data-centre fibre (~$25–27/km) runs above telco D-fibre (~$15/km).
Watch items
  • Germanium export ban to the US is suspended only to 27-Nov-2026 (MOFCOM 2025 No. 72); the 28-Sep-2026 Kuala Lumpur extension to 10-Jan-2027 does not name that announcement — unconfirmed. The Chinese licence regime persists. [LIVE]
  • India's ADD on SMOF to Aug-2028; China extended its own ADD on Indian SMOF for 5 yrs to Aug-2031. [CYCLICAL]
  • HFCL preform facility commissioning Oct-2028; Finolex preform Phase-2; STL ₹3,000 cr capex FY27-29 (capacity +50%). [CYCLICAL]
  • BharatNet Phase III (₹65,000 cr) — STL and Polycab invoked force majeure on the fibre/router cost spike. [LIVE]
Coverage gaps

No listed pure-play for fibre-grade silica, GeCl₄/dopants, deposition equipment, specialty grades or ceramic ferrules — nearest: STL (integrated), HFCL/Finolex (building).

05 Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunity dual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

Preform import dependenceIndia imports ~65–75% of its preform; domestic capacity of ~18–22 mn fkm-equivalent meets only ~25–35% of national demand, leaving the whole fibre chain exposed to Chinese and Japanese supply.

Opportunity

Backward-integration waveSTL (₹3,000 cr, capacity +50% by FY29) + HFCL (₹1,800 cr, 600 MT by Oct-2028) + Finolex (100→200 MT) are pulling the most valuable layer onshore.

Risk

Germanium / GeCl₄ export controlsChina produces >60% of the world's GeCl₄ (>90% of germanium refining, S&P) and has run it under an export-licence regime since Aug-2023; the export ban to the US and Japan is suspended only through Nov-2026. Prices are ~9–10× their 2023 level and licences can take ~80 days.

Opportunity

Diversify & recycleGermany, the US and Kazakhstan have smaller germanium capacity; new ex-China projects (Canada, Korea, USA) target 126 t by 2030. Recycling of fibre-production scrap and India's Critical Minerals Mission are the medium-term substitutes.

Risk

Preform price spike & supply deficitPreform prices ran from ~$55/kg (mid-2025) to $120–140/kg (mid-2026), with HFCL citing ~$300/kg in Q2 FY27; the AI-data-centre fibre boom has outstripped an 18–24-month capacity-expansion cycle, and the deficit is expected to persist to ~2028.

Opportunity

Integrated players win the queueIn a glass shortage, whoever makes their own preform fills orders others cannot; STL's integration lifted its ex-China fibre share to 9% (Jun-2026) and it signed ~$2.9 bn of contracts in 2026.

Risk

Deposition technology concentrationOnly 6–10 firms worldwide can make telecom-grade preform at scale; deposition lathes/furnaces come from a handful of Japanese and European suppliers and global majors have been reluctant to transfer deposition technology to Indian counterparts.

Opportunity

A two- then three-player IndiaSTL owns the only Indian preform facility; HFCL's greenfield line (600 MT by Oct-2028) and Finolex's (200 MT) make India a multi-integrated-player market by 2028–29.

Risk

Value sits upstream, not in cableCable assembly is competitive and structurally lower-margin; most "fibre exposure" on NSE/BSE is really cable exposure, not preform exposure.

Opportunity

Own the choke pointWhoever controls preform self-sufficiency controls margin expansion through the current global fibre up-cycle; STL is the listed vehicle today, HFCL and Finolex the ones to watch.

Deepest hidden import dependency: germanium tetrachloride (GeCl₄) for core doping, and the preform-deposition equipment (MCVD/OVD/VAD lathes) used to build the preform itself — neither has a listed Indian pure-play.
06 Value pools & cost waterfall where value is captured · and which pool leaks to imports
Where value is captured
  • Preform captures ~60–70% of the optical-fibre chain's value — largely leaked to imports, now being pulled home (STL, HFCL, Finolex).
  • STL captures it domestically as the only integrated player; HFCL becomes the second by Oct-2028.
  • Import-substitution prize: ~$300–400 mn/yr of value.
  • Deposition equipment is a hidden second import layer.
Who captures it (India-listed)
  • Sterlite Technologies (STL) NSE/BSE: STLTECH — India's only fully backward-integrated preform maker (MCVD, Aurangabad + a plant in China); ₹3,000 cr capex FY27-29, capacity 50→75 mn fkm, ~70% utilisation [REPORTED]
  • HFCL NSE/BSE: HFCL — ₹1,800 cr total OF/OFC/preform capex; 600 MT/yr preform via HFCL Technologies by Oct-2028 [REPORTED]
  • Finolex Cables NSE/BSE: FINCABLES — preform Phase-1 (100 MT) commissioned Mar-2026, adding 100 MT to 200 MT; fibre 4→8 mn fkm [REPORTED]
  • Aksh Optifibre NSE/BSE: AKSHOPTFBR — draws fibre and makes FRP rods (a key OFC input) [REPORTED]
  • Polycab India NSE/BSE: POLYCAB — OFC vertical at Halol; preform in pilot phase [INFERENCE]
  • Birla Cable NSE/BSE: BIRLACABLE — OFC & copper telecom cables; being amalgamated into Vindhya Telelinks [REPORTED]
  • Vindhya Telelinks NSE/BSE: VINDHYATEL — OFC + speciality fibre cables (Rewa) [REPORTED]
Pools that leak to imports
  • No listed pure-play for fibre-grade silica, GeCl₄/dopants, deposition equipment, specialty grades or ceramic ferrules — nearest: STL (integrated), HFCL/Finolex (building).
  • Bottleneck nodes: Optical Fibre Preform (glass rod), High-Purity Silica / Glass Feedstock, Germanium & Dopant Chemicals (GeCl₄ / SiCl₄), Preform Deposition Equipment & Lathes, Preform Deposition (MCVD / OVD / VAD / PCVD), Cleanroom-Grade Manufacturing, Ceramic Ferrules (Zirconia).
