01Digestshort summary
Snapshot

India's basic inorganic chemical complex — the alkali and acid backbone that feeds alumina, glass, textiles, water treatment, steel and agriculture. A process industry, so the BOM is a recipe and energy balance, not an assembly tree. It spans chlor-alkali, soda ash, industrial gases, mineral acids and phosphate fertilisers.

Process & equipment
  • Chlor-alkali: brine purification → membrane-cell electrolysis; ~2,150–2,200 kWh/t; electricity is the dominant cost.
  • Soda ash: Solvay route, then compaction to dense grade; emerging electric calciners for low-CO₂ ash.
  • Gases: cryogenic air-separation units (ASU) and PSA; acids: DCDA sulphur-burn and smelter by-product plants.
Bottlenecks
  • Rock phosphate / phosphoric acid — imported; MONTHS–YEARS to substitute.
  • Potash — 100% imported; STRUCTURAL, no localisation path.
  • Chlorine imbalance — weak chlorine offtake can force caustic output cuts; MONTHS.
  • Soda-ash import surge — protected only by MIP / anti-dumping; MONTHS.
  • Turnkey equipment (ASU, cells, acid plants) — YEARS.
Value pools
  • Caustic soda is a thin-margin commodity; the value is migrating downstream into chlorine derivatives, where India is under-integrated.
  • Soda ash value is policy-set — MIP and anti-dumping determine realisations; solar glass is the new demand pool.
  • Industrial gases are contract-locked and the most attractive pool — but the bulk leaks to unlisted MNC JVs.
  • In phosphate fertilisers the pool is captured by the subsidy, not the market; importers absorb losses.
  • Smelter by-product acid is a genuine domestic-cost hedge against imported sulphur.
  • Localisation prize sits in rock-phosphate / phosphoric-acid capacity and in downstream chlorine integration.
Watch items
  • Soda-ash MIP ₹20,108/t extended to 31-Dec-2025; AD duties recommended; fresh safeguard-QR probe initiated 2026.
  • NBS subsidy rabi 2025-26: P ₹47.96/kg, S ₹2.87/kg.
  • Rock-phosphate price-linkage notification (IBM formula) — draft, gazette pending.
  • GHCL ₹6,500 cr greenfield and Tata Mithapur +350 kt dense soda-ash timetables.
Coverage gaps

Bulk industrial gases (INOX Air Products, Air Liquide India, Air Water India all unlisted), rock-phosphate mining, natural soda ash, RSPL/SOKA and the IFFCO cooperative, and the turnkey equipment layer have no NSE/BSE coverage.

03Value-chain ladder & supplier tiersfeedstock → end use
Raw material
Salt & brine (domestic), limestone (domestic), sulphur — imported rock phosphate — imported potash — imported natural gas.
Refining
Brine purification, elemental-sulphur burning (DCDA), and smelter SO₂ by-product capture from zinc / copper / lead smelting.
Basic inorganic
Caustic soda, chlorine, hydrogen, soda ash, H₂SO₄, H₃PO₄, HNO₃, O₂ / N₂ / Ar.
Derivative
PVC / CPVC, chloromethanes, chlorotoluenes, hydrogen peroxide, DAP / NPK / SSP, sodium bicarbonate.
End use
Alumina & aluminium, float & solar glass, textiles, soaps & detergents, pulp & paper, water treatment, steel, agrochemicals, pharma, food.
Supplier tiers
  • Tier-4 feedstock (salt, sulphur, rock phosphate, potash) → Tier-3 basic inorganic → Tier-2 derivative → Tier-1 integrated producer → end-use OEM.
  • Aftermarket: membrane replacement, spares, ZLD / effluent, and circularity (smelter acid, gypsum).

06Bottlenecks — risk & opportunitydual-readable
Risk
Rock phosphate / phosphoric acid import dependence

Almost all rock phosphate, phosphoric acid and ammonia are imported. DAP feedstock is hostage to Jordan / Morocco pricing, and DAP importers face losses the subsidy only partly covers.

Opportunity
Domestic PA capacity & upstream rock stakes

GSFC's Sikka phosphoric + sulphuric acid expansion, Coromandel's 45% stake in Senegal's Baobab Mining, and the rock-phosphate price-linkage notification are the localisation plays. Resilience class MONTHS–YEARS.

Risk
Potash — 100% import dependent

~2 MMT/yr sourced from Russia, Israel, Belarus and Jordan, with no domestic substitute. Resilience class STRUCTURAL.

Opportunity
None at the material layer

There is no localisation path for potash; the only levers are agronomic efficiency and long-term offtake contracts. This is a node to avoid, not to build.

Risk
Chlorine imbalance

Caustic and chlorine are co-produced ~1:0.89; weak chlorine offtake forces caustic output cuts. India is caustic-centric with low chlorine integration. Resilience class MONTHS.

Opportunity
Downstream chlorine integration

Grasim's captive chlorine use reached 53%; GACL commissioned India's largest chlorotoluenes plant; DCM Shriram is building a chlorine pipeline to Aarti. This is the single biggest value-migration in the complex.

Risk
Soda-ash import surge

China's ~10 MMT Inner Mongolia surplus and Turkish / US capacity additions have pushed imports to ~25% of demand, eroding domestic realisations. Resilience class MONTHS (AD / MIP protected).

Opportunity
Solar-glass demand & domestic capacity

India's solar-glass capacity scaling toward 40 GW needs ~400 kt extra soda ash. GHCL's ₹6,500 cr / 1.1 MMT greenfield and Tata's Mithapur +350 kt dense capture the import gap.
