Snapshot

A four-subsystem apparatus sector — ophthalmic/optical devices, diagnostic imaging, surgical instruments and patient-care/monitoring/implants. India localises assembly, disposables and lenses well but imports the core imaging hardware, advanced optics and medical-grade materials. ~65% of demand is imported; ~800 manufacturers, ~90% MSME.

Process & equipment
  • Assembly spine: precision machining, injection moulding, cleanroom build, sterilisation (EtO/steam/gamma), calibration.
  • Lens optics: casting/polymerisation, surfacing, spin & PVD coating, edging.
  • Imaging: gantry/detector/tube assembly, magnet winding & cryogenics, transducer fabrication.
  • Hidden import: the machines that make the components — tube-winding, detector litho, precision optics.
Bottlenecks
  • X-ray tubes & flat-panel detectors — imported (US/DE/JP); Varex localising at AMTZ. Resilience: months–years.
  • Superconducting MRI magnets & liquid-helium cryogenics — thin domestic base.
  • Advanced optics & CMOS image sensors (endoscopy, ophthalmic) — imported.
  • Medical-grade polymers & specialty alloys (PEEK, silicone, L605 CoCr, nitinol) — imported.
  • Certification lead time (CDSCO/BIS/AERB) & skilled manpower — domestic constraints.
Value pools
  • Disposables & infusion therapy — the deepest listed pool (Poly Medicure; also a large exporter).
  • Spectacle lenses & eyewear retail — GKB Ophthalmics (lens maker) and Lenskart (listed Nov-2025).
  • Interventional cardiology (stents) — Poly Medicure live; Sahajanand (pre-IPO) and Meril (private) lead volume.
  • Imaging & monitoring systems — mostly foreign-listed MNCs (GE, Siemens Healthineers, Philips) plus unlisted Indian OEMs.
  • Dialysis, orthopaedics and renal — Poly Medicure’s fastest-growing verticals.
  • Components & materials (tube, detector, magnet, optics) leak almost wholly to imports.
Watch items
  • All Class C/D devices under CDSCO licensing from 1-Oct-2023; Class A/B from 1-Oct-2022.
  • PLI medical devices runs FY2023-24 to FY2026-27 (expiry within ~1 year).
  • Varex 2nd AMTZ line (CsI + glass tubes) commissioned Jul-2025; ramp still early.
  • Sahajanand Medical Technologies IPO — SEBI nod Nov-2025, listing pending.
  • Opto Circuits under CIRP since Nov-2022 — distressed, not investable.
Coverage gaps
  • IOLs, ophthalmic diagnostics, endoscopy, ventilators, cath labs and robotic surgery have no listed Indian pure-play (private or foreign).
  • X-ray tubes/detectors, superconducting magnets and medical-grade materials have no NSE/BSE exposure at all.

05 Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunity dual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

X-ray tubes & flat-panel detectorsImported (USA ~55–66%, Germany, Japan); HHI concentration very high. Every Indian CT/DR/cath-lab OEM depends on them. Resilience: months–years.

Opportunity

AMTZ imaging-hardware chainVarex (USA) is building India’s first CsI-coating and medical-glass-tube lines at AMTZ; local tube + detector assembly would move the whole imaging value chain onshore.

Risk

Superconducting MRI magnets & cryogenicsLiquid-helium-cooled magnets and superconducting wire were a pure import; MRI is the highest-value single device in the market.

Opportunity

Domestic magnet productionAMTZ superconducting-magnet and cryogenics lines let three OEMs build MRI in India, reportedly halving scanner cost (~₹6 cr → ~₹3 cr).

Risk

Advanced optics & image sensorsEndoscope optics, ophthalmic optics and CMOS image sensors are imported (Olympus, Karl Storz, Sony). India assembles but does not make the optics. Resilience: months–years.

Opportunity

Indian optics challengersForus Health and Remidio (fundus/retinal imaging) and Endo-Med (endoscopy) prove Indian optics design; local lens & sensor sourcing is the next step.

Risk

Medical-grade materialsPEEK, silicone, medical PVC/PC, L605 cobalt-chromium, nitinol and piezo ceramics are imported; a device is only as local as its material.

Opportunity

Specialty compounding & alloysDomestic polymer compounding and CoCr/nitinol tube-making would unlock stents, implants and disposables upstream — a protected, high-margin niche.

Risk

Certification lead time & skilled manpowerCDSCO/BIS/AERB approvals and NABL-grade testing were a domestic bottleneck; biomedical engineers and regulatory-affairs talent are named binding constraints.

Opportunity

Test infrastructure & skillsAMTZ common test centres (EMC, biomaterial, gamma) and the IBSC skills programme shorten approval cycles — qualification-gated roles carry durable scarcity value.

Deepest hidden import dependency: not the assembled scanner but the components and the machinery that make them — X-ray tube inserts, detector arrays, piezo transducer ceramics, superconducting wire, precision optics and the tube-winding / detector-fab / precision-polishing equipment behind them. India’s localisation so far stops at assembly, lenses and disposables.
06 Value pools & cost waterfall where value is captured · and which pool leaks to imports
Where value is captured
  • Upstream materials & specialty components (imaging tube/detector, superconducting magnets, advanced optics, medical-grade polymers) carry the highest value per unit — and are the least indigenous.
  • Disposables & infusion therapy hold the largest absolute listed pool; India is globally competitive and export-led.
  • Interventional cardiology (stents, balloons, catheters) carries proprietary margin; India has scale, mostly private.
  • OEM assembly of imaging & monitoring is scale-driven and thin-margin for unlisted Indian OEMs; the brand margin sits with MNCs.
  • Aftermarket (service, spares, probes, consumables) is the most resilient pool, riding the installed base of scanners, monitors and dialysis machines.
Who captures it (India-listed)
  • Poly Medicure — broadest listed device content (infusion, renal, cardiology, critical care, ortho); FY25 net sales ₹1,619.8 cr.
  • GKB Ophthalmics — the listed lens-making play (semi-finished & Rx lenses).
  • Lenskart Solutions — vertically integrated eyewear retail + in-house lens making; ₹6,653 cr FY25 revenue.
  • Hemant Surgical Industries — the listed turnkey & distribution play (OT / ICU / dialysis / imaging).
  • Small-cap set — KMS Medisurgi, Centenial Surgical Suture, Raaj Medisafe, Avience, Nureca, Tarsons, Fabtech.
Pools that leak to imports
  • X-ray tubes & flat-panel detectors (USA / Germany / Japan).
  • Superconducting magnets & cryogenics (liquid helium).
  • Advanced optics & CMOS image sensors (endoscopy, ophthalmic).
  • Medical-grade polymers & specialty alloys (PEEK, silicone, L605 CoCr, nitinol, piezo ceramics).
  • Capital equipment (tube-winding, detector-fab, precision optics, cleanroom / sterilisation plant).
  • Collectively the bulk of India’s ~65% import dependence.
