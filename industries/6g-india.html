00 Digest short summary · full detail in the sections below
Snapshot

6G (IMT-2030) — the next generation of mobile networks: sub-THz radio, an AI-native core, integrated sensing and non-terrestrial access. India is pre-commercial: standards are in study phase and the first specs freeze in Dec-2028. Scope: India, NSE/BSE.

Process & equipment
  • Radios, AAUs and baseband are SMT-assembled and RF/OTA-tested; sub-THz and RIS/metasurface lines are lab- to pilot-stage.
  • Key equipment: SMT lines, OTA/anechoic chambers, sub-THz vector network analysers and signal generators, environmental chambers, and an RFIC/GaN cleanroom.
  • Silicon (RFIC/baseband SoC) and sub-THz test gear are the deepest imported inputs.
Bottlenecks
  • RAN & modem silicon — imported; no listed pure-play.
  • Sub-THz / THz devices and test equipment — no commercial domestic capability.
  • GaN/GaAs RF front-end — imports; Indian GaN only at pilot scale.
  • Spectrum timing — upper-6 GHz auction vs satellite coexistence; WRC-27 decides.
  • Standards/IP — India targets 10% of global 6G patents from a low base.
Value pools
  • Value concentrates upstream in silicon, RF front-end and sub-THz devices.
  • Indian value-add today is assembly, integration and software — thin margin.
  • The proven indigenous 5G stack (Tejas / C-DoT / TCS) is the export bridge into 6G.
  • Optical fibre and transport is the one node where India is already a global exporter.
  • Test & measurement is a near-total import leak with no listed Indian player.
Market & growth
  • 6G revenue is ~zero before 2030 — pre-commercial; forecasts begin at 2030.
  • Global 6G market: ~US$11.4 bn (2030) → ~US$110 bn (2036), ~46% CAGR [REPORTED-T2].
  • An earlier forecast put 2035 at US$68.7 bn on a 76.9% CAGR — wide dispersion [CONFLICTING DATA].
  • India 6G forecasts range ~35% to ~52% CAGR to 2035, all forward-looking [CONFLICTING DATA].
  • Growth is policy- and R&D-led now (₹271–542 cr TTDF; ₹1.0 lakh cr RDI), not revenue-led; volume CAGR [UNAVAILABLE].
Watch items
  • WRC-27 (Oct–Nov 2027) decides 6G spectrum bands under agenda item 1.7.
  • 3GPP Release 21: Stage-1 freeze Mar-2027; first 6G specs Dec-2028; code Mar-2029.
  • India upper-6 GHz (6425–7125 MHz) auction; 6725–7025 MHz targeted by 31 Dec 2030.
  • Commercial 6G chipsets expected before end-2029.
Coverage gaps

No listed Indian pure-play in 6G RAN silicon, sub-THz devices or test & measurement — nearest proxies (Tejas, Astra Microwave, BEL, MosChip) are indirect; foreign OEMs (Nokia, Ericsson, Samsung, Keysight, Rohde & Schwarz) fill the gap.

05 Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunity dual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

RAN & modem siliconRFICs, baseband SoCs and FPGAs are almost fully imported (Qualcomm, AMD/Xilinx, Marvell, Analog Devices); India has no listed RAN-silicon pure-play and no commercial fab.

Opportunity

Design-led manufacturing + OSATThe Design-Linked Incentive, MosChip/Saankhya design houses and the new OSAT build-out (CG Semi, Kaynes Semicon, Suchi) let India capture design and packaging value without a leading-edge fab.

Risk

Sub-THz devices & test equipmentThere is no commercial domestic supply of sub-THz front-ends or sub-THz test gear; capability sits in R&D (SAMEER, IITs). This is the least mature, most import-dependent node.

Opportunity

First-mover on the SAMEER THz testbedIndia's 6G THz testbed (SAMEER + IIT-M/G/P) already demonstrated a 6,400 Mbps link at 270 GHz; TTDF funding can turn a testbed lead into component capability.

Risk

Spectrum timing and coexistenceThe 6 GHz band is contested between licensed IMT and de-licensed Wi-Fi, and satellite incumbents constrain sub-THz use; WRC-27 (Oct-2027) decides the bands, delaying any operator capex decision.

Opportunity

A predictable national spectrum pipelineIndia's Spectrum Roadmap gives 10-year visibility (upper-6 GHz ~700 MHz identified; 400 MHz readied for auction). Predictability is itself the enabler of an indigenous 6G equipment market.

Risk

GaN/GaAs RF front-endPower amplifiers and MMICs are imported; indigenous GaN (AGNIT, GAETEC) is at pilot/low-volume scale and most semiconductor-grade materials and substrates are still imported.

Opportunity

Compound-semiconductor fabs under ISM 2.0GaN foundry and compound-semi capacity (Crystal Matrix, AGNIT's planned fab, GAETEC) can seed an indigenous RF front-end supply as 5G/6G base stations all use GaN PAs.

Risk

Standards & IP leadershipIndia contributes to 3GPP/ITU/oneM2M but holds a small share of 6G core patents; its 10%-by-2030 target implies a very steep climb from today's base.

Opportunity

A seat at the standard-setting tableThe Bharat 6G Alliance (90 members, 7 working groups) and the TDIP scheme fund standards participation; standards influence is the cheapest route to value capture in a pre-commercial market.

Risk

A zero-revenue window to ~2030No 6G revenue exists until roughly 2030, so any 6G exposure is a multi-year option, funded by 5G-Advanced and export revenue in the meantime; the TTDF outlay (₹271–542 cr) is small against the task.

Opportunity

Sell the 5G bridge, then the 6G upgradeThe proven indigenous 4G/5G stack (100,000+ BSNL sites) is already exportable to Africa, SE Asia and Latin America; the installed base annuitises into software upgrades as standards evolve toward 6G.

Deepest hidden import dependency: the RF/baseband silicon and the sub-THz test-and-measurement stack. These sit below the OEM assembly layer India has begun to localise, are almost entirely imported, and gate the ability to design, qualify and manufacture any 6G radio. No listed Indian company provides pure-play exposure to either.
06 Value pools & cost waterfall where value is captured · and which pool leaks to imports
Where value is captured
  • Value concentrates upstream in silicon, RF front-end and sub-THz devices.
  • Indian value-add today is assembly, integration and software — thin margin.
  • The proven indigenous 5G stack (Tejas / C-DoT / TCS) is the export bridge into 6G.
  • Optical fibre and transport is the one node where India is already a global exporter.
  • Test & measurement is a near-total import leak with no listed Indian player.
Who captures it (India-listed)
  • Tejas Networks NSE/BSE: TEJASNET — Indigenous 4G/5G RAN + optical; 6G CoE (RIS, ISAC, NTN) [REPORTED]
  • ITI Limited NSE/BSE: ITI — Contract-manufactured RAN, ODC and optical equipment [REPORTED]
  • HFCL NSE/BSE: HFCL — #1 OFC supplier; 5G RAN products; ~15 6G patents filed [REPORTED]
  • Astra Microwave NSE/BSE: ASTRAMICRO — RF/microwave T/R modules and MMICs (radar/space, 6G-adjacent) [REPORTED]
  • Bharat Electronics NSE/BSE: BEL — GAETEC GaN MMICs, radar/EW and integration [REPORTED]
  • Tata Consultancy Services NSE/BSE: TCS — Mission-mode lead of the BSNL indigenous stack; core software [REPORTED]
  • Reliance Industries (Jio) NSE/BSE: RELIANCE — In-house 5G stack and 6G spectrum/standards driver [REPORTED]
Pools that leak to imports
  • Silicon (RFIC / baseband SoC / FPGA) — no listed pure-play.
  • Sub-THz / THz devices — no commercial domestic supply.
  • Test & measurement (sub-THz VNA / OTA) — fully imported.
  • RF front-end / GaN MMICs — imported; Indian GaN pilot-stage only.
  • High-frequency PCB laminate — largely imported.
