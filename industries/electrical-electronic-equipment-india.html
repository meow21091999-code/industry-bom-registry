Snapshot

Two chains in one sector: heavy electrical (grid-linked, policy-driven) and electronic (export-led, mobile-anchored). Demand is de-risked by a decade-long ₹9.16 L cr T&D capex plan; supply-side value leaks to imports.

Process & equipment
  • Transformer spine: CRGO core cutting → coil winding → core-coil assembly → oil/vacuum impregnation → tank & bushing fitment → routine + impulse testing.
  • Cable spine: wire drawing → stranding → XLPE/PVC extrusion → armouring → HV/EHV type-testing.
  • Electronics spine: SMT (printer, pick-and-place, reflow) → AOI/X-ray → box-build → burn-in; semiconductor back-end is OSAT (assembly, test, package).
  • Core machinery (core-cutting, HV winding, CCV extrusion, SMT lines, OSAT handlers) is largely imported from Europe, Japan, Korea and China.
Bottlenecks
  • CRGO steel — ~90% imported; BIS-gated; live anti-dumping probe (Jun 2026). Resilience: months–years.
  • Power semiconductors — ~100% of valves imported; domestic OSAT only now starting. Resilience: years.
  • PCBs — ~90% of demand imported; laminates the upstream gap. Resilience: months–years.
  • Certified skills & HV test labs — bind tighter than materials.
  • Grid execution — right-of-way / forest-clearance delays run 3–18 months.
Value pools
  • Transformers & HV switchgear — strong order books, pricing power at 765 kV/HVDC; value stays with the domestic builder.
  • Cables & wires — largest pool; new conglomerate entrants compress the moat.
  • EMS/ODM — huge revenue, thin margin; value migrates up only via backward integration.
  • Smart meters — annuity-like AMISP cash flows; most visible domestic pool.
  • Leakage — CRGO, power semis, PCBs, passives and machinery sit offshore; ECMS/ISM exist to pull them onshore.
Watch items
  • CRGO anti-dumping probe (initiated 22 Jun 2026) — resets transformer input cost.
  • ECMS outlay raised to ₹40,000 cr (Budget 2026-27).
  • Semicon 2.0 (₹1,27,500 cr, Jul 2026) — equipment / materials / design-IP.
  • BEE star-label upgrade for distribution transformers (Jan 2025).
Coverage gaps
  • Passive components & specialty chemicals — no listed NSE/BSE pure-play; nearest proxies are EMS players building backward integration.
  • Insulators — thin listed coverage; the largest domestic maker is unlisted.

05 Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunity dual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

CRGO electrical steel~90% imported; BIS certification gates suppliers; a live anti-dumping probe (Jun 2026) could raise transformer costs. Resilience: months–years.

Opportunity

Domestic GOESJSW–JFE scaling 50 → 250 ktpa at Nashik and 62 → 100 ktpa at Vijayanagar (~350 ktpa by FY28) — a protected import-substitution franchise.

Risk

Power semiconductors (IGBT / SiC)~100% of valves and ~90% of wide-bandgap devices imported; no domestic fab. Resilience: years.

Opportunity

OSAT & SiCKaynes Semicon (Sanand) already shipping India-packed power modules; CG Power OSAT and RIR SiC under construction — the largest untapped pool (~US$140 bn global).

Risk

PCBs~90% of demand imported; laminates are the binding upstream gap; anti-dumping duty on ≤6-layer PCBs since H1 CY24. Resilience: months–years.

Opportunity

ECMS-backed PCB linesAmber, Syrma, Kaynes and Dixon are building multi-layer / HDI / flex capacity under ECMS with local-laminate incentives.

Risk

Certified skills & HV test labsHV winding, NDT and fab process engineers, plus impulse/short-circuit test capacity, bind tighter than materials.

Opportunity

Training & testing infrastructureSlowest to fix, so qualification-gated roles and certified labs carry durable scarcity value.

Risk

Grid executionRight-of-way, forest-clearance and land delays run 3–18 months, gating equipment offtake timing.

Opportunity

EPC & project managementMulti-year order books for T&D EPC players; execution capability is itself scarce.

Deepest hidden import dependency: the machinery that builds the equipment — CRGO core-cutting lines, HV winding machines, CCV/VCV cable extrusion lines, SMT lines and OSAT handlers. Procurement lead times run into years, and this gap sits below every other node in the tree.
06 Value pools & cost waterfall where value is captured · and which pool leaks to imports
Where value is captured
  • Transformers & HV switchgear — record order books; pricing power at 765 kV / HVDC.
  • Cables & wires — largest pool; margin decided by organised share and branding.
  • Smart meters — AMISP annuities give multi-year visibility.
  • EMS/ODM — revenue-rich, margin-poor; value migrates up via backward integration.
  • Machine tools and HV test capacity carry durable scarcity value.
Who captures it (India-listed)
  • Hitachi Energy India NSE/BSE: POWERINDIA — transformers, HVDC, FACTS; FY25 order book ~₹19,246 cr
  • CG Power NSE/BSE: CGPOWER — transformers, switchgear, motors, OSAT; FY25 revenue ~₹9,329 cr standalone
  • Transformers & Rectifiers NSE/BSE: TARIL — pure-play transformer maker
  • Polycab India NSE/BSE: POLYCAB — largest W&C maker; FY25 W&C revenue ~₹18,888 cr
  • Dixon Technologies NSE/BSE: DIXON — largest EMS; FY25 revenue ~₹38,860 cr
  • Genus Power NSE/BSE: GENUSPOWER — smart metering AMISP; order book ~₹28,750 cr
Pools that leak to imports
  • CRGO steel — ~90% imported; domestic ~10–12% of demand.
  • Power semiconductors — ~100% of valves; no domestic fab.
  • PCBs — ~90% imported; laminates the upstream gap.
  • Passive components, specialty chemicals, NdFeB magnets, machinery — no domestic scale.
