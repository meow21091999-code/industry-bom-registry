Snapshot

Bulk electricity transmission at 220 kV and above — lines, substations, HVDC, cables, EPC and the regulated-return developer layer. NEP 2022–32 capex is ₹9.16 L cr (~₹6.60 L cr on ISTS).

Process & equipment
  • Line stringing and tower fabrication are domestic; transformers and GIS are OEM-built to order.
  • HVDC valves and control software are a foreign oligopoly; core materials (CRGO, XLPE compound, power semiconductors) are imported.
  • Machine tools and HV test bays for building the equipment are the hidden import.
Bottlenecks
  • CRGO electrical steel — ~90% imported; live anti-dumping probe.
  • EHV transformer capacity — lead times up from 4–6 to 9–18 months.
  • HVDC converter equipment — few global suppliers; STATCOM semiconductors ~60–65% imported.
  • Skilled manpower named as a binding constraint.
Value pools
  • Transformer / switchgear OEMs — Hitachi Energy India, CG Power, GE Vernova T&D, BHEL.
  • HVDC — three qualified suppliers.
  • TBCB developers — ~₹2.22 L cr under execution.
  • Machine tools and test capacity carry durable scarcity value.
Watch items
  • TBCB pipeline — 82 projects (Jan 2026).
  • Transformer lead times 9–18 months.
  • CRGO anti-dumping probe; domestic GOES (JSW-JFE) scaling.
Coverage gaps
  • HVDC control software and power semiconductors have no domestic fab; CRGO domestic share is only ~10–12%.

05 Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunity dual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

CRGO electrical steel~90% imported; a live anti-dumping probe could raise transformer costs; BIS-licence lapses have cut supply. Resilience: months–years.

Opportunity

Domestic GOESJSW-JFE Nashik scaling to ~3,50,000 tpa by FY2028; a second line would be a protected import-substitution play.

Risk

EHV transformer capacityLead times 4–6 → 9–18 months; some DISCOM categories 12–18 months; Tamil Nadu ISTS delayed on transformer + GIS shortage.

Opportunity

Capacity build-out~315 GVA announced/under construction (~Rs 66 bn); incumbents hold pricing power near-term.

Risk

HVDC converter equipmentFew global suppliers, unable to run many large projects at once; Ladakh high-altitude scheme had no global precedent.

Opportunity

Localised HVDCThree qualified suppliers in India; domestic valve/IGBT lines being built (GE's $16 m India line).

Risk

STATCOM / power semiconductors~60–65% of core components (IGBT, SiC, HV capacitors) imported.

Opportunity

Integration today, upstream tomorrowBHEL/L&T assemble domestically; the upstream semiconductor gap is the open prize.

Risk

Skilled manpowerNamed as a binding constraint by PGCIL and state utilities — capacity can be bought, people cannot.

Opportunity

Training pipelinesSlowest to fix, but qualification-gated roles carry durable scarcity value.

Deepest hidden import dependency: the machine tools and vacuum/oil-processing plants used to build transformers themselves — procurement lead times of 2–4 years for high-end large-power-transformer manufacturing systems.
