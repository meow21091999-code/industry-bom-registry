DGDigest — at a glanceshort summary; full detail below
Snapshot
  • RDSS (2021) funds 20.33 crore meters — 19.79 crore consumer, 52.53 lakh DT and 2.05 lakh feeder meters — through AMISPs on TOTEX/DBFOOT annuities, with a sunset date now extended to 31-Mar-2028.
  • 5.73 crore were installed under the scheme at 30-Jun-2026 (7.24 crore across all schemes). Meter assembly is domestic; the metering IC, MCU, comms baseband and relays are imported, so the value still leaks to silicon.
Process & equipment
  • The meter is three boards — comms, main (MCU + display + power) and power — plus CT/shunt, latching relay, SMPS and enclosure, built on SMT lines with in-circuit and functional test.
  • Accuracy calibration and type testing run to IS 16444 / IS 15884 and DLMS/COSEM; RF-mesh gateways/DCUs aggregate 300+ meters each and HES/MDMS turns the data into billing determinants.
Bottlenecks
  • Metering silicon — ‘entirely imported’ (ADI, TI, NXP, Renesas), with design-in-India mandated from 1-Jun-2028. Resilience: months–years.
  • Imported input costs — the West Asia disruption and a weaker rupee compressed OEM margins in Q2 FY27; fixed-price contracts carry the risk.
  • Latching relays and PCB components — 100% India-made relay mandate from 1-Jun-2028; ~88% of PCB demand is still imported.
  • Tendering and execution — Tamil Nadu re-floated 1.77 crore meters in Oct-2026 after two cancelled rounds.
  • AMISP credit — direct-debit bucket-filling mitigates, but does not remove, discom payment risk.
Value pools
  • Metering silicon keeps the highest margin per meter and is still entirely foreign; the 2028 mandate is the entry ticket, not yet a domestic pool.
  • The AMISP annuity (~₹60–80 per meter per month over ~93 months) is the durable revenue, and it is consolidating — Adani’s ₹3,050 crore purchase of IntelliSmart was cleared by the CCI on 30-Oct-2026.
  • Meter assembly is the contested pool: ₹3,600–3,700 a meter on tender, now earning 12–19% EBITDA as input costs bite (HPL metering EBIT fell to 12.28% from 17.59%).
  • Relay, PCB and enclosure localisation is dated and protected by the 2028 MLC rules, but small in rupees per meter.
  • HES/MDMS software is the capital-light, sticky pool — small in absolute value, high in cash conversion.
Market & growth
  • Volume: 20.33 crore sanctioned and 5.73 crore installed at 30-Jun-2026 [REPORTED-T1: MoP Rajya Sabha reply, 27-Jul-2026] leaves ~14.6 crore in the 21 months to 31-Mar-2028 — a ~8.3 crore/year requirement [CALCULATED] against ~1.0 crore a quarter achieved recently and ~1.1 crore/year over the programme’s first five years.
  • Value: a ~₹90,000 crore revenue opportunity for meter makers across the rollout [REPORTED-T2: CRISIL Ratings, Jun-2025]. Scope: meter-maker revenue, not full discom/AMISP project cost.
  • Different scope, not reconciled: at ~₹4,400–4,500 all-in project cost, the remaining ~14.6 crore meters imply ~₹64,000 crore of project value still to be awarded and executed [CALCULATED: 14.6 cr × ₹4,400].
  • No published value CAGR series exists for meter making, so value growth is [UNAVAILABLE] rather than inferred; volume is the only honest growth read, and it is a deadline-driven catch-up, not a compounding market.
  • [CONFLICTING DATA] Power Line (6-Nov-2026) reports >13 crore awarded and >6.4 crore installed under RDSS — a later and looser count that is not reconciled with the T1 5.73 crore at 30-Jun-2026; both are carried, neither is averaged.
Watch items
  • Genus Power Q2 FY27 results are due 14-Nov-2026, after the 4-Nov-2026 death of chairman Ishwar Chand Agarwal; Salzer reports 12-Nov-2026.
  • 1-Jun-2028 MLC mandate: 100% India-made mechanical relays and India-designed chipsets in public procurement (MoP order, 13-Jul-2026).
  • Kerala Phase II award pending — Gridcrest L1 at ₹1,399.79 crore for ~50 lakh meters against Adani’s ₹3,568 crore on the same base scope (5-Sep-2026).
  • Tamil Nadu 1.77 crore-meter, seven-package AMISP tender (Oct-2026) is the largest live award in the pipeline.
Coverage gaps
  • No listed metering-IC / AFE maker in the selected universe; foreign silicon dominates and the domestic candidates are programme-stage.
  • Secure Meters (major OEM) and IntelliSmart (AMISP) are unlisted; CyanConnode is AIM-listed. SMPS, display, tamper sensors and enclosures have no listed pure-play — [UNAVAILABLE].

05 Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunity dual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

Metering chipsets / MCUChips in India’s smart meters are ‘entirely imported’ (ADI, TI, NXP, Renesas), and qualification cycles are long. Resilience: months–years.

Opportunity

India-designed siliconMosChip’s VIDYUT100 taped out in Q1 FY27 and completed initial silicon bring-up on 17-Sep-2026; Cyient-backed Azimuth’s 40 nm SoC is production-ready. The 1-Jun-2028 design-in-India rule is the demand guarantee both need.

Risk

Imported input costsWest Asia disruption and a weaker rupee raised component costs on fixed-price contracts: Genus’s gross margin fell to 37.0% from 39.6% and EBITDA margin to 19.1% from 21.2% in Q1 FY27.

Opportunity

Domestic component substitutionEvery imported relay, bare PCB and passives line is a cost-hedge as well as a margin pool; ECMS-backed PCB and relay capacity is the beneficiary, and the 2028 MLC rules make the switch contractual.

Risk

Latching relayRelays and PCB components are imported; offshore 90 A relays are marketed into India at scale.

Opportunity

India-made relay mandateThe MoP order requires 100% India-made mechanical relays from 1-Jun-2028; AEW adds 5 million units a year and Salzer is backward-integrated into relays.

Risk

Communication basebandThe NB-IoT / RF-mesh modem baseband is the hardest component to localise; RF mesh is led by CyanConnode (AIM-listed) reference designs.

Opportunity

Module and NIC assemblyIndian OEMs build NICs and gateways in-house (Genus) and EMS players make RF nodes (Syrma) — the assembly is already local even though the silicon inside is not.

Risk

Tendering and executionTamil Nadu scrapped two AMI tender rounds before re-floating 1.77 crore meters in Oct-2026; the state had installed 9,746 meters against 3.05 crore sanctioned at 30-Jun-2026.

Opportunity

Re-tender pipelineCancelled and re-floated packages are deferred volume, not lost volume: ~8–9 crore meters are expected to be tendered in FY27 against ~14.6 crore still to install.

Risk

AMISP payment cycleCollections depend on discom credit; unpaid invoices beyond 45 days attract interest, and CRISIL measures 90–180 day cycles cutting AMISP IRRs by up to 150 bps.

Opportunity

DDF bucket-fillingThe direct-debit facility requires discoms to hold inflows of at least 5× the monthly service charge, which turns an unsecured receivable into a ring-fenced flow.

Deepest hidden import dependency: the metering silicon stack (metering IC + MCU + comms baseband) — effectively 100% imported today, with a dated procurement clock (1-Jun-2028) forcing design-in-India chips and 100% India-made relays.
06 Value pools & cost waterfall where value is captured · and which pool leaks to imports
Where value is captured
  • AMISP annuity: ~₹60–80/meter/month over the ~93-month operating phase; Genus guides O&M revenue of ₹600–700 crore from FY28, rising to ₹1,100–1,200 crore as the base matures.
  • Meter OEM manufacturing: ~₹3,600–3,700/meter blended procurement, earning 12–19% EBITDA.
  • HES/MDMS software: licensed or SaaS, capital-light.
  • Relay and PCB localisation: dated by the 2028 MLC rules.
  • Chip design: programme-stage (MosChip, Cyient/Azimuth), policy-backed.
Who captures it (India-listed)
  • Genus Power NSE:GENUSPOWER — OEM + AMISP + HES/MDMS + gateway; order book ₹24,020 cr at 30-Jun-2026, FY27 revenue guidance ₹6,000–6,500 cr
  • Adani Energy Solutions NSE:ADANIENSOL — largest AMISP; 1 crore meters delivered Feb-2026; CCI clearance for IntelliSmart on 30-Oct-2026
  • HPL Electric NSE:HPL — metering revenue ₹270.85 cr in Q2 FY27 (+18.2%), ₹2,700 cr order book at 2-Nov-2026
  • Salzer Electronics NSE:SALZERELEC — 4 mn/yr meter capacity commissioned, backward into relays
Pools that leak to imports
  • Metering IC/AFE, MCU and comms baseband — ~100% imported.
  • PCBs and passives — ~88% of PCB demand imported on a ₹35,000 cr FY25 base; projected to fall to ~69% by FY29.
  • SMPS, display, tamper sensors — imported components with no listed domestic pure-play.

Meter manufacturing still earns money but less of it: Genus ran a 19.1% EBITDA margin in Q1 FY27 against 21.2% a year earlier on a 44.8% revenue jump, HPL’s metering EBIT margin fell to 12.28% from 17.59%, and Salzer’s PAT halved to ₹8.33 cr on a 6.29% EBITDA margin. capital5%
Working capital, not plant, is the binding constraint, and the evidence got worse this run: Genus expects positive operating cash flow only from FY28 after a 50–75 day working-capital improvement, Salzer carried ₹12 cr of quarterly finance cost against ₹498 cr of revenue, and AMISPs fund the whole rollout before collecting. Capex per meter of capacity is modest; the balance sheet is the barrier.

technology5%
The product is mature and the migration is in components — RF mesh to NB-IoT/PLC communications, and imported silicon to India-designed chipsets. That migration is now real rather than aspirational: MosChip’s VIDYUT100 taped out in Q1 FY27 and completed initial silicon bring-up on 17-Sep-2026, and Cyient-backed Azimuth’s 40 nm SoC is production-ready at 20–30% claimed local value addition. Nothing here is frontier; everything is qualification-gated.
