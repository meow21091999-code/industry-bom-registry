DGDigest — at a glanceshort summary; full detail below
Snapshot
  • RDSS (2021) funds a 20.33 crore-meter rollout via AMISPs on a TOTEX/DBFOOT annuity model; ~4.69 cr installed under RDSS as of Mar 2026 (PIB).
  • Meter assembly is domestic; the metering IC, MCU, comms baseband and relays are imported — the value leaks to silicon and components.
Process & equipment
  • Meter built as three boards (comms module, main board MCU+display+power, power board) plus CT/shunt, latching relay and SMPS.
  • RF-mesh gateways/DCUs aggregate ~300+ meters per gateway; HES/MDMS software meters the data.
  • BIS/IS 16444 & IS 15884 type testing and DLMS/COSEM conformance gate the rollout.
Bottlenecks
  • Metering chipsets — ‘entirely imported’ (ADI/TI/NXP/Renesas); localisation mandated by Jun 2028. Resilience: months–years.
  • Latching relays & PCB components — imported; 100% India-made relay mandate from Jun 2028.
  • Comms baseband (NB-IoT / RF mesh) — hard to localise; RF mesh led by CyanConnode (AIM-listed).
  • AMISP payment cycle — DDF ‘bucket-filling’ mitigates discom credit risk but does not remove it.
  • Execution, not demand — eight states had installed zero RDSS meters as of Jun 2025.
Value pools
  • AMISP annuity: ~₹60–80 per meter per month over the ~93-month operating phase.
  • Meter OEM: ~₹3,600–3,700/meter blended procurement; ~₹4,400–4,500 all-in project cost.
  • HES/MDMS software: licensed or SaaS subscriptions.
  • Relay manufacturing: new ECMS-backed capacity (AEW, 5 mn units/yr).
  • Chip design: MosChip VIDYUT100 and Cyient 40nm SoC — nascent but policy-backed.
Watch items
  • MoP order (13 Jul 2026): 100% India-made relays + India-designed chipsets in public procurement from 1 Jun 2028.
  • RDSS deadline extended to Mar 2028 (Moneycontrol, Sep 2026).
  • Adani–IntelliSmart ₹3,050 cr acquisition (CCI pending, Sep 2026).
  • BIS certification made mandatory for imported smart meters.
Coverage gaps
  • No listed pure-play metering-IC / AFE maker; foreign silicon (ADI, TI, NXP, Renesas) dominates.
  • Secure Meters (major OEM) and IntelliSmart (AMISP) are unlisted; CyanConnode is AIM-listed.
  • SMPS, display, tamper sensors and enclosures have no listed pure-play — [UNAVAILABLE].

05 Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunity dual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

Metering chipsets / MCUChips in India's smart meters are ‘entirely imported’ (ADI, TI, NXP, Renesas). Resilience: months–years.

Opportunity

India-designed siliconMosChip VIDYUT100 and Cyient's 40nm SoC, backed by a Jun-2028 design-in-India procurement mandate.

Risk

Latching relayRelays and PCB components are imported; offshore 90A relays are marketed into India.

Opportunity

India-made relay mandateMoP requires 100% India-made mechanical relays from Jun 2028; AEW adds 5 mn units/yr under ECMS.

Risk

Communication basebandThe NB-IoT / RF-mesh modem baseband is a hard localisation; RF mesh is led by CyanConnode (AIM-listed).

Opportunity

NIC assembly localisedIndian OEMs build NICs in-house (Genus) and EMS players make RF nodes (Syrma).

Risk

AMISP payment cycleCollections depend on discom credit; unpaid invoices beyond 45 days attract interest.

Opportunity

DDF bucket-fillingA direct-debit-facility requires discoms to hold inflows of at least 5x the monthly service charge.

Risk

Testing & certificationOnly a limited set of labs handle DLMS/COSEM conformance and IS 16444/IS 15884 testing.

Opportunity

Lab expansionBIS-recognised/NABL and multiple CPRI units reduce the certification queue.

Deepest hidden import dependency: the metering silicon stack (metering IC + MCU + comms baseband) — effectively 100% imported today, with a policy clock forcing design-in-India chips from mid-2028.
06 Value pools & cost waterfall where value is captured · and which pool leaks to imports
Where value is captured
  • AMISP annuity: ~₹60–80/meter/month over the ~93-month operating phase.
  • Meter OEM manufacturing: ~₹3,600–3,700/meter blended procurement.
  • HES/MDMS software: licensed or SaaS subscriptions.
  • Relay manufacturing: new ECMS-backed capacity.
  • Chip design: nascent but policy-backed (MosChip, Cyient).
Who captures it (India-listed)
  • Genus Power NSE:GENUSPOWER — OEM + AMISP + HES/MDMS + gateway; order book ~₹25,173 cr
  • Adani Energy Solutions NSE:ADANIENSOL — largest AMISP; 1 crore+ meters
  • HPL Electric NSE:HPL — ~20% meter share
  • Salzer Electronics NSE:SALZERELEC — backward-integrated meter + relay plant
Pools that leak to imports
  • Metering IC/AFE, MCU and comms baseband — ~100% imported.
  • PCBs and passives — ~88% of PCB demand imported.
  • SMPS, display, tamper sensors — imported components.
