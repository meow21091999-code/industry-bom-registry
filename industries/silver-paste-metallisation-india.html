Snapshot

The cell metallisation step — silver and aluminium pastes, glass frit, printing and co-firing. Silver is 16–17% of module cost (2026) and ~30% of total cell cost; silver peaked at ~$84/oz in Dec-2025; a TOPCon cell uses 86 mg of silver.

Process & equipment
  • Silver powder, glass frit (Pb-Te-O), organic vehicle and paste formulation, plus fine-mesh screens.
  • Screen printing and co-firing turn paste into the Ag fingers, busbars and Al rear.
Bottlenecks
  • Silver paste — DKEM, Fusion, Solamet, DuPont, Heraeus, Samsung SDI, all foreign.
  • Silver powder, glass frit and screens; silver-price volatility.
Value pools
  • Paste makers are foreign/unlisted; cell makers (Premier, Waaree, Websol, Emmvee) bear the cost.
  • Silver recovery from end-of-life panels is emerging.
Watch items
  • Silver price; plated-TOPCon silver reduction (Fraunhofer, 1.1 mg/Wp); the copper-substitution race.
Coverage gaps
  • All paste makers are foreign/unlisted; only Hindustan Zinc (silver by-product) is listed.
01System OverviewMetallisation turns a passivated wafer into a current-delivering cell by printing (or plating) a conductive grid. Gold-bordered nodes below are the HIGH-priority subsystems carried into the drill-down tree — the silver powder, the glass frit, the front-side paste, the printing step, the copper-substitution pathways and the upstream silver metal. This is the node where the module's largest cost line now sits, and where the technology is actively migrating.

05Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunitydual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

Silver Paste & MetallisationPaste formulation itself — the technology and IP — sits with foreign suppliers (DKEM, Fusion, Solamet, DuPont, Heraeus, Samsung SDI). The only listed NSE/BSE exposure is the upstream silver metal.

Opportunity

Silver Paste & Metallisation — localisation playListed incumbents — Hindustan Zinc, Vedanta — can deepen local capability at this node.

Risk

Silver metal & silver powderHigh-purity silver powder is imported; high-purity silver bars (≥99.9%) were moved to the Restricted import category by DGFT from 16 May 2026.

Opportunity

Silver metal & silver powder — localisation playListed incumbents — Hindustan Zinc, Vedanta — can deepen local capability at this node.

Risk

Front-side Ag pasteNO COVERAGE IN SELECTED UNIVERSE. Supplied by DKEM (300842.SZ), Fusion, Solamet, DuPont, Heraeus, Samsung SDI. This is the largest single cost line in the cell.

Opportunity

Front-side Ag paste — localisation playNo NSE/BSE pure-play; domestic qualification of this node is the substitution play. [DERIVED]

Risk

Glass frit (Pb-Te-O)NO COVERAGE. The Pb-Te-O glass-frit formulation is the core IP moat of the paste — patented by the DuPont/Solamet line and now litigated by DKEM. No NSE/BSE pure-play.

Opportunity

Glass frit (Pb-Te-O) — localisation playNo NSE/BSE pure-play; domestic qualification of this node is the substitution play. [DERIVED]

Risk

Screen printing & dryingNO COVERAGE. Screen printers, dryers and the metallisation line are foreign-supplied; no listed Indian equipment maker.

Opportunity

Screen printing & drying — localisation playNo NSE/BSE pure-play; domestic qualification of this node is the substitution play. [DERIVED]

Risk

Silver-coated copper pasteNO COVERAGE. The main near-term de-silvering route; led by DKEM (high-copper paste at GW scale in 2026) and Solamet (PV43T). No NSE/BSE pure-play.

Opportunity

Silver-coated copper paste — localisation playNo NSE/BSE pure-play; domestic qualification of this node is the substitution play. [DERIVED]

Risk

Pure copper pasteNO COVERAGE — early-commercial; oxidation and copper-diffusion unsolved. Solamet PV46 series, DKEM hybrid preservation. No listed Indian pure-play.

Opportunity

Pure copper paste — localisation playNo NSE/BSE pure-play; domestic qualification of this node is the substitution play. [DERIVED]

Risk

Ni/Cu/Ag electroplatingNO COVERAGE. Fraunhofer ISE / RENA pilot lines cut silver to 1.1 mg/Wp; equipment and chemicals come from European / American suppliers (RENA, Atotech). No listed Indian pure-play.

Opportunity

Ni/Cu/Ag electroplating — localisation playNo NSE/BSE pure-play; domestic qualification of this node is the substitution play. [DERIVED]
