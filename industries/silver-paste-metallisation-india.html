Snapshot

The cell metallisation step — silver and aluminium pastes, glass frit, printing and co-firing. Silver is 16–17% of module cost (2026) and ~30% of total cell cost; silver peaked at ~$84/oz in Dec-2025; a TOPCon cell uses 86 mg of silver.

Process & equipment
  • Silver powder, glass frit (Pb-Te-O), organic vehicle and paste formulation, plus fine-mesh screens.
  • Screen printing and co-firing turn paste into the Ag fingers, busbars and Al rear.
Bottlenecks
  • Silver paste — DKEM, Fusion, Solamet, DuPont, Heraeus, Samsung SDI, all foreign.
  • Silver powder, glass frit and screens; silver-price volatility.
Value pools
  • Paste makers are foreign/unlisted; cell makers (Premier, Waaree, Websol, Emmvee) bear the cost.
  • Silver recovery from end-of-life panels is emerging.
Watch items
  • Silver price; plated-TOPCon silver reduction (Fraunhofer, 1.1 mg/Wp); the copper-substitution race.
Coverage gaps
  • All paste makers are foreign/unlisted; only Hindustan Zinc (silver by-product) is listed.
01System overview

Metallisation turns a passivated wafer into a current-delivering cell by printing (or plating) a conductive grid. Gold-bordered nodes below are the HIGH-priority subsystems carried into the drill-down tree — the silver powder, the glass frit, the front-side paste, the printing step, the copper-substitution pathways and the upstream silver metal. This is the node where the module's largest cost line now sits, and where the technology is actively migrating.

02Value-chain ladder

Raw material → aftermarket, with the Tier-4 → OEM supplier bands. The value and the IP sit in the paste formulation (glass frit + silver powder + vehicle) and now increasingly in the copper-substitution and plating pathways — almost entirely outside India.

Tier 4 · Raw
Silver metal · aluminium · copper · tellurium / lead (frit) · organic solvents Hindustan Zinc (silver, by-product)
Tier 3 · Specialty
Silver powder · glass frit (Pb-Te-O) · organic vehicle · paste formulation · fine-mesh screens
Tier 2 · Component
The printed / plated metallisation stack — Ag fingers & busbars, Al rear, Ag/Al pads
Tier 1 · Paste makers
DKEM · Fusion · Solamet · DuPont · Heraeus · Samsung SDI all foreign-listed / unlisted in India
OEM / Downstream
Cell makers — Premier · Waaree · Websol · Emmvee — the cost-bearers of the silver spike
Aftermarket
Module / cell recycling · silver recovery from end-of-life panels emerging, largely informal
