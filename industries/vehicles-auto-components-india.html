Snapshot

India's auto-component industry turned over ₹6.73 lakh crore in FY25 and flipped a rare $453 mn trade surplus, but that became a ~$200 mn deficit in H1 FY26 as US tariffs stalled exports and China imports jumped. Two-wheelers are the most localised chain; every EV powertrain node hides an import wall.

Process & equipment
  • Spine: melting → forging/casting → precision machining → heat treatment → assembly → test/homologation.
  • Forging presses, HPDC die-casting machines, CNC tools and cell-fab/SMT lines are all imported — the deepest hidden dependency.
  • EV adds motor winding, power-module assembly, cell electrode coating/formation and pack EOL testing.
Bottlenecks
  • Rare-earth permanent magnets — single-source (China >90% of processing). Resilience: years.
  • Li-ion cells & active materials — ACC-PLI has commissioned ~1.4 GWh of 50 GWh, zero disbursals.
  • Automotive semiconductors (MCU/SoC, SiC/IGBT, sensors) — near-total import.
  • Electrical steel and capital equipment (presses, CNC, cell lines).
Value pools
  • Upstream materials, cells, magnets and semiconductors capture the highest value — and leak to imports.
  • Electronics & software carry the fastest-rising content-per-vehicle and proprietary margins.
  • Forging/casting/fasteners are globally competitive but capital-heavy and margin-thin — the export franchise (Bharat Forge, Ramkrishna, Sundram).
  • Broadest value-added content stays domestic with Uno Minda and Samvardhana Motherson; deepest EV-tech with Sona Comstar.
  • Aftermarket (₹99,948 cr) is the most resilient domestic pool, riding the ageing parc.
Watch items
  • 31 Jul 2026 — PM E-DRIVE e-2W demand-incentive terminal date.
  • India–US interim deal cut auto-part tariffs from ~50% to 0–18% (Feb 2026).
  • REPM scheme (₹7,280 cr, 6,000 MTPA) tenders expected 2026; production ~2 years out.
  • China eased light-RE magnets to four Indian firms; heavy magnets still restricted.
Coverage gaps
  • No NSE/BSE pure-play in rare-earth magnets, cell active materials, automotive semiconductors or electrical steel; nearest exposure is via conglomerates or unlisted arms.

05 Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunity dual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

Rare-earth permanent magnetsSingle-source — China controls >90% of global processing, and India imported 60–80% of magnet value from it. Design and homologation requalification for magnet-free motors is slow. Resilience: years.

Opportunity

REPM scheme + magnet-free motorsThe ₹7,280 cr REPM scheme (6,000 MTPA, rare-earth corridors in Odisha/Kerala/AP/TN) and ferrite/SRM motor design (Sona Comstar, Uno Minda) are the domestic capture plays.

Risk

Li-ion cells & active materialsCathode, anode, electrolyte and separator are overwhelmingly imported; ACC-PLI has commissioned ~1.4 GWh of a 50 GWh target with zero disbursals. Resilience: years.

Opportunity

Gigafactories & upstream materialsAgratas (Tata), Ola, Reliance, Exide–SVOLT and Amara Raja–Gotion are building cells; the cathode/anode/electrolyte chain is an almost-empty domestic market.

Risk

Automotive semiconductorsMCU/SoC, power (SiC/IGBT), analog and MEMS are near-totally imported; AEC-Q requalification is slow. Resilience: years.

Opportunity

ATMP, design & software valueCG Power ATMP and Tata Electronics (fab + OSAT, unlisted) build back-end capacity; KPIT and Tata Elxsi capture ECU/ADAS/SDV value without owning a fab.

Risk

Capital equipment & electrical steelForging presses, HPDC machines, CNC tools, SMT lines and cell-fab equipment are imported; motor-grade electrical steel is largely imported too. Resilience: months–years.

Opportunity

Machine-tool localisationCapital-goods PLI and Indian cell-line/SMT integrators; domestic CRGO/CRNGO expansion (JSW, Tata Steel) is a prerequisite for localising magnet-free motors and chargers.

Deepest hidden dependency: imported capital equipment and upstream specialty materials — magnets, cells, electrical steel and semiconductors — sit under every "made-in-India" vehicle. Localising assembly without localising these leaves the highest-value layers imported.
06 Value pools & cost waterfall where value is captured · and which pool leaks to imports
Where value is captured
  • Upstream materials & specialty inputs (magnets, cells, electrical steel, semiconductors) capture the highest, most defensible value — and mostly leak to imports.
  • Electronics & software (ADAS, ECUs, SDV) carry the fastest-rising content-per-vehicle and proprietary margins.
  • Forging/casting/fastener tiers are globally competitive but capital-heavy and margin-thin — India's strongest export franchise.
  • OEM assembly is scale-driven; EV OEMs are burning cash to hold share.
  • Aftermarket (₹99,948 cr) is the most resilient domestic pool, riding the ageing parc.
Who captures it (India-listed)
  • Uno Minda — broadest value-added content (switches, lighting, alloy wheels, EV systems); ₹19,589 cr FY26.
  • Samvardhana Motherson — wiring harness + modules at scale; ₹126,104 cr FY26.
  • Sona Comstar — deepest EV-tech content (motors, controllers, driveline); Denso JVs.
  • Bharat Forge / Ramkrishna / Sundram Fasteners — export-led forging & fastener margins.
  • KPIT / Tata Elxsi — software value with no capex.
Pools that leak to imports
  • Rare-earth magnets (60–80% of value).
  • Li-ion cells & active materials.
  • Semiconductors (MCU, power, sensors).
  • Electrical steel.
  • Capital equipment (presses, CNC, SMT, cell lines).
  • Collectively these are the bulk of India's ~20–30% import dependence.
