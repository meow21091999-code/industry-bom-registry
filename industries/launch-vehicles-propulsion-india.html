DDigestshort summary · full detail below
Snapshot
  • India's launch segment: PSLV/GSLV/LVM3/SSLV plus emerging private small launchers.
  • ~90–95% domestic content — the most indigenous part of the space chain.
Process & equipment
  • Solid-stage casting and proof-pressure testing; cryogenic engine assembly; composite winding and precision machining.
  • Launch-vehicle integration and last-mile at SDSC SHAR.
Bottlenecks
  • Launch cadence — ~12/yr vs 50–60 needed (months–years).
  • Cryogenic engine hardware — narrow-source (months–years).
  • Private launch ramp-up — Vikram-1 reached orbit (Jul 2026); scale-up unproven (years).
Value pools
  • Domestic capture: vehicles (HAL, L&T), engines (MTAR), motor casings (Walchandnagar, L&T), avionics (Data Patterns, BEL), materials (MIDHANI).
  • Value increasingly moving to industry as ISRO outsources serial production.
Watch items
  • HAL SSLV production ramp; HAL–L&T PSLV deliveries.
  • Vikram-1 reached orbit (Jul 2026); commercial ramp and LVM3 commercial missions.
Coverage gaps
  • No NSE/BSE pure-play for private small launch vehicles.

05Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunitydual-readable
Risk

Launch cadenceIndia flies ~12 missions/yr against a stated need for 50–60. Resilience: months–years.

Opportunity

Industry-built launchersHAL's SSLV ToT (₹511 cr) and the HAL–L&T PSLV consortium (₹860 cr) shift serial production to industry.

Risk

Cryogenic engine hardwareTurbo-pumps, injectors and thrust chambers are narrow-source and precision-bound. Resilience: months–years.

Opportunity

Deepen the qualified supplier baseMTAR, L&T and Godrej (unlisted) form a growing domestic cryo/propulsion supplier base; SCE-200 semi-cryo adds volume.

Risk

Private orbital launchFirst private orbital launch achieved (Vikram-1, Jul 2026), but scaling to a reliable commercial cadence is unproven. Resilience: years.

Opportunity

Private launch marketSkyroot (Vikram-1) and Agnikul open domestic launch-on-demand at lower cost.
