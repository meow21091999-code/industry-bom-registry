Snapshot

Li-ion cell manufacturing is the single largest value pool in the battery chain and India's deepest import gap: ~2 GWh installed against a ~260 GWh tender pipeline, with cells 25–40% costlier to make locally. Chemistry is LFP-dominant for BESS; NMC serves higher-energy uses.

Process & equipment
  • Process train: slurry mixing → coating & drying → calendering → slitting/die-cutting → stacking or winding → tab welding → electrolyte filling → formation → ageing → grading.
  • Cell-line equipment is ~55–60% of a 5 GWh plant's cost and >75% of global capacity is Chinese (Wuxi Lead, Yinghe, Hangke, Hymson).
  • India's live value-add is module/pack/container assembly (Semco, Itech), not cell making.
Bottlenecks
  • Cathode & anode — China ~90% of LFP CAM and >90% of graphitisation; LFP tech on China's restricted-export list (Jul-2025).
  • Separator & copper foil — near-100% / 80–85% imported.
  • Cell-line machinery — on China's dual-use list; the 10 Nov 2026 suspension expiry is a live cliff.
  • Scale economics — a 5 GWh plant runs at ~−10% EBITDA; breakeven ~10 GWh.
Value pools
  • Cathode CAM (35–45% of material cost) — Himadri (HSCL), Gujarat Fluorochemicals (FLUOROCHEM), Tata Chemicals.
  • Anode AAM (12–18%) — HEG, Himadri; Epsilon/TACC unlisted.
  • Separator & electrolyte — TCPL (TCPLPACK) separator; Neogen (NEOGEN) + GFL electrolyte/salts.
  • Foils & casing — Hindalco (HINDALCO) battery-grade Al/Cu foil.
  • Cells — Ola (OLAELEC), Exide (EXIDEIND), Reliance (RELIANCE), Amara Raja (ARE&M), Waaree (WAAREEENER).
Watch items
  • 10 Nov 2026 — China's suspension of battery-machine & graphite-anode export controls expires.
  • Final 10 GWh ACC-PLI tender (grid-scale) closes 13 Oct 2026; disbursements from FY28.
  • A ≈₹12,000–13,000 cr battery-component incentive scheme (CAM, AAM, separator, foil) in final approval.
  • BCD exemption on ~85 cell-making machinery categories extended to 31 Mar 2029.
Coverage gaps
  • No listed pure-play for cell-line machinery (foreign OEMs). Cell entities are unlisted subsidiaries (OCT, EESL, ARACT, Reliance New Energy, Agratas).

05 Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunity dual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

Cathode & anode (CAM / AAM)China holds ~90% of LFP cathode and >90% of graphitisation; LFP technology and graphite/anode equipment are under Chinese export control. Resilience: structural.

Opportunity

Domestic CAM & anodeHimadri, GFL, HEG/TACC and Epsilon are commissioning CAM/AAM; a ~₹12,000–13,000 cr component scheme is in final approval.

Risk

Cell-line machinery & the 10 Nov 2026 cliff<75% of global Li-ion equipment capacity is Chinese; winding/stacking/filling/formation machines sit on China's dual-use list, suspended only until 10 Nov 2026. Resilience: weeks (policy) to months (lead times).

Opportunity

Machinery duty relief~85 categories of cell-making machinery are BCD-exempt to 31 Mar 2029, and Indian pack/module-line integrators (Semco, Itech) are scaling.

Risk

Scale economicsA 5 GWh plant runs at ~−10% EBITDA; breakeven ~10 GWh and positive margins need ≥20 GWh; domestic cells cost 25–40% more than imports. Resilience: years.

Opportunity

Policy-pulled demandCEA's storage mandate, the 20% DCR on VGF BESS and grid-forming rules create captive volume; PLI-ACC disbursements begin FY28.

Risk

Technology licences & skilled manpowerNearly all players rely on Chinese/Korean technology licences, now restricted; Chinese-engineer visa delays slow commissioning; LFP process know-how is tacit. Resilience: structural.

Opportunity

In-house developmentAgratas developed LFP in-house after concluding Chinese licensing was near-nil; Indian process-engineering depth is building with each line commissioned.

Deepest hidden dependency: the cell-making machinery itself — electrode coaters, stackers, formation cabinets and HV test rigs — is Chinese-dominated and export-control exposed, so it is the true gate on how fast India can localise cells, ahead even of the materials.
