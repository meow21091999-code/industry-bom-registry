Snapshot

5G/4G RAN — radios, baseband units and Massive-MIMO AAUs, the access layer of the network. India assembles but imports the silicon. Scope: India, NSE/BSE.

Process & equipment
  • Radios and BBUs are SMT-assembled and RF-tested in India; AAUs integrate antenna arrays with radios.
  • SMT lines, RF test benches and environmental chambers are the key equipment; RAN silicon is imported.
Bottlenecks
  • RF and component import dependence.
  • RAN silicon (FPGA/SoC/ASIC) — fully imported.
  • RAN OEM concentration (Nokia/Ericsson/Samsung for private telcos).
  • Trusted-source (NSCS) gatekeeping; PLI under-disbursal and lumpy capex.
Value pools
  • Value sits in imported silicon and foreign OEMs.
  • Indian RAN value-add is assembly and integration.
  • The BSNL order proved capability but is lumpy.
  • Offset play: AMC / software-upgrade annuitisation.
Watch items
  • PLI disbursal.
  • 5G densification and 5G-Advanced.
  • Open-RAN adoption.
  • Nokia / Ericsson localisation.
Coverage gaps

No listed RAN-silicon pure-play; RAN OEM exposure is indirect (Tejas, HFCL, ITI, Dixon) — nearest proxies noted on the tree.

05 Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunity dual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

RF and component import dependenceOnly ~4% of a RAN BOM is localised; more than 80% of critical components such as 4G/5G antennas and signal processors come from China, and India imports US$4–5 bn of telecom gear a year.

Opportunity

PLI localisation roadmapDoT has defined staged targets for RAN radios — plastics year 2, metal parts year 3, antennas year 4, RF filters and connectors year 5 — plus a design-led manufacturing incentive.

Risk

RAN silicon dependencyL1-PHY FPGAs, SoCs and ASICs are almost fully imported (AMD/Xilinx, Altera, Marvell, Qualcomm); there is no listed Indian pure-play and domestic fabs are not yet at scale.

Opportunity

Disaggregation lowers the silicon lock-inJio virtualised L1 on general-purpose processors; indigenous O-RAN 32T32R radios (WiSig, COMET) show an India-built path around custom silicon.

Risk

RAN OEM concentrationPrivate operators — nearly 98% of demand — still buy Nokia, Ericsson and Samsung, while Indian OEMs are concentrated on a single BSNL order that has now run its course.

Opportunity

A proven Indian OEM and an export doorTejas delivered 100,000+ sites (a world-scale proof point) and its NEC partnership opens advanced RAN and global go-to-market; Dixon, HFCL and VVDN hold PLI Open-RAN approvals.

Risk

Antennas and populated boardsAntennas are ~69% Chinese and populated PCBs ~55% Chinese, and Indian manufacturers face costs 10–15% higher than global OEMs on imported inputs.

Opportunity

The narrow, under-capitalised seamAntennas, RF front-ends and populated boards are the physical components where a domestic substitute is most plausible and the incumbent Indian firms are visibly under-capitalised.

Risk

Trusted-source gatekeepingNSCS trusted-source clearance and NCCS ITSAR certification (now covering disaggregated gNB and O-RAN RIC) gate every vendor; Huawei and ZTE remain excluded, adding compliance cost and time.

Opportunity

A certification moat in India's favourApproved Indian vendors — Tejas, HFCL, Dixon — and foreign OEMs that manufacture locally clear the gate first, raising entry barriers in their favour.

Risk

PLI under-disbursal and lumpy capexOnly about 15% of the ₹12,195 cr telecom PLI outlay was disbursed and Samsung exited the scheme; the BSNL RAN order that powered FY25 will not repeat at the same scale.

Opportunity

Annuitising the installed baseAMC, managed services and 4G→5G software upgrades convert 100,000+ installed sites into a recurring stream — Tejas guides to “several hundred crore” from BSNL 5G upgrades.

Deepest hidden import dependency: the RF front-end and RAN silicon — power amplifiers, filters/duplexers and the L1-PHY FPGAs/SoCs that do the baseband maths. These sit below the OEM assembly layer India has localised, are almost entirely imported, and remain majority-Chinese even as the trusted-source wall keeps Chinese finished gear out.
