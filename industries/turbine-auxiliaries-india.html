Snapshot

The turbine-island auxiliaries — the feedwater train (condensate pumps, LP/HP feedwater heaters, deaerator, boiler feed pumps), the moisture separator reheater, the lube/control-oil system and the turbine-governing + DCS/control layer that supervise the turbo-generator. India’s feedwater equipment is now largely own-design: KSB localised the 700 MWe nuclear main boiler feed pump (2021), BHEL builds heaters and deaerators up to 1000 MW, and L&T/ISGEC/Thermax cover the heat exchangers. The residual import gap is the high-end DCS/governing platform, high-spec deaerator internals and specialty seal/valve IP.

Process & equipment
  • Pump shaft and casing forgings, mechanical seals, control-valve internals, heavy pressure-vessel fabrication (heaters / deaerators / MSR) and seismic-qualified pump-set assembly and testing.
  • High-end digital control and turbine-governing platforms are imported; large tray-type / membrane deaerator internals are also partly imported.
Bottlenecks
  • High-end DCS and governing platforms are dominated by a few global OEMs; BHEL builds its own EHC but the top-end DCS stack is import-led. Resilience: months.
  • High-spec (large tray-type / membrane) deaerator internals are import-dependent (~55–65% of Indian consumption). Resilience: months.
  • High-end mechanical-seal and control-valve IP is partly imported. Resilience: months.
  • Boiler feed pump was localised only in 2021; nuclear-grade qualification depth is still maturing. Resilience: months.
Value pools
  • BHEL NSE: BHEL — feedwater heaters, deaerators, BFPs, MSR, governing and C&I; outstanding OB ₹2.58 lakh cr (Sep-2026).
  • KSB NSE: KSB — BFPs and nuclear PCPs; nuclear OB ₹1,235 cr (Jun-2026); Shirwal +20% capacity.
  • Kirloskar Brothers NSE: KIRLOSBROS — power / nuclear pumps; PCP prototype tested Q3 FY26.
  • Larsen & Toubro NSE: LT — feedwater heaters, deaerators and heat exchangers, incl. nuclear-grade.
  • ISGEC NSE: ISGEC · Thermax NSE: THERMAX · WPIL NSE: WPIL — heat exchangers, pressure vessels, BoP and pumps.
  • Honeywell Automation India NSE: HONAUT · ABB India NSE: ABB · Siemens Energy India NSE: ENRIN — DCS / governing (the pool that leaks to imports).

  • High-end DCS / governing platforms and high-end mechanical-seal / control-valve IP are partly imported; the automation nodes are covered only by MNC India subsidiaries (Honeywell Automation India, ABB India, Siemens Energy India), not by an Indian-owned platform.

Risk

High-end DCS & governing platforms importedThe digital control and turbine-governing platforms are dominated by a few global OEMs; BHEL builds its own EHC but the top-end DCS stack is import-led. Resilience: months.

Opportunity

Listed automation players localiseHoneywell Automation India, ABB India and Siemens Energy India can extend localisation into turbine-island control and protection — a ~$350 mn (2030) India power-DCS pool.

Risk

High-spec deaerator internals importedLarge tray-type / membrane deaerator internals are import-dependent (est. 55–65% of Indian consumption), even though BHEL and L&T build the vessels domestically. Resilience: months.

Opportunity

Internals localisationDomestic fabrication of high-performance tray / spray internals would close a deaerator gap in a 6–8% CAGR India market and serve the 47.5 GW thermal pipeline.

Risk

Mechanical seal & control-valve IPHigh-end mechanical seals and control-valve internals carry imported IP and precision. Resilience: months.

Opportunity

Seal & valve localisationA domestic high-end seal and control-valve base would de-risk the feedwater and governing sub-systems across thermal and nuclear sets.

Risk

BFP localised only in 2021KSB’s 700 MWe nuclear main BFP was India’s first; scale-up and nuclear-grade qualification depth are still maturing, with Kirloskar Brothers a developing second source. Resilience: months.

Opportunity

Second-source the BFP lineKBL’s tested PCP prototype and KSB’s Shirwal expansion (+20% capacity) give India two qualified nuclear-pump lines for fleet-mode 700 MWe and supercritical demand.

Where value sits: the auxiliaries are the most thoroughly localised layer of the turbine island — feedwater heaters, deaerators, BFPs and oil systems are covered by KSB, BHEL, Kirloskar Brothers, WPIL, L&T, ISGEC and Thermax. The residual import gap is the high-end DCS / governing platform, high-spec deaerator internals and specialty seal / valve IP.
06 Value pools & cost waterfall where value is captured · and which pool leaks to imports
Where value is captured
  • The feedwater train (BFPs, heaters, deaerators) is the anchor value pool and is largely domestic; BHEL is the broadest single supplier. Value leaks to imports only at the control/governing platform and specialty seal/valve and deaerator internals.
Who captures it (India-listed)
  • BHEL NSE: BHEL — feedwater heaters, deaerators (7–1000 MW), BFPs, MSR (220/500/700 MWe), governing and C&I; outstanding OB ₹2.58 lakh cr (Sep-2026)
  • KSB Ltd NSE: KSB — BFPs and nuclear primary coolant pumps; nuclear OB ₹1,235 cr (Jun-2026); Shirwal +20% capacity
  • Kirloskar Brothers NSE: KIRLOSBROS — power and nuclear pumps; PCP prototype tested Q3 FY26; standalone pending OB ₹2,375 cr (Jun-2026)
  • Larsen & Toubro NSE: LT — feedwater heaters, deaerators and heat exchangers incl. nuclear-grade
  • ISGEC Heavy Engineering NSE: ISGEC — heat exchangers and pressure vessels
  • Thermax NSE: THERMAX — BoP heat-transfer equipment
  • WPIL NSE: WPIL — pumps for power applications
  • Honeywell Automation India NSE: HONAUT · ABB India NSE: ABB · Siemens Energy India NSE: ENRIN — DCS and turbine control
Pools that leak to imports
  • High-end DCS / governing platforms (MNC-designed, few global OEMs).
  • High-spec deaerator internals and high-end mechanical-seal / control-valve IP.
  • Bottleneck nodes: Boiler Feed Pumps; Turbine Governing, EHC & Protection.
