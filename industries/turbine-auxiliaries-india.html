Snapshot

Turbine-island auxiliaries — boiler feed pumps, primary coolant pumps, seals, valves and the governing/DCS platforms. India's first fully indigenous 700 MWe nuclear main boiler feed pump has been built; high-end DCS platforms remain imported.

Process & equipment
  • Pump shaft and casing forgings, mechanical seals, control-valve internals and seismic-qualified pump sets.
  • High-end digital control and turbine-governing platforms are imported.
Bottlenecks
  • High-end DCS and governing platforms are dominated by a few global OEMs.
  • Boiler feed pump was only recently localised.
  • High-end seal and valve IP is partly imported.
Value pools
  • KSB (₹500 cr NPCIL order; Shirwal facility), BHEL pump works, and listed automation players (Honeywell Automation India, ABB India, Siemens Energy India).
Watch items
  • First indigenous 700 MWe nuclear main boiler feed pump.
  • DCS localisation by listed automation players.
  • 60-year asset life and recurring auxiliaries aftermarket.
Coverage gaps
  • High-end DCS/governing platforms and high-end seal/valve IP are partly imported.

05Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunitydual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

High-end DCS & governing platforms importedThe digital control and turbine-governing platforms are dominated by a few global OEMs; BHEL builds its own EHC but the top-end DCS stack is import-led. Resilience: months.

Opportunity

Listed automation players localiseHoneywell Automation India, ABB India and Siemens Energy India can extend localisation into turbine-island control and protection.

Risk

Boiler feed pump recently localisedIndia’s first fully indigenous 700 MWe nuclear main boiler feed pump was delivered only in 2021; scale-up and qualification depth are still maturing. Resilience: months.

Opportunity

Scale the indigenous BFP lineKSB’s Shirwal facility and BHEL’s pump works give a platform to serve fleet-mode and supercritical demand.

Risk

Mechanical seal & control-valve IPHigh-end mechanical seals and control-valve internals carry imported IP and precision. Resilience: months.

Opportunity

Seal & valve localisationA domestic high-end seal and control-valve base would de-risk the feedwater and governing sub-systems.

Risk

MSR is a nuclear-specific buildThe moisture separator reheater is a large, code-gated fabrication supplied by a small pool. Resilience: months–years.

Opportunity

MSR capacity for fleet modeBHEL, L&T and Walchandnagar can scale MSR and secondary-cycle fabrication as fleet mode ramps.

Where value sits: the auxiliaries are the most thoroughly localised layer of the turbine island — feedwater heaters, deaerators, BFPs and oil systems are covered by KSB, BHEL, Kirloskar Brothers, WPIL, L&T, ISGEC and Thermax. The residual import gap is the high-end DCS / governing platform and specialty seal/valve IP.
