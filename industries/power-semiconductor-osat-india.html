Snapshot
  • India imports ~100% of power valves (IGBT/thyristor) and ~90% of wide-bandgap devices; back-end OSAT is the chosen entry point.
  • Of 12 ISM-approved units, 9 are packaging (ATMP/OSAT); front-end fab is only now starting (Tata Dholera).
Process & equipment
  • Front-end: mature-node (90–350nm) Si fab; SiC adds crystal growth and epitaxy.
  • Back-end OSAT: die attach, wire bonding, moulding, singulation and electrical test.
  • Module assembly integrates multiple dies (e.g. an IPM5 module has 17 dies) into one package.
Bottlenecks
  • Leadframe — ~95% imported; 35–40% of module material cost. Resilience: critical.
  • Bonding wire — ~100% imported; held by Tanaka/Heraeus. Resilience: critical.
  • Moulding compound — ~90% imported; no Indian EMC qualified at an OSAT. Resilience: high.
  • ABF/organic substrate — ~100% imported, longest lead time. Resilience: high.
  • Equipment & machinery — ~100% imported. Resilience: critical.
Value pools
  • Back-end OSAT capacity build-out (Kaynes, CG Semi, Tata, HCL-Foxconn).
  • Front-end SiC compound fab (RIR, SiCSem).
  • Packaging-materials localisation — the revenue-fat, >90% import-dependent middle.
  • PCB/substrate substitution (~₹40,000 cr annual import bill).
  • HVDC SiC MOSFET modules (MeitY–MoP–POWERGRID programme).
Watch items
  • Semicon 2.0 guidelines for Pillars 2–4 issued Sep 2026.
  • Tata Dholera first commercial chips targeted late 2026–2028.
  • Tata Jagiroad OSAT pilot production Oct 2026; inauguration Dec 2026–Jan 2027.
  • RIR SiC epitaxial wafer production targeted Q2–Q3 FY27.
Coverage gaps
  • Device/IDM leaders with no Indian fab (Infineon, ST, Onsemi, Mitsubishi, Wolfspeed).
  • Unlisted Indian players (Tata Electronics, SiCSem, Sagar Semiconductors, CDIL, Micron India).
  • No Indian leadframe stamper, moulding-compound or bonding-wire producer; gate-driver IC absent.

05 Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunity dual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

Leadframe supplyImported ~95%; 35–40% of module material cost; no Indian stamper/etcher qualified at an OSAT. Resilience: critical.

Opportunity

Precision-metal localisationHindalco copper strip and Motherson stamping capability are the identified feeders — not yet qualified.

Risk

Bonding wire (Au/Cu)~100% imported; held by Tanaka (Japan) and Heraeus (Germany). Resilience: critical.

Opportunity

None todayNo domestic producer identified; precious-metal import licensing adds friction.

Risk

Moulding compound (EMC)~90% imported; package-family specific; no Indian EMC qualified at any OSAT line. Resilience: high.

Opportunity

Upstream epoxy entryEpigral and Anupam Rasayan attack the upstream resin, not the finished compound.

Risk

Organic / ABF substrate~100% imported, longest lead time, the most expensive item in advanced packages. Resilience: high.

Opportunity

Substrate JVsAmber's Ascent-K (Korea Circuit JV) targets HDI/flex/substrate from ~FY2028.

Risk

Equipment & machinery~100% imported; ‘no semiconductor equipment manufacturing is happening in India’. Resilience: critical.

Opportunity

Semicon 2.0 Pillar 2Machines & Materials pillar offers 30% capex + BoM-linked PLI from FY2028-29.

Deepest hidden import dependency: the packaging-materials and equipment layer beneath the OSAT — leadframe (~95% imported), bonding wire (~100%), moulding compound (~90%), ABF substrate (~100%) and all fab/assembly equipment (~100%). India can assemble the package but cannot yet supply what goes into it.
