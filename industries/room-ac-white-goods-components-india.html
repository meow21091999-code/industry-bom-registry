Snapshot

India's air-conditioning industry was worth ~₹35,500 cr in FY26 and sold 11.0–11.5 mn room-AC units after a record 12.5–13.0 mn in FY25. Almost every unit is assembled in India; a large share of the value inside it is not. This entry dissects the component ecosystem that RACs share with refrigerators and washing machines.

Process & equipment
  • Spine: coil making (tube bend → fin press → hairpin expand → braze) → press shop → moulding → SMT → refrigerant charge and leak test → performance test.
  • Imported machines gate every expansion: fin presses, high-cavity moulds, high-speed presses, SMT lines, helium leak detectors, calorimeter rooms.
  • An efficiency re-rating is a manufacturing event: new coil geometry, new compressor, new board, full model re-testing.
Bottlenecks
  • Compressors — 7–8 mn capacity vs ~15 mn demand; import quota since 8-May-2026. Resilience: years.
  • Inner-grooved copper tube and fin stock — QCO relief only to 30-Nov-2026. Resilience: months.
  • Control electronics — boards assembled here, IPM/MCU and laminate imported. Resilience: months to years.
  • Capital equipment, toolrooms and accredited test labs — no domestic answer yet.
Value pools
  • Compressors (20–30% of AC cost) plus condensers, blower motors and PCBs are 55–60% of cost — the pools that leak.
  • Indian-captured: heat exchangers, sheet metal, moulding, PCBA and ODM assembly — real but thin (Amber CD EBITDA 7.1%, EPACK 6.0% in FY26).
  • Refrigerants are a genuine Indian high-margin pool (SRF, GFL), capped by the Kigali quota.
  • Copper and aluminium semi-fabrication is the newest Indian pool: Hindalco IGT and fins, MetTube–Adani at Sanand and Mundra.
Market & growth
  • Volume: FY26 −10–15% to 11.0–11.5 mn [REPORTED-T2]; FY27E +12–14% to 12.6–12.9 mn; FY26–FY30 volume CAGR 10–15% [CALCULATED].
  • Value: ₹35,500 cr in FY26, nominal, retail scope. A five-year nominal value CAGR is [UNAVAILABLE] — published series mix retail and ex-factory bases.
  • Price/mix: BEE re-rating plus copper and rupee drove 5–15% price increases in CY2026, so part of value growth is price, not physical units.
  • Volume is the honest growth measure here; treat any value figure as price-inflated.
Watch items
  • 30-Nov-2026: copper IGT QCO exemption expires; 1-Dec-2026: wrought-aluminium QCOs bite.
  • 31-Mar-2027: compressor QCO import relief expires.
  • PLI White Goods ends FY2028-29; industry has asked DPIIT for a 2–3 year extension [LIVE].
  • 1-Jan-2028: Kigali HFC freeze begins the end of the R-32 era.
Coverage gaps
  • No NSE/BSE pure-play in compressors, expansion valves and brass, coil-making capital equipment, or accredited test labs; nearest listed exposure is Kirloskar Pneumatic, LG Electronics India or the ODM group.

05 Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunity dual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

Compressors — the quota regime arrives before the capacity. Domestic AC-compressor capacity is 7–8 mn units against ~15 mn of demand, and the compressor is 20–30% of a room AC's cost. The DPIIT order of 8-May-2026 caps imports of sub-2-tonne rotary compressors at 30% of FY25 volumes. Domestic plants are foreign-owned, and any switch of source needs ~6 months of validation. Resilience: years.

Opportunity

The compressor is the single largest localisation prize left — roughly ₹4,000–6,000 cr of annual value at current volumes. Kirloskar Pneumatic is the only listed Indian entrant with a PLI commitment; Daikin–Rechi, GMCC, Highly and Atomberg are building unlisted capacity. Every rupee of compressor value localised removes an import at the top of the cost stack.

Risk

Inner-grooved copper tube and aluminium fin stock — a two-metal chokepoint. IGT and hydrophilic fin stock are precision semi-fabrications, not commodity metal. The QCO exemption that lets manufacturers import uncertified IGT runs only to 30-Nov-2026 and is capped at 50% of FY24–26 average volumes; wrought-aluminium QCOs bite from 1-Dec-2026. Copper and aluminium prices plus a weak rupee drove 5–15% AC price rises in CY2026. Resilience: months.

Opportunity

Hindalco's 25 KTPA IGT line at Vadodara and AC-fin line at Taloja, plus MetTube–Adani's Sanand and Mundra plants, are building the first genuine Indian position in this layer. If they qualify, the coil becomes a domestic value pool instead of an import line — and India gains an export base in ACR tube.

Risk

Control electronics — assembled here, designed and diced elsewhere. PCBA is now made in India at scale (Amber/ILJIN, Dixon, PGEL, Syrma, Kaynes), but the inverter board's intelligent power module, MCU, gate drivers and sensor die are imported, and the copper-clad laminate under the board is imported too. An efficiency re-rating raises semiconductor content per unit. Resilience: months for assembly, years for the devices.

Opportunity

The localisable value is real and near-term: control assemblies, box-build and bare PCB (Amber's Ascent and Shogini, with ECMS approvals for multilayer and HDI). The device layer is a longer play — ATMP and OSAT capacity (CG Power, Kaynes) is the first rung, and appliance-grade power devices are an obvious first product for it.

Risk

The machines, the moulds and the labs. Fin presses, tube benders, hairpin expanders, brazing furnaces, high-cavity moulds, SMT lines and helium leak detectors are imported, with multi-quarter lead times. Toolrooms are fragmented. Accredited calorimeter/ISEER test capacity is thin, so every BEE re-rating or QCO change re-queues every model. Resilience: years.

Opportunity

This is the layer where 'Make in India' has no answer yet, and therefore where a domestic capital-goods and toolroom business would face the least competition. A shared, NABL-accredited test facility for MSME component makers is the cheapest single intervention available — the CEEW diagnosis is that MSMEs cannot individually fund labs and R&D.

Deepest hidden dependency: the machines, the moulds, the semiconductor devices and the accredited labs. India now makes the coil, the board and the cabinet — it does not yet make the fin press that forms the coil, the IPM that drives the board, or the toolroom that cuts the cabinet mould. Localising assembly without localising these leaves the highest-value and highest-barrier layers imported.
06 Value pools & cost waterfall where value is captured · and which pool leaks to imports
Where value is captured
  • Compressors capture 20–30% of a room AC's cost; with condensers, blower motors and PCBs the critical-component group is 55–60% (CEAMA, via India Ratings).
  • Heat exchangers, sheet metal, moulding and PCBA are genuinely Indian pools, but they are competitive and thin-margin.
  • Refrigerants are a high-margin Indian oligopoly (SRF, GFL), volume-capped by the Kigali quota.
  • Copper and aluminium semi-fabrication is the newest Indian pool and the most defensible, because precision forming is a real barrier.
  • Assembly and ODM margin is the thinnest pool and the most cyclical.
Who captures it (India-listed)
  • Amber Enterprises — broadest component stack (heat exchanger, copper tube, motor, sheet metal, moulding, PCBA); CD division ₹8,383 cr FY26.
  • EPACK Durable — second-largest RAC ODM; components revenue +103% in FY26, the fastest-growing listed component line.
  • PG Electroplast — AC and washing-machine ODM with in-house PCB, moulding and sheet metal.
  • Kirloskar Pneumatic — the only listed Indian compressor entrant with a PLI commitment.
  • Hindalco — IGT copper tube and AC fin stock, the metallics layer.
  • SRF and GFL — refrigerants.
Pools that leak to imports
  • Compressors and their sub-parts (motor, drive, shell).
  • Expansion valves, brass fittings and solenoids.
  • IPM, MCU, gate drivers and sensor die.
  • Copper-clad laminate and specialty PCB materials.
  • Fin presses, moulds, SMT and test equipment.
  • Rare-earth magnets and motor-grade electrical steel.
