Snapshot

High- and extra-high-voltage power cables above 33 kV (XLPE, land and submarine). India's wires & cables market is ₹90,000 cr (FY25); EHV/HV is 17–19% of power-transmission cables. The highest EHV grade now made in India is 500 kV; there are zero submarine-cable plants.

Process & equipment
  • Continuous vulcanisation (CCV/VCV) lines extrude the XLPE insulation; the conductor is Milliken-segmented; joints and terminations are mostly made in-house.
  • Submarine-cable capability is entirely absent.
Bottlenecks
  • HV/EHV XLPE compound and semiconductive compound — foreign (Borealis/Borouge).
  • Pre-moulded joints and terminations; global HV procurement lead times of 2–3 years.
  • The submarine-cable gap is the single largest hole.
Value pools
  • Cable OEMs — Polycab, KEI, Universal, Apar, Finolex, RR Kabel, Havells.
  • EPC — KEC, L&T, Kalpataru.
  • Copper/aluminium recovery; submarine cables are the open prize.
Watch items
  • Sterlite Electric greenfield (Vadodara, up to 400–550 kV).
  • KEI full-spectrum HVDC by FY27; Polycab EHV by 2027.
Coverage gaps
  • No submarine-cable plants; HV XLPE compound is foreign.

05Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunitydual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

XLPE insulation systemThe technology core of the cable. HV/EHV-grade XLPE and semiconductive compounds are a genuine material bottleneck — super-clean grades come from a handful of global producers, not from India. No NSE/BSE pure-play.

Opportunity

XLPE insulation system — localisation playNo NSE/BSE pure-play; domestic qualification of this node is the substitution play. [DERIVED]

Risk

XLPE compound (HV/EHV grade)NO COVERAGE IN SELECTED UNIVERSE. Borealis/Borouge, Hanwha (foreign); BLS Polymers and Ankit Polymers are unlisted. Nearest listed proxy: none.

Opportunity

XLPE compound (HV/EHV grade) — localisation playNo NSE/BSE pure-play; domestic qualification of this node is the substitution play. [DERIVED]

Risk

Semiconductive compoundNO COVERAGE. Supplied by the same global compound majors (e.g. Borealis/Borouge Antwerp). No listed Indian pure-play.

Opportunity

Semiconductive compound — localisation playNo NSE/BSE pure-play; domestic qualification of this node is the substitution play. [DERIVED]

Risk

Joints, terminations & accessoriesPre-moulded joints and terminations are a certification-heavy, historically imported layer. Raychem RPG (private) is the localiser (CPRI-approved 245 kV). No listed pure-play.

Opportunity

Joints, terminations & accessories — localisation playListed incumbents — Universal Cables — can deepen local capability at this node.

Risk

CCV / VCV line & equipmentNO COVERAGE. Catenary / vertical continuous vulcanisation lines come from Troester, Maillefer, Scholz (Europe) and Chinese OEMs; X-ray gauges from Sikora. No listed Indian equipment maker.

Opportunity

CCV / VCV line & equipment — localisation playNo NSE/BSE pure-play; domestic qualification of this node is the substitution play. [DERIVED]

Risk

Submarine cable & lay vesselsNO COVERAGE IN SELECTED UNIVERSE — and no Indian manufacturer at all. Global supply is concentrated (Prysmian, Nexans, NKT, Sumitomo, LS, Taihan) and only ~20 cable-lay vessels exist worldwide. The single largest localisation gap in the chain.

Opportunity

Submarine cable & lay vessels — localisation playNo NSE/BSE pure-play; domestic qualification of this node is the substitution play. [DERIVED]
