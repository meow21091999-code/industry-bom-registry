A short summary of the analysis. Structure and inputs are on the tree; process, value pools and watch items are summarised here.

Snapshot

High- and extra-high-voltage power cables above 33 kV (XLPE, land and submarine). India's wires & cables market is ₹90,000 cr (FY25); EHV/HV is 17–19% of power-transmission cables. The highest EHV grade now made in India is 500 kV; there are zero submarine-cable plants.

Process & equipment
  • Continuous vulcanisation (CCV/VCV) lines extrude the XLPE insulation; the conductor is Milliken-segmented; joints and terminations are mostly made in-house.
  • Submarine-cable capability is entirely absent.
Bottlenecks
  • HV/EHV XLPE compound and semiconductive compound — foreign (Borealis/Borouge).
  • Pre-moulded joints and terminations; global HV procurement lead times of 2–3 years.
  • The submarine-cable gap is the single largest hole.
Value pools
  • Cable OEMs — Polycab, KEI, Universal, Apar, Finolex, RR Kabel, Havells.
  • EPC — KEC, L&T, Kalpataru.
  • Copper/aluminium recovery; submarine cables are the open prize.
Watch items
  • Sterlite Electric greenfield (Vadodara, up to 400–550 kV).
  • KEI full-spectrum HVDC by FY27; Polycab EHV by 2027.
Coverage gaps
  • No submarine-cable plants; HV XLPE compound is foreign.

02Value-chain ladder

Raw material → aftermarket, with the Tier-4 → OEM supplier bands. Note the inversion: value capture sits upstream in the XLPE/semicon compound, the accessories and the VCV/CCV line — not in Indian cable assembly, which is competitive and thin-margin.

Tier 4 · Raw
Copper cathode / rod · aluminium · ethylene (XLPE base) · lead · HDPE · steel armour Hindalco (Birla Copper) · Vedanta · NALCO · Hindustan Copper · Adani (Kutch Copper)
Tier 3 · Specialty
HV/EHV XLPE compound · semiconductive compound · peroxide (DCP) · pre-moulded joints & terminations Borealis/Borouge, Hanwha (foreign) · BLS Polymers, Ankit (unlisted)
Tier 2 · Component
Milliken conductor · extruded insulated core · metallic screen · moisture barrier · accessories mostly in-house at the cable OEM
Tier 1 · System / OEM
EHV cable OEMs — Polycab · KEI · Universal Cables (UNISTAR) · Apar · Finolex J-Power (JV) · KEC Asian Cables · RR Kabel · Havells
OEM / Asset Owner
PGCIL · state TRANSCOs · private transmission developers · EPCs (KEC · L&T · Kalpataru) TBCB / regulated-return
Aftermarket
Jointing & termination · DTS/DAS fibre monitoring · fault location & repair · copper / aluminium recovery largely unlisted / OEM service arms
