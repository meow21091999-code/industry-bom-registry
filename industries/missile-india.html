Snapshot

India’s guided-missile ecosystem: tactical systems (Akash, Akash-NG, QRSAM/Anant Shastra, Astra, Nag, VSHORADS, Pralay) and the BrahMos cruise missile, designed by DRDO and built by BDL, BEL and an expanding private ring. Demand is now budget-led rather than nomination-led: FY27 capital acquisition is ₹1.85 lakh cr and the “other equipment” head that carries missiles rose 62% to ₹82,218 cr. The binding constraints have moved from demand to two places — energetics capacity and the profit pool, which sits upstream of integration, not in it.

Process & equipment
  • Case-bonded solid motors, cast and proof-pressure tested; ramjet/SFDR lines for BrahMos and Astra Mk-3, scramjet still development-stage.
  • Maraging-steel casing forming and welding, composite filament winding, precision machining, thermal-protection lay-up, RF/IIR seeker assembly and anechoic calibration.
  • Flight test at ITR Chandipur and Dr APJ Abdul Kalam Island, now extended by Junput (first NOTAM window 6–9 Oct 2026) and Nagayalanka (Navdurga, ₹1,460 cr) for BMD Phase-II geometry.
Bottlenecks
  • Special metals & alloys — maraging, tungsten, cobalt, nickel; MIDHANI is the domestic source but a large part of the input metal is imported.
  • Energetics — solid propellant is a near-duopoly; Premier is lifting RDX/HMX from 100 to 600 t/yr, first commercial output targeted Dec-2026.
  • Seekers & RF front-end — GaN AESA seeker still in development; MMIC/die content imported.
  • Integration capacity — BDL is the single qualified integrator for most DRDO missiles; the Aug-2026 ToT opening is the fix, and it is not yet contracted.
Value pools
  • Energetics and seekers keep the money: Premier’s book is ₹1,642 cr at 96% defence, Astra Microwave runs 33.8% EBITDA and Solar’s defence margin sits near 29%.
  • Integration books the revenue and gives away the margin: BDL’s Q2 FY27 revenue rose 45% while EBITDA fell 46% to 6.1% — the single most important number on this page.
  • Materials are the import-substitution pool: MIDHANI’s book is ₹2,164 cr with maraging at 15% of revenue.
  • Test ranges are now a public good, not a market: Junput and Nagayalanka are DRDO-owned, so they gate throughput without creating a listed revenue pool.
  • The ToT opening moves value, not just volume — five private contenders for Astra Mk-2 will price against BDL’s nomination-era economics.
Market & growth
  • Five-year (2025–2030): USD 468.6 mn → USD 2,512.7 mn, 39.9% nominal CAGR, scope = India missile hardware procurement only [REPORTED-T2: MarketsandMarkets, Aug-2026]. Volume: no unit-of-missile series exists in the public domain [UNAVAILABLE].
  • Three-year forward (2026→2029), from the same series’ year path: 66.5% [CALCULATED: (2,195.5/475.5)^(1/3)−1]. The series is back-loaded — it shows +1.5% in 2026, then a step-change from 2027.
  • Last year: +1.5% on that series [CALCULATED]. The wider envelope grew faster — indigenous defence production ₹1.78 lakh cr in FY26, +15.6%, exports ₹38,424 cr, +62.7% [REPORTED-T1: PIB / MoD, 17-Jun-2026].
  • Value versus volume: this is a procurement-timing series, not a price series — India buys in programme batches (QRSAM ~1,000 missiles, Akash-NG ~1,000), so growth is lumpy and physical. Inside one programme value and volume can diverge: BDL’s Q2 FY27 revenue rose 45% on volume while EBITDA fell 46% on mix [REPORTED-T1: BDL filing, 12-Nov-2026].
  • Scope warning: a same-house India “rocket and missile” series is USD 5.64 bn (2024) → USD 8.31 bn (2029), 8.1% [REPORTED-T2: MarketsandMarkets, Aug-2026] — a different scope, never summed with the 39.9% series [CONFLICTING DATA on level and rate; direction agrees].
Watch items
  • QRSAM / Anant Shastra CCS clearance and contract signature — reported near-final on 6-Nov-2026, still unsigned on 15-Nov-2026.
  • Astra Mk-2 RFP to private industry (reported imminent, Nov-2026); Pralay is the next candidate.
  • Premier’s 600 t RDX/HMX line — commercial production targeted Dec-2026, ~300 t in FY28.
  • BDL’s FY27 revenue guidance of ₹5,500 cr and ₹15,000 cr of inflows — H2 FY27 carries the whole year.
Coverage gaps
  • No NSE/BSE pure-play for scramjet/hypersonic propulsion, GaN AESA seeker dies, or range infrastructure; BrahMos Aerospace is an unlisted Indo-Russian JV.

05Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunitydual-readable: every vulnerability restated as the substitution it creates
Risk

Special metals & alloysMaraging steel, tungsten, cobalt, nickel and molybdenum are the casing and nozzle chain. MIDHANI is the domestic source of maraging and ultra-high-strength steel and its Q2 FY27 revenue rose 41% to ₹296 cr with a ₹2,164 cr book, but a large share of the input metal itself is imported — the gap the previous pass sized at roughly ₹8,000 cr remains an estimate, not a disclosed figure [INFERENCE]. Resilience class: MONTHS to YEARS (import substitution needs qualification, not just supply).

Opportunity

Materials — localisation playMIDHANI is the listed position and it is growing faster than integration (PAT +115% YoY in Q2 FY27). The unlisted gap is upstream vacuum-arc remelting and mill capacity for the input grades; that is where a new ₹8,000 cr-scale pool would sit. [DERIVED]

Risk

Energetics & solid propellantA near-duopoly: Premier supplies propellant for MRSAM, LRSAM and Astra and its order book stands at ₹1,642 cr (96% defence, 4.23× FY26 revenue), with Solar as the other qualified source. Premier is lifting RDX/HMX from 100 to 600 t/yr with commercial production targeted for Dec-2026 and about 300 t in FY28 — a real but slow relief valve. Ammonium perchlorate and some precursors remain import-linked. Resilience class: MONTHS.

Opportunity

Energetics — capacity playPremier’s expansion and Solar’s ₹17,568 cr defence book show where the pricing power is: Premier’s Q2 FY27 EBITDA margin recovered to 11.9% on 92% revenue growth, and Solar runs near 29%. The substitution play is high-energy-material capacity, not missile assembly. [DERIVED]

Risk

Seekers & RF front-endTerminal guidance is the narrowest qualification gate. BEL holds the indigenous Ku-band RF seeker for Astra Mk-1 and the IIR seekers; the GaN AESA seeker is still in development. Astra Microwave’s ₹3,937 cr standalone book is dominated by radar and EW, not seekers, so seeker-specific capacity remains thin, and MMIC/die content is imported. Resilience class: MONTHS to YEARS.

Opportunity

Seekers — localisation playThe RF electronics layer already earns the best margins in the chain (Astra Microwave 33.8% EBITDA, Data Patterns 23.6%), which is the incentive to localise the die and module level. The missing piece is domestic GaN and MMIC wafer supply, not assembly. [DERIVED]

Risk

Integration capacity & the pending pipelineBDL is the single qualified integrator for most DRDO conventional missiles, and its lines are carrying a ₹26,176 cr book plus export commitments. Roughly 2,000 missiles (QRSAM and Akash-NG) sit behind a CCS decision that had not produced a signed contract by 15-Nov-2026, seven years after development trials closed in Dec-2019. Resilience class: YEARS (qualification-gated).

Opportunity

Integration — the ToT openingThe 25-Aug-2026 ToT approval and the 15-Sep-2026 licensing round (nine agreements, 13 partners, technologies of 50 missiles released) open integration to ICOMM, Adani, Bharat Forge, Tata and Mahindra, with the Astra Mk-2 RFP reported imminent and Pralay next. This is the largest single change in the industry’s structure in a decade — and the reason integration’s value-capture score falls this pass. [DERIVED]

Risk

Test-range throughputChandipur was saturated, and interceptor trials were capped by the ~74 km Chandipur–Abdul Kalam Island separation. Junput adds an eastern node — the first NOTAM test window ran 6–9 Oct 2026 and the combined corridor gives roughly 150 km — while Nagayalanka (Navdurga, ₹1,460 cr, CCS-cleared Oct-2024) is being built for BMD Phase-II geometry. Relief has arrived but the Andhra range is not yet in service. Resilience class: MONTHS, easing.

Opportunity

Ranges — throughput, not revenueBoth ranges are DRDO-owned, so the opportunity is schedule certainty for everyone else rather than a listed pool; private access runs through DRDO booking and the revised proof-range SOP. No NSE/BSE exposure exists. [DERIVED]

Risk

Profit, not revenueThe integrator’s economics are the weakest in the chain and getting weaker: BDL’s Q2 FY27 EBITDA margin fell 1,027 bps to 6.1% on a 45% revenue increase, PAT fell 15%, and management guides 10–12% for FY27. Working capital sits in inventory and receivables ahead of acceptance. Resilience class: STRUCTURAL for the integrator model as currently priced.

Opportunity

Mix shift — the upstream tradeIf integration is a pass-through, the investable pool is energetics, RF electronics and materials, which print 12–34% EBITDA margins on the same programmes. The ToT opening pushes integration margins toward competitive bidding, which strengthens that conclusion rather than weakening it. [INFERENCE]

Where value sits: energetics (Premier, Solar), RF/EW electronics (Astra Microwave, Data Patterns, BEL) and special materials (MIDHANI) capture margin; BDL and the incoming ToT licensees capture revenue. The ₹82,218 cr FY27 “other equipment” allocation funds both, but it does not change which layer keeps the money.
06 Value pools & cost waterfall where value is captured · and which pool leaks to imports
Where value is captured
  • Energetics — Premier Explosives and Solar Industries; capacity-constrained, export-priced, 12–29% EBITDA.
  • RF, seeker and EW electronics — BEL, Astra Microwave, Data Patterns; 23–34% EBITDA.
  • Special materials — MIDHANI (maraging, titanium, superalloys); 16.8% EBITDA in Q2 FY27.
  • Integration and programme management — BDL, BEL, BrahMos (unlisted); large revenue, 6–12% EBITDA.
Who captures it (India-listed)
  • Premier Explosives NSE: PREMEXPLN — solid propellants, rocket motors, RDX/HMX; book ₹1,642 cr, Oct-2026
  • Solar Industries NSE: SOLARINDS — warheads, energetics, Pinaka rockets; defence book ₹17,568 cr
  • Astra Microwave NSE: ASTRAMICRO — RF front-end, seekers, radar; consolidated book ₹4,708 cr
  • Data Patterns NSE: DATAPATTNS — seeker and avionics electronics; book ₹2,698 cr incl. negotiated
  • Mishra Dhatu Nigam NSE: MIDHANI — maraging steel, titanium, superalloys; book ₹2,164 cr
  • Bharat Dynamics NSE: BDL — integration; book ₹26,176 cr, margin 6.1% in Q2 FY27
Pools that leak to imports
  • Input special metals (tungsten, cobalt, nickel, molybdenum) and some titanium sponge.
  • Seeker dies and MMICs, GaN devices, and high-end INS/IMU components.
  • Ammonium perchlorate and selected energetic precursors.
  • Bottleneck nodes: special metals, energetics, seekers, integration capacity, range throughput.
