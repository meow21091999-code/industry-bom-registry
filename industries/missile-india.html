DDigestshort summary · full detail below
Snapshot
  • India's guided-missile ecosystem spans tactical (Akash, Astra, QRSAM, Nag, VSHORADS) and strategic (Agni, K-series) systems plus the BrahMos cruise missile, run by DRDO with DPSUs and, increasingly, private industry.
  • Defence exports hit a record ₹38,424 cr in FY26, led by BrahMos and Akash.
Process & equipment
  • Solid motors are case-bonded and proof-pressure tested; liquid/cryogenic and ramjet-SFDR lines run separately.
  • Composite filament winding, maraging-steel forming, precision machining, and RF/IIR seeker assembly and anechoic calibration.
  • Flight-tested at ITR Chandipur and Abdul Kalam Island; new ranges at Junput and Nagayalanka.
Bottlenecks
  • Special metals & alloys — ~₹8,000 cr imported (nickel, cobalt, moly, tungsten); months–years.
  • Seeker qualification — narrow-source; GaN AESA seeker still in development; months–years.
  • Test-range throughput — new ranges under construction; months.
  • Solid-propellant capacity — near-duopoly; months.
Value pools
  • Integration and electronics (BDL, BEL) capture system value.
  • Propulsion (Solar, Premier) and warheads are the fastest-growing private pools.
  • Materials (MIDHANI) and casings (Walchandnagar) sit upstream and are import-substitution plays.
  • Most private defence revenue is still ammunition/rockets, not full missiles — the Aug-2026 ToT opening shifts this.
  • System IP stays with DRDO; royalty/licence value is not yet separately monetised.
Watch items
  • 25-Aug-2026: ToT of DRDO conventional missiles to industry (Astra Mk-2 RFP expected).
  • ~1,000 QRSAM and ~1,000 Akash-NG orders pending CCS clearance.
  • Revised proof-range SOP (Oct-2026) eases private access to test facilities.
Coverage gaps
  • No listed pure-play for scramjet/hypersonic propulsion or private launch/loitering munitions.
  • BrahMos Aerospace is an unlisted Indo-Russian JV.

05Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunitydual-readable
Risk

Special metals & alloysMissile-grade metals worth ~₹8,000 cr are imported; MIDHANI itself depends on imported nickel, cobalt, molybdenum and tungsten. Resilience: months–years.

Opportunity

Import substitution at MIDHANIThe same ~₹8,000 cr sits within MIDHANI's declared capability; indigenising maraging steel, superalloys and titanium casting is a captive, policy-backed market.

Risk

Seeker qualificationActive RF and IIR seekers are narrow-source and certification-gated; the GaN AESA seeker for Astra Mk-2/3 is still in development. Resilience: months–years.

Opportunity

Private seeker entryBEL's indigenous Ku-band RF seeker is qualified and Astra Microwave is developing a BrahMos-NG seeker — the terminal-guidance IP is opening to private firms.

Risk

Test-range throughputFlight-testing is concentrated at ITR Chandipur and Abdul Kalam Island, capping trial throughput as private production scales. Resilience: months.

Opportunity

New ranges + eased accessJunput (WB) and Nagayalanka (AP, ~₹1,460 cr) add capacity, and the Oct-2026 revised SOP waives proof charges and reserves quarterly slots for industry.

Risk

Execution / backlog conversionBDL's ₹26,176 cr order book is ~10.7x FY26 revenue; imported seeker/radar supply disrupted Akash and Barak-8 deliveries. Resilience: months.

Opportunity

Private surge capacityThe Aug-2026 ToT opening lets qualified private firms (Adani, Bharat Forge, ICOMM, Solar) add missile lines, cutting single-prime dependency.

06Value pools & cost waterfallwhere value is captured · and which pool leaks to imports
Where value is captured
  • System integration and mission electronics (BDL, BEL) capture the largest share of a missile's value.
  • Propulsion, propellants and warheads (Solar, Premier, BDL) are the fastest-growing private pools.
  • Materials and casings (MIDHANI, Walchandnagar) are upstream and import-substitution plays.
Who captures it (India-listed)
  • DPSU primes: BDL (missiles, launchers, warheads), BEL (seekers, guidance, Akash electronics).
  • Private specialists: Solar Industries, Premier Explosives (propulsion/energetics), Astra Microwave, Data Patterns (seekers/avionics), MIDHANI (materials).
Pools that leak to imports
  • Specialty metals/alloys (~₹8,000 cr) and some seeker/radar electronics remain import-dependent.
  • BrahMos propulsion and some JV system IP sit offshore (Russia/Israel partner arrangements).
