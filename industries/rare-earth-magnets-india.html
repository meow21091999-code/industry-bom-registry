Snapshot

Rare-earth permanent magnets (chiefly sintered NdFeB) sit in every servo motor, EV traction motor, wind turbine and hard-disk actuator. India imports ~95%, mostly from China. The REPM Scheme is now the live localisation vehicle, targeting 6,000 MTPA across five beneficiaries, with the first integrated plant due Q1-2028.

Process & equipment
  • NdPr oxide → strip-cast alloy → hydrogen decrepitation → jet milling to powder → pressing in a magnetic field → vacuum sintering → machining → coating.
  • Gating equipment: strip caster, jet mill, vacuum sintering furnace, magnetic press, and heavy-rare-earth grain-boundary diffusion — largely imported.
Bottlenecks
  • No integrated domestic plant yet (first due Q1-2028).
  • NdPr oxide supply concentrated in IREL.
  • Heavy rare earths (Dy/Tb) for high-temperature grades.
  • China price undercutting; strip-casting and jet-milling know-how.
Value pools
  • The magnet is the highest-value item in the motor BOM and the pool India is trying to capture.
  • Bidders span listed groups (Reliance, L&T, Vedanta, Coal India) and specialists (Sona Comstar, Attero, Lohum, Midwest).
  • Recycling (Attero, Lohum) is an emerging adjacent pool.
Market & growth
  • Value (nominal, T1 trade data): India imported permanent magnets worth ₹1,744 crore in FY25, only +5% on FY24’s ₹1,660 crore, despite tonnage rising 87% to 53,748 tonnes from 28,700 tonnes — unit value therefore fell roughly 44%. [REPORTED-T1: DGCIS trade data via Indian Express, 9-Jun-2025; tonnage confirmed by Reuters, 26-Nov-2025] [CALCULATED: unit value = value ÷ tonnage]
  • Volume: magnet consumption has gone 12,400 t (FY21) → 28,700 t (FY24) → 53,700 t (FY25) [REPORTED-T1: DGCIS trade data], and the government projects REPM consumption will double by 2030 from 2025 — an implied ~15% volume CAGR. [REPORTED-T1: PMO / PIB, 26-Nov-2025] [CALCULATED: 2^(1/5)−1] A rupee-value CAGR is [UNAVAILABLE] — the FY25 value series is distorted by Chinese price cuts.
  • Policy anchor: the REPM scheme (₹7,280 crore; ₹6,450 crore sales-linked incentive plus ₹750 crore capital subsidy) targets 6,000 MTPA of integrated capacity across five beneficiaries at ≤1,200 MTPA each, on a two-year gestation plus five years of incentive — i.e. first integrated plants around 2028. [REPORTED-T1: Union Cabinet 26-Nov-2025, notified 15-Dec-2025; Lok Sabha answer]
  • Price penalty: finished REPMs are about 43% costlier in India than in international markets, and import dependence ran 59.6–81.3% by value and 84.8–90.4% by quantity over FY23–FY25 — the cost gap is the localisation problem in one number. [REPORTED-T1: PIB background note, Dec-2025] [REPORTED-T2: Financial Express, 26-Nov-2025]
Watch items
  • REPM beneficiary award (LIVE) and NAN MagneTech commissioning Q1-2028.
  • China export controls on magnets (Apr-2025) and rare-earth tech.
  • IREL NdPr oxide allocation to the three lowest bidders.
Coverage gaps

No NSE/BSE pure-play magnet maker today. The strongest listed exposure is Sona Comstar (largest importer, bidder, robotics/EV demand); Reliance, L&T, Vedanta and Coal India are conglomerate bidders. IREL is state-owned/unlisted.

05 Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunity dual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

No integrated domestic plantIndia has no commercial sintered-NdFeB production; ~95% imported. Resilience: years / structural (first plant Q1-2028).

Opportunity

REPM scheme (₹7,280 cr)6,000 MTPA across five beneficiaries; 20 bids received — the localisation vehicle is live.

Risk

NdPr oxide supplyThe chain rests on IREL, the sole domestic miner; oxide is allocated to the three lowest bidders. Resilience: years.

Opportunity

Upstream integrationThe scheme explicitly builds NdPr-oxide-to-magnet; ARCI's pilot plant de-risks the process.

Risk

Heavy rare earths (Dy/Tb)High-temperature grades need Dy/Tb, largely from China/Myanmar. Resilience: structural.

Opportunity

Grain-boundary diffusionReduces Dy/Tb need; a technical hedge for domestic makers.

Risk

China price undercuttingChinese magnets are cheaper; matching pricing is the scheme's core concern. Resilience: years.

Opportunity

Sales-linked incentive₹6,530 cr sales incentive (capped ₹2,150/kg, 40% of turnover) bridges the price gap.

Risk

Recycling is nascentRecovering rare earths from end-of-life magnets is barely scaled. Resilience: months–years.

Opportunity

Recycling poolAttero and Lohum are building it; a distinct, fast-growing value pool.

Deepest hidden dependency: NdPr oxide and the heavy rare earths above it. Even with the REPM scheme, India's magnet chain begins at IREL's oxide — and China still dominates rare-earth processing (~90%) and sintered output (~94%).
06 Value pools & cost waterfall where value is captured · and which pool leaks to imports
Where value is captured
  • The sintered magnet is the highest-value item in a motor's BOM.
  • Today the pool is captured by Chinese makers; the REPM scheme is the attempt to repatriate it.
  • Recycling is an emerging adjacent pool.
Who captures it (India-listed)
  • Sona Comstar — largest importer, REPM bidder, EV/robotics demand base.
  • Conglomerate bidders: Reliance, L&T, Vedanta, Coal India, Midwest.
  • IREL (state-owned) is the oxide supplier.
Pools that leak to imports
  • Sintered NdFeB magnets, strip-cast alloy and heavy rare earths — imported.
  • Magnet-making equipment (strip caster, jet mill, sintering furnace) — imported.
