Snapshot

Indian green hydrogen’s first real market: renewable power plus air-separated nitrogen through a dynamic Haber–Bosch loop, refrigerated, then shipped or piped to a fertiliser plant. Demand is contracted — 7.24 lakh t/yr domestically, 5.56 lakh MTPA of hydrogen offtake mostly for export — while almost nothing produces yet.

Process & equipment
  • Integrated complex: RE park → electrolyser → ASU → synthesis loop → tanks → berth; 0.18 t H₂ and 0.82 t N₂ per tonne NH₃.
  • Long-lead items: electrolyser, licensor-supplied dynamic loop, ASU, double-wall cryogenic tanks (4 × 20,000 t at Kakinada).
  • Most projects are brownfield urea-complex conversions — ammonia train reused.
Bottlenecks
  • Financial closure, not chemistry — awards placed no orders; electrolyser commissioning slipped to Aug-2027.
  • Delivered RTC power — over 65% of cost, plus ₹0.5–1.5/kWh wheeling and firming.
  • Synthesis-loop IP — Casale, Topsoe, KBR and tk Uhde license every Indian plant.
  • Tanks, berths and certification — few designers; GHCI plus RFNBO means two audits, hourly matching from 2030.
Value pools
  • The licensor keeps the best economics — fees and proprietary equipment booked pre-construction, no Indian alternative.
  • RTC renewable power is the largest revenue line in the chain, but a PPA annuity with no pricing power.
  • The developer carries the most capital and the thinnest spread — USD 1,373/t cost against offtake at grey parity.
  • Offtakers convert subsidy into import substitution — ~USD 2.5 bn of forex saving over ten years.
  • Leaks to imports: synthesis licence, stack IP, membranes, PGM catalysts, tank design, loading arms, certification.
Market & growth
  • Committed offtake 555,610 MTPA, up about 2.6× last year [CALCULATED].
  • Domestic SIGHT demand 7.24 lakh t/yr — ~₹36,000–47,000 cr/yr at discovery prices [CALCULATED]; supply from ~2029.
  • 5-yr and 3-yr value CAGR [UNAVAILABLE] — no revenue series and no CAGR off zero; prices nominal, SIGHT support included.
Watch items
  • Tranche-II (54,000 t/yr) bids closed 27-Oct-2026; a 3 lakh t/yr green-urea round awaits approval.
  • Electrolyser commissioning extended to Aug-2027 — the tell for project financial closure.
  • GHCI exemptions to 31-Mar-2027; RFNBO hourly matching from 1-Jan-2030; Kakinada COD 31-Mar-2029.
Coverage gaps

No NSE/BSE exposure for the synthesis licensor, tank design, membranes, PGM or synthesis catalysts, or certification bodies; AM Green, Avaada, Hygenco and OCIOR are unlisted.

05 Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunity dual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

Demand & financial closure, not technology7.24 lakh t/yr is contracted but projects have not closed financing or placed equipment orders; electrolyser makers deferred commissioning from Dec-2026 to Aug-2027 because no orders arrived, and one listed equipment supplier described energy-transition demand as subdued for two years. Resilience: months–years.

Opportunity

Offtake-backed bankability is the productSECI as intermediary procurer, a 10-year GAPA and a 3 lakh t/yr green-urea pipeline convert policy demand into bankable contracts — whoever can underwrite and execute against those contracts (EPC plus O&M, not chemistry) captures the first-mover pool.

Risk

Delivered cost of round-the-clock renewable powerIndustry puts RE at over 65% of production cost, and the number that matters is delivered RE-RTC: transmission, wheeling, grid connection and firming add ₹0.5–1.5/kWh even after the ISTS waiver. Resilience: years (structural cost).

Opportunity

Firming is the missing businessPumped-hydro and BESS co-location, banked renewable energy and state open-access structuring are where a developer can move the single largest cost line — and where listed storage and PSP owners can sell firmness rather than electrons.

Risk

Synthesis-loop IP and cryogenic storage designEvery Indian green-ammonia plant runs on a foreign licence (Casale, Topsoe, KBR, thyssenkrupp Uhde), and the 20,000 t double-wall tanks and marine loading arms come from a handful of designers. Resilience: years — requalification is design-led, not supplier-led.

Opportunity

Module fabrication and tank-build is the addressable sliceIndia does not need to invent Haber–Bosch to capture value: licensor-partnered module fabrication (Praj GenX, L&T), cryogenic tank erection, and O&M of licensed loops are real, near-term domestic pools.

Risk

Certification divergence & MRV capacityGHCI (≤0.38 kg CO₂e/kg NH₃, well-to-gate, BEE-accredited verifiers, derivatives methodology still to be issued) and EU RFNBO (70% saving, additionality, monthly then hourly matching from 2030) must both be satisfied for export volumes. Resilience: months (documentation) to years (hourly matching infrastructure).

Opportunity

Verification and hourly-matched power contractingAccredited-verifier capacity, MRV software and hourly-matched PPAs with guarantees of origin are a services market that scales with every tonne certified — currently unlisted and uncontested.

Risk

CO₂ for urea & the import-substitution trapUrea needs 570 kg NH₃ plus CO₂ per tonne, so green ammonia cannot green urea without an external CO₂ stream; the easily substitutable slice is only the ~2.5 mt/yr of imported ammonia used for DAP/NPK. Resilience: structural for urea, weeks for phosphatics.

Opportunity

Start with phosphatics, then capture CO₂The 13 fertiliser units already contracted are mostly phosphatic; pairing green ammonia with captured CO₂ from refineries, steel or cement (the HURL–NTPC Barauni model) is how the urea slice eventually opens.

06 Value pools & cost waterfall where value is captured · and which pool leaks to imports
Cost waterfall (USD/t NH₃, modelled)
  • Electricity ≈ 522 (38%) — 10.6 MWh/t at ~USD 49/MWh delivered.
  • Financing ≈ 399 (29%) — capital charge on ~USD 2.85 bn overnight capex.
  • Electrolyser depreciation, O&M and stacks ≈ 275 (20%).
  • N₂ separation, synthesis and storage depreciation ≈ 55.
  • Total ≈ 1,373/t plant gate; delivered to a European importer ≈ 1,460/t. Grey ammonia: USD 475–650/t.
Where value is captured
  • Synthesis licensor — fees, proprietary equipment and catalyst, booked before construction, near-total lock-in.
  • RTC renewable power — the largest revenue line in the chain, a 25-year PPA annuity.
  • Developer — largest revenue, thinnest spread; option value on a contracted book.
  • Fertiliser offtaker — converts subsidy into import substitution (~USD 2.5 bn forex saving over 10 years).
  • Storage and port — small revenue, high strategic choke, long-lead.
Who captures it (India-listed)
  • Larsen & Toubro NSE: LT — electrolysers (300 MW), EPC, tankage and pipelines.
  • Reliance Industries NSE: RELIANCE — 300 MW electrolysers, Jamnagar complex, export molecules.
  • Adani Enterprises NSE: ADANIENT — 300 MW electrolyser capacity, Mundra cluster.
  • Waaree Energies NSE: WAAREEENER — 300 MW electrolysers plus a proposed 1.2 MTPA ammonia plant.
  • NTPC / NTPC Green NSE: NTPC, NTPCGREEN — the RTC power annuity and co-firing.
Pools that leak to imports
  • Synthesis-loop licences and proprietary equipment — four foreign licensors, no Indian alternative.
  • Electrolyser membranes, PGM catalysts, titanium PTLs and stack IP.
  • Cryogenic tank design, marine loading arms, ammonia carriers.
  • Certification and MRV for EU-bound volumes.
