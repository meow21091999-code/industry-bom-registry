Snapshot
  • The demand-side value pool: broadband, DTH, VSAT services, EO data, navigation and transponder leasing.
  • India satcom market estimated at ~$2.3 bn, heading to ~$20 bn by 2028 (KPMG).
Process & equipment
  • Service delivery runs on gateways, OSS/BSS and analytics platforms rather than heavy equipment.
  • EO data flows from ground reception through processing to analysis-ready products.
Bottlenecks
  • Spectrum allocation & rollout — policy timing (months).
  • Downstream data concentration — few listed players (structural).
  • Navigation & transponder leasing — no listed pure-play (structural).
Value pools
  • Subscription value: broadband (Jio, Bharti/OneWeb), DTH (Dish TV), enterprise VSAT (Nelco, Hughes).
  • Data value: geospatial analytics (MapmyIndia, Genesys, Ceinsys).
  • Capacity leasing anchored by NSIL (unlisted).
Watch items
  • Satcom spectrum: DCC cleared 5% AGR (Sep 2026); Cabinet + security clearance pending.
  • EO-PPP constellation formalised (Jan 2026); deployment over 4 years.
Coverage gaps
  • No NSE/BSE pure-play for navigation services or transponder leasing.

05Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunitydual-readable
Risk

Spectrum allocation & rolloutDCC cleared a 5% AGR spectrum charge (Sep 2026), but Cabinet approval, formal assignment and security clearance are still pending. Resilience: months (policy).

Opportunity

First-mover service marketOnce spectrum lands, GMPCS operators and their partners (Nelco, Hughes) can monetise a large addressable market.

Risk

Downstream data concentrationOnly a handful of listed firms have direct EO-data/geospatial exposure. Resilience: structural.

Opportunity

Geospatial analyticsMapmyIndia, Genesys and Ceinsys are direct plays on a market estimated at ~₹1 tn by 2030.

Risk

Navigation & transponder leasingNo listed pure-play; NSIL (unlisted) anchors capacity leasing. Resilience: structural.

Opportunity

Downstream NavIC chips & servicesNavIC adoption in devices and defence electronics is an emerging, if hard-to-access, pool.
