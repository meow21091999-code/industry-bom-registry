Snapshot

The condenser and heat-rejection system — the turbine-island equipment that condenses exhaust steam. BHEL builds 660/800 MW surface and air-cooled condensers and the 700 MWe nuclear condenser; condenser performance follows ASME PTC 12.2.

Process & equipment
  • Titanium or stainless/cupro-nickel condenser tubing, tubesheet plate, and LP heaters in the condenser neck.
  • Titanium tubing is the gating imported input.
Bottlenecks
  • Titanium condenser tubes are imported (erosion/corrosion-resistant, standard for seawater cooling).
  • Air-cooled condensers for water-scarce sites need a different, machinery-intensive design.
Value pools
  • BHEL (own-design condensers and feedwater systems); Thermax can scale air-cooled condenser capability.
Watch items
  • Air-cooled condensers for dry sites.
  • Specialty-tube qualification.
  • BHEL's online fouling system to ASME PTC 12.2.
Coverage gaps
  • Titanium condenser tubing is imported; no listed Indian specialty-tube producer.

05Bottlenecks — Risk & Opportunitydual-readable: every risk is a substitution play
Risk

Titanium condenser tubes importedThe erosion/corrosion-resistant titanium tube (standard for seawater cooling) is a specialty import item; a supply disruption is hard to re-route and retubing cycles create recurring demand. Resilience: months–years.

Opportunity

Specialty-tube qualificationThe titanium and specialty-tube chain could qualify condenser tube grades, closing the page’s central import gap.

Risk

Air-cooled condensers for dry sitesWater-scarce sites need ACCs, a different and more machinery-intensive design than surface condensers. Resilience: months.

Opportunity

ACC capability at scaleBHEL and Thermax can scale ACC capability for India’s water-stressed thermal fleet.

Risk

Fouling, salinity and air-ingressCondenser performance degrades with tube fouling, salinity ingress and air ingress — monitored by specialty instrumentation BHEL has developed in-house. Resilience: months.

Opportunity

Instrumentation & O&M servicesOnline fouling / salinity / air-ingress systems and cleaning services are a recurring, defensible value pool.

Where value sits: the condenser is India’s most mature heat-rejection node — BHEL, L&T, Walchandnagar, ISGEC and Thermax cover the body, ACC and heat exchangers, and Kirloskar Brothers, KSB and WPIL cover the CW pumps. The single import gap is the titanium tube.
